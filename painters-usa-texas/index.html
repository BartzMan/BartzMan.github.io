Coating cross-section

Every layer
has a job.

Select a layer to see what it does.

Topcoat

The outer finish takes the direct exposure. Choose it for the conditions the surface will see, such as sunlight, abrasion or regular cleaning.

Intermediate

A middle coat can build thickness or add a barrier when the surface needs more protection than a primer and finish provide.

Primer

A primer helps the coating system bond to the substrate. Its selection depends on the material and the condition of the prepared surface.

Surface prep

Cleaning and profiling remove contamination and loose material. The right preparation depends on the existing finish, corrosion and substrate.

Pressure washing

Before a repaint

Plan around
the workday.

For a working facility, take a walk-through with the people who use the space. Make a short list before you discuss the job.

Find the quiet windows

Mark when each area is least busy and note any equipment or routes that need to stay available.

Map access

Identify loading doors, narrow aisles and anything overhead that may affect access.

Record the surface condition

Take photos of peeling paint, corrosion or recurring moisture and note where each appears.

Review site rules

Bring the facility’s site rules and access restrictions to the planning conversation.
