Two kinds of roof. One standard.

18.4°

Residential roofing

A new roof or a full replacement for the house you live in. This is the service when the roof has reached the end of its life, not just a leak in one spot.

26.6°

Roof inspections

A close look at the roof, the flashing and the gutters to see what shape they are in. Useful after a storm, before buying a house, or when something just looks off.

33.7°

Roof repair

Fixing a leak, replacing missing shingles or sealing around a vent. The right call when the rest of the roof is still in good shape.

1.2°

Commercial roofing

Flat and low-slope roofs on shops, offices, warehouses and apartment buildings. Usually a membrane or coating system rather than shingles.

26.6°

Roof inspections

A walk along the seams, drains and rooftop units so a building owner can see what needs attention now and what can wait.

4.8°

Roof repair

Tracking down a leak around a vent, a seam or a drain, then sealing it. A common fix on flat roofs.
