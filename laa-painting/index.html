Do you stain decks and wooden play sets?

Yes. Deck staining is a big part of the work, and play sets, fences and railings take stain the same way. Send a photo of the wood as it looks now.

Should a deck be power washed before it is stained?

Usually. Washing lifts dirt, mildew and loose old finish so the new stain can soak into the wood. The wood then needs to dry before staining.

Can a bathtub, tile or kitchen cabinets be refinished instead of replaced?

Often, yes. Tubs, tile and cabinets can be refinished in place. A photo of the piece and its condition is the best place to start.

What should I send when I get in touch?

Your town, what you want painted, stained or refinished, and a few photos. Call or text (862) 621-5386 or email laapainting1@gmail.com.
