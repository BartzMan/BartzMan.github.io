Bay 1 · New Residential

New homes,rough-in to set-out.

Plumbing for new houses, carried through the stages of the build, on the builder's job and the builder's plans.

  1. 01

    Rough-in

    Drain, waste and water lines set in the ground before the slab is poured.

  2. 02

    Top-out

    Lines run up through the framing and vents out through the roof, before the walls close.

  3. 03

    Set-out

    Fixtures, faucets and trim set once the finishes are in.

Questionsfrom builders.

Straight answers about what RTR Plumbing takes on, and how to get a project in front of the office.

Call (940) 433-3994
1Do you take repair or service calls?

No. RTR Plumbing works new construction only: New Residential, Multi-Unit Projects and Light Commercial. For a leak or a repair in an existing home, please call a service plumber.

2What kinds of projects do you plumb?

Three kinds, all new construction: New Residential, Multi-Unit Projects and Light Commercial.

3What should I send for a bid?

The plans and your schedule. Email them to rtrplumbingllc@gmail.com with the project city, the number of homes or units, and when you expect to start.

4How do I reach the office?

Call or text (940) 433-3994, or email rtrplumbingllc@gmail.com. The form on this page opens your own email app with your project details filled in.

5Where are you based?

In Boyd, Texas, at the tan steel shop with R, T and R painted on its three bay doors.
