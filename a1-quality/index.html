p.07 Questions

Questions in the margin.

My AC runs but the house won't cool. What's wrong?

It could be a dirty coil, a refrigerant problem, a failing part or a clogged filter. Troubleshooting at the unit narrows it down to the one that is actually causing it.

How dirty does a coil have to get before it matters?

Any layer that blocks air between the fins cuts how much heat the coil can move. If you can see a mat of dirt like the coil photo on this page, it is time for a cleaning.

Why is water leaking from my attic unit or ceiling?

Usually the condensate drain: a clogged or broken drain line, or a drain pan that has filled up. Turn the system off, then call or text with a photo of the pan if you can reach it safely.

Do you work on furnaces in the attic?

Yes. Furnaces and attic units are part of the work, along with the coil and the drain that sit beside them.

What should I send when I get in touch?

Your neighborhood, what the system is doing, and a photo of the unit and its label if you can. Call or text (281) 713-0434 or email a.1qualitycontracting@yahoo.com.
