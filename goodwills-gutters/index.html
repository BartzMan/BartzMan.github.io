Homeowner ground check

Read the water marks.

After rain, walk around the house at ground level. These five signs can help you describe where water is collecting.

Pooling near a wall

Notice where puddles stay close to the house after rain has passed.

Splash marks

Look for a strip of dirt or staining where water repeatedly hits siding or masonry.

Gaps beside the foundation

Check for places where soil has pulled away or washed out.

Gutters spilling over

From indoors or the ground, note which sections overflow during a storm.

Runoff at the outlet

See whether water from the downspout flows back toward the house.
