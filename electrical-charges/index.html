How a job starts

Bare ceiling to switched on.

  1. Send the room.

    Text a photo or call (347) 600-7722. The ceiling, the walls and the fixture you have in mind say a lot.

  2. Plan the light.

    Where each fixture hangs, what lights the walls and shelves, and which switch runs what.

  3. Wire and hang.

    Boxes, circuits and the fixtures themselves go in, placed where the plan says.

  4. Switch on.

    The lights come on, and the room is yours again.
