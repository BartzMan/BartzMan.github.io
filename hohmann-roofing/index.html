Why it matters here

Winter is when a roof earns its keep.

From the first lake-effect band to the last thaw, a Buffalo-area roof carries snow, freezes, thaws and freezes again. Small weak spots at the eaves, flashing and walls turn into leaks.

Roofing and siding are one conversation: the roof sheds the water and the walls keep it out. Looking at both together is how problems get found early.

What the work covers

Roofing or siding, one job at a time.

Roof replacement

When shingles are worn, curling or leaking in several places, a new roof is usually the cleaner answer than repair after repair.

Winter damage

After a hard winter, stains on ceilings or ice at the eaves point to meltwater getting in. A look at the roof and attic finds where.

Replacing worn or damaged siding protects the walls from water and wind and freshens how the house looks.

Watch an ice dam form, then stop

Ice at the eave starts as heat in the attic.

  • Heat leaving the house
  • Cold air moving through

Ice dam forms

Heat from the rooms leaks into the attic and warms the roof deck. Snow on the upper roof melts, runs down to the cold eave, and refreezes. The ice grows into a dam, and meltwater backs up under the shingles.

A winter guide for homeowners

Ice dams: why they form and what actually stops them.

Heat leaking from the house warms the roof from below. Snow melts, the water runs down to the cold eave, and it refreezes there. The ice builds into a ridge, a dam, that traps more meltwater behind it.

Keep heat in the house with insulation and air sealing in the attic floor, and keep the roof cold with ventilation from the eaves to the ridge. Together they treat the cause, not just the ice.

Never chip ice off a roof, and never climb up to do it. It damages shingles and people fall. Clearing snow from the edge with a long roof rake, standing on the ground, is safer.

Water stains on a ceiling or at the top of an outside wall, peeling paint near the ceiling, or damp insulation in the attic can mean meltwater is getting in. That is the time to call a roofer.
