Privacy notice

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This notice explains how Pacific Air Conditioning & Refrigeration, Corp. ("Pacific", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit this website, ask for a quote or contact us by phone, text or email.

1. Who we are

Pacific Air Conditioning & Refrigeration, Corp., Miami, Florida. Phone (305) 633-6722. Email pacrefcorp@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Details about your request: the type of property, the system, where it is and anything else you tell us in a message.
  • Technical information our website host may record automatically, such as IP address, browser, device and pages requested, so the site can run securely.

This website does not set advertising cookies. A simple visit counter records the page visited, device type and time zone; it does not identify you.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your questions and quote requests.
  • To arrange, carry out and follow up on work.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records, and to operate and secure this website.

4. The request form, calls and texts

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text to stop receiving them. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of receiving service.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers that help us run the business (for example website hosting and email), when the law requires it, or with a successor if the business changes hands.

6. Other websites

This site links to services such as Google Maps and Facebook. They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it to reply to you, complete work and meet our business, tax and legal obligations. After that we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

8. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it by calling or emailing us. We will respond as required by the law that applies to you.

9. Security and children

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or email service is completely secure, so please do not send payment card numbers or government ID numbers through this site. This site is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes and questions

We may update this notice and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Call (305) 633-6722 or email pacrefcorp@gmail.com.
