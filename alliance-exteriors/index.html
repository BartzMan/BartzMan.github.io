North Texas storm guide

After the hail.

Once the weather clears, take a walk around your home. You can make these checks with both feet on the ground.

Look along the roofline

Look for missing shingles, loose pieces or a new gap at the edge. Use your phone’s zoom from the yard if the roof is hard to see.

Check gutters and downspouts

Look for dents, sections pulled away from the house or a downspout that has shifted.

Look up indoors

Note any fresh ceiling stains. Photograph what changed, then share the pictures when you call a roofer. Stay off the roof.
