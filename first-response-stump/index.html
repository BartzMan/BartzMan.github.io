UNIT 2 / RUN BOARD

The run board.

Four kinds of job. Tap a card to see what the job involves and what is left behind when the machine drives away.

What the job involves

  • The grinder is brought in to the stump on its own wheels
  • The cutting wheel sweeps across the stump in passes
  • The top of the big roots goes with it

What is left behind

  • A bed of chips and soil where the stump stood
  • Roots below, left to break down in the ground
  • Room to fill, seed, sod or plant

What the job involves

  • Working one stump at a time along the line
  • Staying clear of the wall, the hedge and the pavement
  • Clearing loose soil and chips off the sidewalk

What is left behind

  • A clear strip of ground along the hedge
  • Chips that stay for the bed or go, worth deciding before the job starts
  • Space for new planting or sod

What the job involves

  • A look at the lean, the wires and what sits under the tree
  • The crown taken down in sections, lowered rather than dropped
  • Trunk and limbs cut down for hauling

What is left behind

  • An open yard where the tree stood
  • A stump, which can be ground as the next run
  • Light and sky for the lawn again

What the job involves

  • Walking the yard to see what is down and what is still hanging
  • Cutting and clearing limbs, split trunks and tipped trees
  • Moving debris away from the house, fence and driveway

What is left behind

  • A yard you can walk across again
  • A hole where a root plate lifted, to be filled
  • Stumps that can be ground in a second run
