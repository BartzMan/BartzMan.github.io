L-09Field notes

Asked on the job site.

01Can you clear a lot before we build?

Yes. Lot clearing and site prep are the work Ramos does for builders and developers. Send the lot address or the plat, say what has to come off and what should stay, and the plan is talked through before any work starts.

02Can you take down a tree that is close to the house?

Yes. A tree beside a house is taken down from the top by a climber, piece by piece, instead of being dropped in one go. Send a photo that shows the tree and the house together.

03What happens to the brush and the wood?

Say what you want when you call. The brush, limbs and wood are part of the plan for the job and are settled before the work starts, so nothing is left as a surprise.

04What should I send to get a quote?

The lot address or the area, a few photos of the trees or the lot, and what the job is: a whole lot, one tree, trimming or a storm clean-up. Call or text (678) 778-3860 or email ramostreeservice@gmail.com.
