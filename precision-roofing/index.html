02Layer two: cover board

Pick your system.

Tap a roof. Read the core: the layers, what it suits and a job of that kind.

TPO

A white single-ply sheet whose seams are heat-welded into one skin, a common pick for big flat roofs.

    Suits Warehouses, retail centers, flat additions.

    Ask about TPO →

    PVC

    A tough single-ply sheet with welded seams, often chosen where a roof has plenty of vents, pipes and edges to work around.

      Suits Roofs with rooftop equipment, edges and details.

      Ask about PVC →

      EPDM

      A rubber membrane that arrives in big sheets, familiar on large flat roofs and flat additions.

        Suits Large, simple flat roofs and flat additions.

        Ask about EPDM →

        Torch-down

        Modified bitumen rolls heated into place in layers, a proven build for low-slope roofs and smaller flat sections.

          Suits Low-slope roofs, small flat sections, additions.

          Ask about torch-down →

          Shingles

          Overlapping asphalt shingles for pitched roofs on homes and smaller buildings.

            Suits Homes and pitched roofs on smaller buildings.

            Ask about shingles →

            Gravel removal

            Old ballast stone comes off with a vacuum instead of a shovel, so the roof underneath can be looked at and redone.

              Suits Older built-up roofs topped with gravel.

              Ask about gravel removal →
