Panels and service upgrades

The panel splits the power coming into your home between all its circuits. You need an upgrade when the home has outgrown it: new appliances, an addition, or a charger.

Good to knowA load check tells you how much room a panel has left.
Service 02

Meters

The meter measures what your home uses, and the equipment around it is where power enters the house. Work here comes up with service upgrades and new builds.

Good to knowMeter and service changes involve the utility, so it pays to plan ahead.
Service 03

EV charger installation

A wall charger on its own circuit charges faster and more safely than a regular outlet. You need one when you bring home an electric car.

Good to knowThe best spot is usually close to the panel and to where the car's charge port ends up.

Before you buy
a charger

1

Panel capacity

A panel can only carry so much. A load check shows whether there is room for a charger, or whether the panel should be upgraded first.

2

Distance to the spot

The nearer the charger is to the panel, the shorter and simpler the wiring run. Longer runs still work. They just need more planning.

3

Indoor or outdoor

A garage wall is sheltered. A driveway or carport is not, so the charger and its fittings need to be rated for the weather.

4

The permit

Charger installs normally need a permit and an inspection. Make sure it is covered at the start, and keep the paperwork.
