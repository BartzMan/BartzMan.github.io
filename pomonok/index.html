Common questions

Before you call

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text us with what is going wrong and the block in Flushing you are in. We look at the job and tell you what the work involves. Anything discussed by phone, by text or at the door is an estimate until you accept it in writing.

Water is leaking, or I can smell gas. What do I do?

Protect yourself first. For flooding, a gas smell or an electrical hazard, get out and call 911, or call your gas or electric utility from outside. Do not wait to hear from us.

Which areas do you work in?

Flushing, Queens. Tell us your block and we will say straight away whether we can take the job or whether it is outside what we cover.

What should I put in a text?

Your name, what the problem is, where it is in the flat or house, and the best time to reach you. A photo of the fixture or the pipe helps more than a description.
