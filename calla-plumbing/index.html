No. 05 — Before you call

Water where it shouldn't be?

A few things worth knowing while you wait for a plumber.

Where is my main water shut-off?

In most older Cincinnati houses it's in the basement, on the pipe where the water line comes through the front foundation wall, near the meter. Turn it clockwise to close. If it's stuck, don't force it: that's a valve worth replacing.

My outdoor faucet split after a freeze. What now?

Close the inside shut-off that feeds that faucet (often a small valve on the basement ceiling near the wall) and open the outside faucet to drain it. Then call about a repair or replacement.

Can you put in a laundry tub?

Yes. Laundry tub installs are one of the things customers mention in their Google reviews. Send the form with where the tub is going and what's there now.
