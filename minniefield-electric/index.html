01 · Services

Nine switches. One electrician.

In their own words: service changes, troubleshooting, wiring, remodels, lights, hot tubs, power, solar panels, home audio and video. For homes and for businesses.

  1. 01

    Service changes

    A new meter base, a new panel, a bigger service coming into the house or the shop. Set level, run neat, ready for the power company.

  2. 02

    Troubleshooting

    Breakers that trip, outlets that quit, lights that flicker or buzz. We trace it back to the cause and fix that, not just the symptom.

  3. 03

    Wiring

    New circuits and new runs, inside and out, including underground runs dug out across the yard.

  4. 04

    Remodels

    Kitchens, basements and rooms opened up. The wiring planned while the walls are open, so outlets and switches land where you live.

  5. 05

    Lights

    Chandeliers, recessed cans, ceiling fixtures and full-room lighting, hung straight and switched where you reach for it.

  6. 06

    Hot tubs & pools

    Power out to the hot tub and the pool equipment, with the outdoor disconnect and conduit to go with it.

  7. 07

    Power

    More outlets, dedicated circuits for the shop, the gym, the garage or the new appliance.

  8. 08

    Solar panels

    The electrical side of a solar panel setup on your home or building.

  9. 09

    Home audio & video

    Wiring for the TV wall, speakers and home audio, with the cables hidden where they belong.

Every job ends at the switch

Every job ends the same way. Panel, wiring, fixtures, power: it all comes down to the moment somebody flips the switch.
