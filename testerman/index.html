Services

The work we take on

Five lines of work. If your job is not on this list, ask about it and we will tell you whether we can do it.

  1. 01

    Plumbing repair and installation

    Leaks, clogs, fixtures and new pipework inside an existing building. Repairing what is already there, or running new lines and setting new fixtures.

  2. 02

    Boilers and heating

    Boilers, heating pipework, circulator pumps and the valves and controls that run them, including work inside a packed boiler room.

  3. 03

    Water heaters

    Storage tanks and tankless units: new installs, replacements, and the copper and gas pipework either side of the unit.

  4. 04

    Bathroom renovation plumbing

    Rough-in and finish plumbing for a bathroom being rebuilt — tub, toilet and basin, plus the drains and vents behind them.

  5. 05

    Copper piping

    Supply lines, risers and mechanical-room pipework in copper, soldered or pressed, including branch lines off a main.

Questions

Four things people ask

How do I get an estimate?

Call or send a text with a short description of the job and where it is in the building. We tell you what the work involves and what it would cost before anything is booked in.

What should I do if there is no heat or a burst pipe?

Leave the building first, then call 911 or your gas utility. Do not rely on this website when water is leaking, a pipe has burst or you smell gas. Once the building is safe, call us and we will come and take a look.

Which areas do you work in?

Glendale, Queens. If you are just outside Glendale, call and ask, and we will tell you straight away whether we can take the job on.

What should I put in a message?

The kind of problem, which room it is in, when it started, and whether water or gas is involved. A photo of the pipework or the unit helps.
