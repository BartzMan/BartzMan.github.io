Where the lines meet

Three jobs, one shoreline.

Hot and cold come into every house and meet at the same few places. Not sure which one you need? Pick what is going on.

Tap one and the right line lights up below.

01

Plumbing

Pipes, fixtures and drains inside the house. For leaks, drips, slow or blocked drains, and faucets or toilets that need fixing or replacing.

02

Water heaters

The tank that makes the house's hot water, and the hot and cold lines that meet at its top. Worth a call when it leaks, runs out of hot water fast, or the water comes out rusty.

Heating

Keeping the house warm through a Grand Lake winter. Worth a call when rooms stay cold, the heat cycles on and off more than usual, or the system starts making new noises.

Find your five shut-offs.

Most houses have the same five valves. Knowing where they are turns a burst pipe into a mop job. Tap a valve on the drawing.

Valve 1 of 5

Main shut-off

Where the water line enters the house, often in the basement or a utility room near the meter. Closing it stops water to the whole house.

Turn valves slowly. If one is stuck or starts to drip when you turn it, leave it and call a plumber.
