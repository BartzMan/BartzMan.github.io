The panel swap, the rewired kitchen, the charger for the car that parks outside. We walk it, measure the real load, and give you the number in writing before anything opens up.

  • Service panels & upgrades — sized for the load you actually carry, labeled so the next person can read it

The outlet that's loose, the switch that clicks, the dimmer that buzzes when the dining light is on. Small jobs get the same walk-through and the same written number as the big ones.

  • Outlets, switches & dimmers — loose, dead or misbehaving: fixed, replaced or moved where you want them
  • Circuits traced — the one that buzzes, the one that drops out, the one nobody can find on the panel

  • Breakers that keep tripping — found and fixed, not just reset for the fourth time
  • Flickering that won't quit — ruled out bulb-first, then chased to the real cause
