The cross-section

Four rings of tree work.

Cut a trunk and you can read its whole life from the bark to the heart. The work reads the same way: from the outer crown down to the ground it grows in.

01

Outer ring

Tree trimming & pruning

Deadwood out, crossing limbs opened up, branches lifted off the roof and the driveway. Shrubs around the house too.

02

Second ring

Tree removal

When a tree has to go, it comes down in pieces, cut and lowered in an order that keeps the house, fence and lawn clear.

03

Third ring

Stump grinding & removal

The stump is ground down below the grass, so the spot can be mowed, planted or turned into a bed.

04

The heart

Grass installation

New grass where the stump was, or over a bare patch the tree shaded out for years.

Up in the tree

Big trees come down in sections.

A big tree in a Plano backyard rarely has room to fall. So it comes down the other way round: top first, a piece at a time, each cut planned around the roof, the fence and the lawn.

  1. Crown firstThe climber takes the top and the small limbs out of the canopy.
  2. Limbs loweredHeavy limbs are cut and lowered, not dropped onto the yard.
  3. Trunk in roundsThe trunk comes down in short rounds that can be rolled and hauled.
  4. Brush through the chipperBranches go through the chipper and the driveway is swept.

Questions

Asked before the first cut.

Four things people tend to ask about a tree job. Anything else, call or text.

Q1When is the right time to trim a live oak?

In North Texas, many tree people leave live oaks alone from about February through June, when oak wilt spreads most easily, and do the bigger oak cuts in the cooler months. If a limb is broken or hanging over the roof, that is a different conversation: call or text and describe it.

Q2What does stump grinding leave behind?

A shallow hole filled with ground wood chips where the stump was. The chips can be raked into a bed or hauled off, and if you want grass back on the spot, grass installation is on the list too.

Q3Is clean-up part of a tree job?

Clean-up and the driveway both come up in what reviewers write about Best One. Ask about the brush, the logs and the chips when you call, so the plan for them is settled before the first cut.

Q4What should I send when I text?

A photo of the whole tree from far enough back to see the house or fence near it, a closer photo of the problem, and the neighborhood or cross street in Plano or wherever you are in the Metroplex.
