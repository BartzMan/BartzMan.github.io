The work

Bathrooms, kitchens & built-ins.

Three rooms of work — flip between them.

  • Full bathroom remodels

    Demolition to fixtures: tubs, walk-in showers, niches, vanities — and the tile that ties the room together.

  • Walk-in showers & tubs

    Freestanding tubs, curbless entries, benches and niches — waterproofed first, tiled second.

  • Kitchen remodels

    Layouts, cabinets, counters and floors — the kitchen rebuilt around how it actually gets used.

  • Backsplashes & counters

    Herringbone, mosaic or field tile over stone and solid counters — the counter's best frame.

  • Built-in cabinetry

    Bookcases, media walls and window seats built into the room — not bought for it.

  • Shelving & storage walls

    Mudroom lockers, closet systems and pantry walls — storage that disappears into the trim.

Useful

Planning a bathroom remodel?

Decide these five before demolition day — the rest of the plan falls out of them.

1
The tub

Freestanding or built-in? It sets the room's center — and the plumbing rough-in before anything else.

2
The shower

Walk-in size, curb or no curb, bench, niche. Decided on paper, not mid-demo.

3
The tile

Pattern and scale first — herringbone, mosaic, large format. Grout color second: grout is the frame.

4
The storage

Vanity, medicine cabinet, towel tower — how much stuff the room has to hide, and where it goes.

5
The order of days

Demolition, rough-in, waterproofing, tile, fixtures. Each step gates the next one.

Bring this list to the first call — five answers and the rest of the plan writes itself.
