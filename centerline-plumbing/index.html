N-1General notes

Generalnotes.

1Do you work on backflow assemblies?

Yes. Backflow assemblies and the water service lines that feed a building are one of our five lines of work. Call or text (954) 868-0578 with the size and location of the assembly and a photo of it, and tell us whether it needs a repair, a replacement or a new installation.

2Can you fit out a commercial restroom?

Yes. Restroom and fixture installation is one of our five lines: vanities, showers, wall-hung urinals and drinking fountains with bottle fillers, like the ones on sheet P-104. Send the plans or a list of fixtures and we will talk it through.

3There is a leak in our commercial building. What do we do?

Call or text (954) 868-0578. Tell us where the water shows up, on which floor, and whether you can shut it off. A short video or a few photos help us understand it before we arrive.

4What should I send with a request?

Photos of the problem or the space, the site address, the kind of building, and the easiest way and time to reach you.
