04 Signs

Signs a flat roof needs attention.

  1. Ponding water

    Puddles that are still sitting there a day or two after the rain has stopped.

  2. Soft or sagging spots

    Look from the ground or a window for dips in the roof line. Stay off the roof itself.

  3. Stains inside

    Brown rings on a ceiling or wall under the roof, especially after rain.

01What are the signs a flat roof needs attention?

Water that stays in puddles a day or two after rain, soft or sagging spots you can see from the ground or a window, brown stains on ceilings or walls inside, and seams or edges that are lifting. A look at the roof itself tells you more than its age does.

02What does a flat roof tear-off involve?

In general, the old roofing comes off down to the boards, damaged boards are replaced with new decking, and a membrane goes over the deck. The photos on this page follow one garage roof through those steps.

03What is a membrane roof?

A membrane roof is a continuous waterproof sheet laid over the roof deck. It is common on flat and low-slope roofs because there are very few joints for water to find.

04What should I send in a text?
