Before you call, check five things

The shop keeps a short list of easy checks homeowners run themselves. Waste a minute, save a trip — or phone it in with the answer ready.

check 1 of 5

Slow-draining sink

Look in the basket strainer first — hair and crumbs sit right there. Lift what you can by hand. If the water still lags, the trap or the line is the trouble, and a pro can clear it from the clean-out.

These are general homeowner checks, not orders for your house. Water problems and anything electrical: turn it off at the valve or breaker, then call.
