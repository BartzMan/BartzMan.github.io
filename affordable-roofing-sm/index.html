What’s in a roof quote?

Open a line to see what it covers and what to look for in the scope.

01Tear-off+

Removing the old roof covering exposes the layers beneath it. Check how many existing layers the scope includes.

02Decking+

Decking is the wood base the roof sits on. Look for how damaged sections will be identified and handled.

03Underlayment+

This layer goes between the decking and the roof covering. Look for the material and the parts of the roof it will cover.

04Flashing+

Flashing directs water around joints and openings. Check what the scope says about edges, walls and roof penetrations.

05Ventilation+

Vents allow air to move through a vented attic. Look for which existing vents stay and what will change.

06Clean-up+

This covers how old material and job debris leave the site. Check who handles haul-away and what clean-up includes.
