Sheet 06 · Hard water

Hard water? 4 signs at home.

Tap each sign to read it. This is general home advice, not a test of your water.

Before you call: have handy

  1. What you see or hear
  2. Where in the house it is
  3. How old the fixture or system is
  4. A photo you can email
Email the photo
01Spots on glasses and dishes

White spots or a cloudy film left on glasses after they dry can be a sign of hard water.

02Scale on faucets and showerheads

Crusty white or chalky build-up around faucets, showerheads and the inside of a kettle is a common hard-water sign.

03Soap that will not lather

If soap and shampoo lather poorly, or towels and laundry feel stiff, the minerals in the water may be the reason.

04A slow shower or weak tap

Scale can clog the tiny holes in a showerhead or a faucet aerator, so the flow slowly weakens.
