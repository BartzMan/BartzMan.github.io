Privacy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This notice explains how Emerson Heating and Air Conditioning (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this website or contact us. We are a heating and air conditioning business in Chardon, Ohio.

What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

If you call, text, email or message us on Facebook, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your number, your email address and what you tell us about your heating or cooling system.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange a visit or a quote.
  • To keep a simple record of the work we discuss or carry out for you.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

Only with the services we use to run the business (for example our phone, email and website hosting providers), or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and job notes only as long as we need them to answer you, do the work and keep reasonable business records.

Website basics

Like most websites, the server that hosts this site may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site working and secure. The site may record anonymous visit counts. It does not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call (440) 742-2889 or email emersonhvacr@gmail.com.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
