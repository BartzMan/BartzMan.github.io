What we do

The job ledger: top of the house to the floor.

Five kinds of work, one walk-through. Every job starts with a look at the roof, the attic or the rooms, then a written scope so you know exactly what is being replaced, repaired or finished before anyone climbs a ladder.

  1. 01

    Roof replacement

    Tear-offDeckingUnderlayment

    Old shingles off down to the deck, soft or rotten decking cut out and replaced, then new underlayment, flashing and architectural shingles laid course by course from the eaves up.

  2. 02

    Roof repair

    Hail & windLeaksRidge caps

    Lifted or missing shingles, leaking valleys and pipe boots, cracked ridge caps and the spots storms work loose. Patches are matched to the existing shingle as closely as the supplier allows.

  3. 03

    Interior paint

    WallsCeilingsTrim

    Walls, ceilings, doors and trim prepped, patched and rolled in the colour you pick, with furniture covered and floors protected while the work is going on.

  4. 04

    Flooring

    PlankLaminateTile

    Laminate, vinyl plank and tile laid over a levelled subfloor, finished with new baseboards and quarter-round so the edges run clean along every wall.

  5. 05

    Kitchen & bath remodels

    CabinetsCountersTile

    Cabinets, counters, backsplash and shower tile, fixtures and lighting, planned around the plumbing you already have so the room is back in use sooner.

Tear-off day: old shingles in the yard, bare deck on the roof.

After a North Texas storm, look for

  • Dents in gutters, vents and the metal around the chimney
  • Shingle granules collecting at the bottom of the downspouts
  • Lifted, creased or missing shingles, seen from the yard
  • New stains on ceilings or in the attic after rain

Roofing in Dallas

Built for Texas hail, heat and wind.

North Texas roofs take a beating every spring and bake every summer. A replacement goes on in layers, and each one does a different job. Here is what goes down, from the deck up.

  1. 1

    Decking

    Checked sheet by sheet once the old roof is off; soft or rotten sections are replaced.

  2. 2

    Synthetic underlayment

    A second line of defence under the shingles for the sideways rain Dallas storms bring.

  3. 3

    Flashing and pipe boots

    New metal at every wall, chimney and vent pipe, where most leaks actually start.

  4. 4

    Architectural shingles

    Heavier dimensional shingles, nailed course by course in a colour that suits the street.

  5. 5

    Ridge and soffit venting

    Sized to the attic so summer heat moves out instead of baking the decking.
