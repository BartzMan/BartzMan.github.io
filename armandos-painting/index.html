Five jobs taped to the board.

Each sheet is one kind of job: the paint, and the remodeling work that has to be right before the paint goes on. Sheets marked their photo are from Armando's own jobs.

Sheet 01 their photo

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, doors and trim inside the house. You need it when a room looks tired or scuffed, or is about to change colour.

Sheet 02 drawing

Exterior painting

Siding, trim, doors and eaves on the outside of the house. You need it when paint is peeling, chalky or faded by the Houston sun.

Sheet 03 illustrative stock photo

Drywall, texture & trim

Patching holes and cracks, matching wall texture, and setting baseboards and casing. You need it after a repair, a leak or a remodel, before any paint.

Sheet 04 their photo

Kitchen remodels

New cabinets, a new island and the walls around them, from the framing to the last coat. You need it when the kitchen's layout or cabinets no longer work.

Sheet 05 their photo

Floors

Plank flooring laid room to room, with baseboards set and painted after. You need it when worn carpet or tired floors are the next thing to go.
