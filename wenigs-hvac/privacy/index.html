Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Wenig's Heating & Cooling ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit this website, request a quote, or contact us by phone, text or email.

1. Who we are

Wenig's Heating & Cooling, Marion, Ohio 43302. Phone: (740) 383-5012. Email: ccw@wenigsinc.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Job details you give us: the equipment involved, what it is doing, your town, and anything else you tell us in a message.
  • Technical information our website host may record automatically, such as IP address, browser, device and pages requested, to run and secure the site.

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

3. The request form

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

4. How we use information

  • To reply to your questions and quote requests.
  • To schedule, carry out and follow up on work.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records, and to operate and secure this website.

5. Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of receiving service.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example website hosting and email), when the law requires it, or with a successor if the business is sold.

7. Other services

This site links to Google Maps, which has its own privacy practices that we do not control.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information as long as we need it to respond to you, complete work and meet business, tax and legal obligations, then delete it or keep it only in a form that does not identify you.

9. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by calling or emailing us. We will honor requests to the extent the law that applies to you requires.

10. Security and children

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or email service is completely secure, so please do not send payment card numbers, bank details or government ID numbers through the site. This site is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

11. Changes and questions

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Call (740) 383-5012 or email ccw@wenigsinc.com.
