What we wire up.

From a single outlet that stopped working to a full panel swap. If it carries current around the house or the shop, ask.

Breaker keeps tripping? Call
  1. 01

    Panel upgrades and breakers

    Replace tired fuse boxes and undersized panels, add circuits, sort out nuisance tripping, and label every breaker so the next person understands it.

  2. 02

    Wiring and rewiring

    New circuits, remodel wiring and dedicated lines for ranges, dryers, EV chargers and workshop tools, in older homes and new builds alike.

  3. 03

    Lighting, inside and out

    Recessed cans, under-cabinet LED, dimmers, pendants, ceiling fans, landscape and security lighting, laid out so the light lands where you need it.

  4. 04

    Mini-split and equipment hookups

    Disconnects, whips and dedicated circuits for ductless units, pool pumps, water heaters, hot tubs and generators.

  5. 05

    Troubleshooting and repairs

    Dead outlets, flickering lights, warm switch plates, buzzing panels. We trace it, explain it, and fix the cause instead of the symptom.

  6. 06

    Outlets, switches and safety devices

    GFCI and AFCI protection, USB outlets, smart switches, whole-home surge protection at the panel, smoke and CO detector wiring.

How a job goes

Four stops, one circuit.

You call or email

Tell us what is happening and where. A photo of the panel or the problem spot helps.

We look and scope it

We check the panel and the run, then talk you through what the job involves before any work starts.

We do the work

Power off, the work done neatly, circuits labelled and everything tested before we pack up.

You see it run

We switch it on with you, walk you through what changed and answer any questions.
