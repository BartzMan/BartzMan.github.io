Privacy policy

Who we are

This website belongs to Main Drain, a plumbing and drain cleaning business based in Middletown, Ohio. You can reach us at (513) 465-2525 or jdabfrog@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you. If you call, text or email us, we see the details you send in the same way.

We do not use advertising trackers on this website. Like most websites, the server that hosts it may keep short technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer your request, talk about the job, arrange a visit and keep a record of work we have done for you. We do not sell your details or give them to anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and job records for as long as they are useful for the work and our records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Contact us by phone or email and we will take care of it.

Other websites

This site links to our Facebook page and our Google listing. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
