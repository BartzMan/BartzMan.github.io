What we do

Six zones.One cool house.

Open a register to see what each zone covers. Whatever your system is doing, call and tell us, and we will work out which zone it lives in.

ZONE 01

AC repair

The air coming out of the vents has stopped feeling like relief. Call and tell us what the system is doing, and we will come and find out why.

ZONE 02

AC installs

Putting in a new air conditioner or replacing an old one: the outdoor unit, the indoor coil and the connections between them.

ZONE 03

Heating

Houston winters are short but they do arrive. Furnaces and heating that come on when you ask them to.

ZONE 04

Ductwork

Cool air only helps if it gets where it is going. Ducts, vents and the runs between them.

ZONE 05

Thermostats

The little box on the wall that tells the whole system what you want from it.

ZONE 06

Tune-ups

Regular check-ups on air conditioners and heaters, so the first sign of trouble is not a hot afternoon.
