Sinks, tubs & toilets

Slow drains and stopped fixtures, cleared.

A slow sink, a tub that holds water, a toilet that will not clear. Tell us what the water is doing and we will take it from there.

02

Main line clearing

The big line from the house to the street.

When more than one fixture backs up, the trouble is often in the main. A drain machine runs the line and works at whatever is stuck.

03

Floor & basement drains

The drain in the floor, back in business.

Floor and basement drains catch whatever the house sends down. When one slows or backs up, we clear it so the water goes where it should.

04

Root-clogged lines

When roots find the pipe.

Roots can work into a line and catch whatever passes. If the same drain keeps slowing down, describe what you are seeing when you call.

Clean-out access

The door into the line.

A clean-out is the capped opening that lets a drain machine into the pipe. Not sure where yours is? Ask when you call.
