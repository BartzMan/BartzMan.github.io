Course 02service

Shingle roofs.

Asphalt shingles, the roof on most San Antonio streets: rows of tabs nailed in overlapping courses, with a cap along every hip and ridge. You need a new one when shingles are curling, cracking or coming loose across the roof.

  • Starter strip at the eave
  • Overlapping courses
  • Hip and ridge caps
exposure line

Course 03service

Metal roofs.

Long metal panels joined by raised seams that run from the ridge to the eave. Light-coloured metal throws back a lot of the Texas sun, and rain runs straight off. You need it when you want a roof built in long runs instead of single tabs.

  • Panels cut to the run
  • Raised seams
  • Ridge and edge trim
exposure line

Course 04service

Roof repair.

Putting back what wind, hail or age took off one part of the roof: a few missing shingles slid in under the course above, a lifted edge nailed down, a cracked vent boot replaced. You need it when the damage is in one spot and the rest of the roof is sound.

Course 05side by side

Shingle or metal?

Both are on S&L's photos. Flip between them to see how each one is put together and where it shines.

  • How it goes on: tabs nailed in rows, each row covering the nails of the one below.
  • The look: many colours, and the familiar texture on most San Antonio streets.
  • Repairs: a few blown-off tabs can be slid in and nailed without touching the rest.
  • How it goes on: long panels cut to the run, joined at raised seams.
  • The sun: light metal reflects more of the Texas sun than dark shingles.
  • Repairs: damage is fixed panel by panel, along the whole run.
