Three kinds of El Paso roof.

FLAT

Tap a roof type to see Vitru’s own photo of it.

Type 01

Flat roof with a parapet

A flat roof drains slowly, and the low wall around its edge, the parapet, frames it. Seams, drains and that edge are where a flat roof is looked at first.

Type 02

Roof with a tile edge

Many El Paso homes mix a clay tile pitch with a flat section. The join between them gets its own careful coating so the two roofs meet cleanly.

Type 03

Balcony deck

A balcony or patio deck takes sun and foot traffic all day. A coating gives it one sealed, light-coloured surface up to the railing and the wall.

What goes on the roof.

  • Flat roofs

    Building or rebuilding the flat roof on a home or small building, from the framing and decking up.

  • Reflective roof coatings

    A white coating rolled or brushed over the roof surface so it reflects more of the sun than a dark roof does.

  • Seams and repairs

    Sealing the lines and joins where a flat roof is most likely to open up, by hand.

  • Decks and balconies

    Coated surfaces for the outdoor decks that sit on top of a house.

What sunlight does to a roof.

A dark surface takes in much of the sunlight that lands on it and turns it into heat. A white surface sends more of it back up. That is the whole idea behind a reflective coating.
