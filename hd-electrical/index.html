Floor 3Before you call

Breaker keeps tripping in an old apartment? Four checks.

Think back.

Which heater, kettle, hair dryer or microwave was on when it tripped? Several big appliances on one circuit is the usual cause.

Note the room.

Which lights and outlets went dead? If it is always the same room, say so to whoever you call.

Once, not twice.

Unplug what was running. If you can reach the breaker without opening a cover, switch it fully off, then on, once. Never open the panel cover.

Then stop.

If it trips again straight away, or you smell burning, leave it off and call an electrician.
