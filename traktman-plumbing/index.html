V‑1The building has no heat. What should I do?

Call (718) 418-3833 and tell us what the boiler is doing: no flame, a tripped switch, low pressure on the gauge, cold radiators on one floor only. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility first.

V‑2Water is coming through the ceiling. What first?

Turn off the fixture above or the valve that feeds it, and if you can't find it, the main shut-off by the water meter. Keep water away from lights and outlets, then call or text us with a photo of the stain and the room above.

V‑3What does “plumbing violations removed” mean?

If the building has open plumbing violations, text us a photo of the notice. We'll look at what it lists and talk you through the plumbing work involved in getting them removed.

V‑4Do you install backflow devices and water meters?

Yes. Backflow installation and water meter installation are both part of the work, along with plumbing repair, leaks, heating and boiler rooms.

V‑5What should I send when I get in touch?

Your name, the neighborhood or ZIP, the kind of building, what's going on and a photo or two. A text to (718) 418-3833 is the quickest way.
