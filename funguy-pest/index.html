06Remarks

Notes.

Four things people ask before they book.

  1. R1What is a WDO inspection?

    WDO stands for wood-destroying organisms, such as termites. A WDO inspection looks for visible signs of them in a home and puts what was seen into a written report, which buyers, sellers, realtors and lenders often ask for during a sale.

  2. R2Does a VA or FHA loan need one?

    Many VA and FHA home loans ask for a WDO inspection. Your lender or realtor can tell you if yours does. FunGuy does VA and FHA loan WDO inspections in Orange Park and the Jacksonville area.

  3. R3How long does the report take?

    Ask when you book. Call (904) 631-5794 with your closing date and you will get a straight answer on timing for your property.

  4. R4What should I send?

    The property address and your closing date. Realtors and lenders can add who should get the report.
