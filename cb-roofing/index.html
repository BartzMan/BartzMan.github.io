Plate 01 · Bright

Shingle roofs

An asphalt shingle roof is the layered roof most houses around here wear. When it is worn out, the old layers come off and a new roof goes on.

  • Tear-off and new shingles
  • Hips, ridges and caps
  • Drip edge along the eaves
  • Dormers and bay-window roofs

Plate 02 · Russet

Roof repairs

A repair fixes one problem area instead of the whole roof: shingles that blew off, a leak above a ceiling stain, a soft spot or a lifted edge.

  • Missing or blown-off shingles
  • Leaks traced from the stain inside
  • Lifted edges and popped nails
  • Wear after a storm
Ask about a repair →

Plate 03 · Umber

Flashing

Flashing is the metal that seals the joints: around a chimney, where a roof meets a wall and down the valleys. Many leaks start right there.

  • Around chimneys
  • Roof-to-wall and step flashing
  • Valleys between two slopes
  • Pipe boots and openings
Ask about flashing →

Plate 04 · Verdigris

Vents & small roofs

Vents let a roof breathe so the attic stays drier. The small roofs over bay windows and dormers need the same care as the main one.

  • Box vents and ridge vents
  • Bay-window and porch roofs
  • Dormer tops and sides
  • Attic airflow questions
