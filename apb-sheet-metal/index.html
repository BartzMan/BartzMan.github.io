03Metal or shingle

Before you choose

Metal or shingle?

Both are good roofs. These are the plain differences, and the questions worth asking whoever quotes you.

  • Long panels. Fewer joints run across the roof, and the raised seams carry water down.
  • Details decide it. Flashing at chimneys, valleys and edges is where metal roofs are won or lost.
  • It moves. Metal expands and shrinks with temperature, so panels have to be fastened to allow for it.
  • Sound. Rain can be louder on metal unless the roof is built to damp it.
  • Familiar. Shingles are everywhere, so most roofers know them well.
  • Many small pieces. The overlap and the layers underneath do the waterproofing.
  • Patch repairs. A few damaged shingles can often be swapped without redoing the roof.
  • Mixed roofs. Many houses combine shingle with metal on a bay, porch or turret.

Tick them off as you ask. Good answers are specific.
