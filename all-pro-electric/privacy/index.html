We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example website hosting and messaging providers), when the law requires it, or with a successor if the business is sold.

6. Third-party services

This site may link to services such as Google Maps. They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information as long as we need it to respond to you, complete work and meet business, tax and legal obligations, then delete it or keep it only in a form that does not identify you.

8. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by calling or texting us. We will honor requests to the extent the law that applies to you requires.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or messaging service is completely secure, so please do not send bank details or government ID numbers through the site. This site is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.
