Before you call

Good questions.

My AC is running but not cooling. What should I check first?

Make sure the thermostat is on cool and set below the room temperature, and that the filter is not clogged. Then look at the outdoor unit: is the fan turning? If you see ice on the lines, or the system runs all day without catching up, switch it off and text a photo of the outdoor unit and the thermostat to (352) 702-7750.

How often should an AC be serviced in Central Florida heat?

Systems here run for most of the year, so a check at least once a year is a sensible minimum, and many owners book it before summer. Pollen, dust and humidity build up on the coils quickly, so coil cleaning is part of the conversation.

Do you work on new-construction homes?

Yes. New-construction HVAC is part of the work: line sets, drains and ductwork roughed in while the walls are open, then the system installed once the house is ready for it. Call or text (352) 702-7750 with the address and where the build is at.

What should I text when I ask for service?

A photo of the outdoor unit, a photo of the label on its side, a line on what it is doing, and your town. That is usually enough to start the conversation.
