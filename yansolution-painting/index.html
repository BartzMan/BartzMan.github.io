The ledger

What goes on the wall

  • No. 01

    Wallpaper hanging

    Damask, botanical, geometric or plain: hung plumb, matched at the seams and trimmed clean at the ceiling and baseboard.

  • No. 02

    Removal and wall prep

    Old paper stripped, walls patched and primed, so the new surface sits flat and stays put.

  • No. 03

    Interior painting

    Walls, ceilings, trim and doors: cut in sharp, rolled even, and the room put back the way you left it.

  • No. 04

    Repairs before coats

    Cracks, dents and water marks fixed first, because paint only shows what is under it.

How a room gets done

    Strips matched and hung, or coats cut in and rolled, room by room.

    Seams checked, trim touched up, floors left clean.
