EL. 5,870 FTTrail 1 · Sun side, shade side

Which wall takesthe sun?

Pick a time of day, or step into the kitchen. In thin high-country air the sun is hard on a paint film, and each wall of a house gets a different dose.

Lit now: the south wall

Noon: the strongest dose.

With the sun high, the south wall and the roofline get the most direct light. In thin air, ultraviolet is strong, which is why colour fades and paint can chalk on the sunniest walls first.

  • Fading or a chalky film when you rub the wall
  • Dried, greying wood trim
  • Caulk that has cracked or pulled away

Shade side: the north wall stays cooler and damper, so watch for mildew and slow-drying paint there.

Step 1 of 5

Clean and degrease.

Kitchen cabinets collect grease and fingerprints, and paint will not hold to a dirty surface. Everything is cleaned before anything else happens.

Why it matters: cabinet paint takes daily wear, so the prep decides how long it lasts.

Inside, outside,and in between.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings and trim freshened up, from a single room to a whole house. You need it when paint is scuffed or tired, a colour has dated, or you are about to sell.

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Doors, trim and baseboards
  • Patching and prep before the first coat

Exterior painting

Siding, trim and doors, protected against sun, dry air and snow. You need it when paint chalks, fades, peels or the caulk has cracked.

  • Siding, fascia and trim
  • Front doors and garage doors
  • Scraping, repair and priming of bare spots

Cabinet painting and remodeling

Existing cabinets repainted instead of replaced, and bigger updates planned around the room. You need it when the layout works but the finish is tired, or a room needs more than a coat of paint.

  • Kitchen and bath cabinets
  • Remodeling projects

EL. 7,310 FTTrail 3 · Check each spring

Colorado exterior checkeach spring: 5 things.

Walk around the house from the ground on a dry day and follow the trail. Tap each marker to see what to look for. If wood feels soft or siding is damaged, a painter or contractor can take a closer look.

Marker 1 of 5

Peeling on the south and west sides.

These walls get the most sun, so they are where a paint film gives up first. Look for lifting, flaking or bubbling paint, especially near the bottom of the siding.
