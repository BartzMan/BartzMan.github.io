From bare decking to ridge cap

Five jobs a roof usually needs, explained the way you would want them explained over the fence.

Straight off the roof.

Old shingles lifted around a vent
01

Tear-off & replacement

The old roof comes off down to the wood and a new one goes on. You need it when shingles are worn out or the roof leaks in more than one spot.

New shingles, finished ridge
02

Shingle installation

New shingles laid in overlapping rows, with caps along the peaks. This is the part of the roof you see from the street.

Chimney base and flashing
03

Flashing, vents & chimneys

Flashing is the metal and sealant where a roof meets a chimney, wall or pipe. These joints are where many leaks begin.

Bare decking, blue chalk line
04

Decking repair

Decking is the wood under the shingles. Soft or damaged boards are cut out and replaced before any new roofing goes on.

05

Roof repair

Replacing a few damaged shingles or resealing a vent when the whole roof does not need to come off. A small fix, done before a leak spreads.

After the hail, check from the ground.

Hail is a fact of life in North Texas. Walk around the house and tap each numbered spot as you look. Stay on the ground: never climb up to check. Also look at ceilings inside for new brown stains.
