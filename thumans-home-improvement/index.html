After an Ohio winter, look for

  • Stains on ceilings under the eaves, a sign of ice dams
  • Heavy icicles or ice building along the gutters
  • Lifted, creased or missing shingles, seen from the yard
  • New stains on ceilings or in the attic after a thaw or rain

Seen one of these? Snap a photo from the ground and send it over. Do not climb up to check.

Roofing

Built for Ohio winters, thaws and summer sun.

Ohio roofs go through freeze-thaw cycles, snow load and hot summers. A replacement goes on in layers, and each one does a different job. Here is what goes down, from the deck up. This is general information, not a claim about any one job.

  1. 1

    Decking

    The wood deck the roof is built on, checked once the old roof is off.

  2. 2

    Underlayment

    A second line of defence under the shingles against wind-driven rain and meltwater.

  3. 3

    Flashing and pipe boots

    Metal at every wall, chimney and vent pipe, where many leaks start.

  4. 4

    Shingles

    Overlapping courses nailed from the eaves up to shed water.

  5. 5

    Ridge and soffit venting

    Lets attic air move so heat and moisture do not sit under the deck.

Planning a remodel? Four questions to answer first

List every room you want touched. Floors and paint often run through doorways, so it helps to plan them together rather than one room at a time.

Decide the most you are comfortable spending before you collect ideas. A ceiling on the budget makes every choice after it easier.

Note what you are keeping: cabinets, flooring or the layout. Moving plumbing or walls changes the size of a job a lot.

Gather photos of finishes you like: a floor, a counter, a cabinet colour. Send them along with your quote request.
