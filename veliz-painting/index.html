Asked at the door.

01Inside or outside first, with Georgia weather?

Outside paint wants dry days, so exterior work is planned around rain and the hottest, stickiest stretches of a Georgia summer. Interior rooms can be painted most of the year. Send a few photos and the timing gets worked out for your house.

02My siding paint is worn and flaking. Can it be saved?

Often, yes. Worn areas are repaired first and then the siding is repainted, the way a chimney and siding with worn paint were handled on a job in Duluth. Photos of the worst spots help.

03What colour should my front door be?

On a white house with dark trim, a black door is the classic match, like the double door on this page. Red, grey and blue work too. Bring a colour you like, or a photo of a door you have seen.

04What should I send to get a quote?

Photos of the walls, rooms or the outside of the house, your town, and what you want painted. Text them to (770) 524-8759, email velizpainting2023@gmail.com, or use the request form.
