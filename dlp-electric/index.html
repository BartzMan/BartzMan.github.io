Questions, wired up

What is old work wiring?

Wiring added to a house where the walls and ceilings are already finished. Cable is fished through the existing walls, and the boxes are a type made to clamp into an opening cut in finished plaster or drywall.

What is new work wiring?

Wiring done while the framing is open, for example in an addition or a gutted room. The boxes are fixed straight to the studs and the cable is run before the walls are closed up.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. Small jobs come up again and again in DLP Electric's Google reviews: a fixture, an outlet, a switch. Call, text or email with what you need.

Can recessed lights go into a ceiling that is already finished?

Often, yes. It depends on the joists and what is above the ceiling. Send a photo of the room and how many lights you have in mind.

What should I send with my message?

Your town, what you need done, and a photo if it helps: the fixture, the room, or the panel with its door open. Email dlpsrv@aol.com or text (973) 223-8276.
