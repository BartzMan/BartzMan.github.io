The diagnosis

Find the real problem before the parts list.

An AC that runs but won't cool can come down to several parts. Replacing them on a guess gets expensive, so the first job is finding which one it is, then explaining it in plain words so you can decide what gets fixed.

  • Weak capacitorFan or compressor won't start
  • Refrigerant leakLow charge, warm air
  • Dirty coilLong run times
  • Frozen evaporatorIce on the copper line
  • Clogged drainWater in the attic pan
  • Failed motorOutdoor fan not turning

My AC is running but not cooling. What should I check?

Make sure the thermostat is on cool and set below the room temperature, and that the air filter isn't clogged. Then look at the outdoor unit. If its fan isn't spinning, or the large copper line is covered in ice, switch the system off and call. Running it while it's frozen can damage the compressor.

Water is dripping from the attic unit. What do I do?

Attic air handlers sit over a drain pan with a drain line. When the line clogs or the pan rusts through, the water ends up in your ceiling. Turn the system off at the thermostat, put a bucket under the drip, and call or text a photo of the unit and the pan.

Do you work on rooftop and package units?

Yes. Rooftop and package units are one of the services Husky Comfort Air lists, from repairs on the roof to lifting a replacement unit into place.
