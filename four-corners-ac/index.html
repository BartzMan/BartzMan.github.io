Four questions. Straight answers.

NW

The AC is running but the house isn't cooling. What now?

Check that the thermostat is set to cool and the filter isn't clogged. If the outdoor unit runs and the air from the vents is still warm, switch the system off and call or text. Running it hard can make the problem bigger.

NE

Is a tune-up before summer worth it?

In Central Florida the AC works most of the year. A tune-up checks the whole system before the hottest months, when a breakdown is hardest to live with.

SW

Can an air handler go in a closet?

Yes. Plenty of homes here keep the air handler in a hallway closet with the return grille low on the wall. The two closet installs on this page are exactly that.

SE

What should I text?

A photo of the label on the unit, outdoor or indoor, plus a wider shot of where it sits. The label shows the model and size. The wide shot shows the access.
