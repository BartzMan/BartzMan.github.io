03Backup power

Backup power, wired in.

A recent SK2 job: a backup generator interlock for a battery backup power station. The interlock lets the house run on backup power, and it stops the main breaker and the backup breaker being on at the same time.

  • Generator interlocksOn the panel you already have, for a portable generator or a battery station.
  • Inlet boxesAn outdoor inlet so the backup plugs in at the wall, not through a window.
  • Manual transfer switchesPick the circuits that matter and switch them over by hand.
