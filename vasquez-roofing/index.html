What we take on

Four jobs, one roofline.

Roof replacement

Replacement means taking the old roof off and building a new one on the same deck. It is for roofs that are worn out or past patching.

Roof repair

Repair fixes one problem, such as a leak, a flashing, a vent or a few lifted shingles, without redoing the whole roof.

Roofing for new construction

On a new build the roof goes onto the framing as the house is closed in, built to the pitch drawn on the plans.

Remodeling

Remodels and additions change the shape of a house. When the roofline changes, the new roof has to tie into the old one cleanly.

Start where your roof is

New build. Remodel. Repair.

On a new build, the plans set the pitch.

The roof goes on once the framing is up and the deck is closed in. The slope is already drawn, so the work is getting every layer onto it straight.

  1. Framing and deck
  2. Underlayment and flashing
  3. Shingles and ridge

On a remodel, the old roof sets the rules.

An addition has to meet the roof that is already there: the same pitch where it can, clean valleys where it cannot. The new lines should look like they were always part of the house.

  1. Match or step the pitch
  2. Tie in at the walls
  3. Valleys and flashing

On a repair, water tells you where to look.

A stain on the ceiling is rarely right under the hole. Repairs start by following the water back up the roof, usually to a flashing, a vent or a valley.

  1. Trace the water
  2. Find the opening
  3. Seal and replace
