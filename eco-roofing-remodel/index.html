Notes Before you call

Notes on the drawing.

N.1Should my roof be repaired or replaced?

It depends on what is under the shingles. Send a photo of the slope from the yard and of any stain inside, and we will tell you what we see and whether it needs a closer look on site.

N.2How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Ask us. It depends on the kitchen, the layout and how much you want to change. Send photos and rough measurements and we will talk it through with you.

N.3Do you frame additions?

Framing is one of the three things in our name. Send a plan, a sketch or photos of where the new room would go and we will talk it through.

N.4What should I send?

Photos and rough measurements. For a roof, the slope from the yard. For a kitchen or bath, the room from the doorway and the length of each wall.
