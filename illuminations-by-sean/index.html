Planning landscape lighting

Light the route first.

  1. Begin where you come in. Walk from the gate or driveway to the door and note where you lose sight of the route.

  2. Look for changes in direction. A few low lights can show the route without lighting the whole garden.

  3. Mark each change in level. Think about where the next tread should be visible from above and below.

  4. Once the route is clear, pick one tree or a planted area for an accent. Look from inside the house as well.
