Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Natchez Roofing ("we," "us") handles personal information when you visit this website, request an estimate, or contact us by phone, text or email.

1. Who we are

Natchez Roofing
Deer Park, TX
Phone: (346) 360-9691

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name and your phone number or email address.
  • Project details you give us: the kind of job, your location, and anything you tell us in a message or send as a photo.
  • Technical information our website host may record automatically, such as IP address, browser, device and pages requested, to operate and secure the site.

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising and does not use analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and estimate requests.
  • To schedule, price, perform and follow up on work.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records, and to operate and secure this website.

4. Calls, texts and messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of receiving service. When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example website hosting and messaging providers), when the law requires it, or with a successor if the business is sold.

6. Third-party services

This site may link to services such as Google Maps and Facebook. They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information as long as we need it to respond to you, complete work and meet business, tax and legal obligations, then delete it or keep it only in a form that does not identify you.

8. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by calling or messaging us. We will honor requests to the extent the law that applies to you requires.

9. Security and children

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or messaging service is completely secure, so please do not send financial account numbers or government ID numbers through the site. This site is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Call (346) 360-9691.
