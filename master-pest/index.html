    What we do

    Four kinds of pest work, one chameleon.

    Each one is for a different part of the property, so each one gets its own approach.

    1. 01

      Habitat: stucco and tile

      General household pest control

      The everyday visitors: ants, roaches, spiders and the rest. This is the service for pests that keep turning up inside the house or along the walls outside.

      Ask about it
    2. 02

      Habitat: attic and garage

      Rodent control

      For mice and rats: scratching in the ceiling, droppings in the garage, gnaw marks on boxes. It is the service to call when something is living where it should not be.

      Ask about it
    3. 03

      Habitat: lawn green

      Lawn and ornamental pest control

      Looks after the lawn, shrubs and palms. Chinch bugs and mole crickets can leave a lawn yellow and thin, and this is the service for pests like those.

      Try the lawn check
    4. 04

      Habitat: termite brown

      Termite and wood-destroying organism control

      For termites and other things that eat wood. Mud tubes, discarded wings or soft, hollow-sounding timber are all reasons to get the wood in a home looked at.

      Ask about it
