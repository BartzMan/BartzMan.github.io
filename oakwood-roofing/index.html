Skylight field notes

Where is the
water coming from?

Start indoors. Note where the moisture appears and what the weather was doing.

At the frame after rain

Stains beside the opening after rain can point to the flashing around the skylight. Take photos from indoors and note which edge is affected.

On the room side of the glass

Droplets on the inside surface during cold weather may be condensation, especially in a room that often feels humid.

Between the panes

Fogging that stays between the panes can point to a failed seal. Note whether you can wipe the moisture off the room-side surface.
