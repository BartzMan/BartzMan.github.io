When you’d need itAfter a move-out, a slammed door, an old picture hook, or a patch that keeps showing through.

Kitchen: what usually needs fixing
  • Sagging cabinet doors and loose handles
  • Scuffed walls behind the stove and sink
  • Trim that has pulled away from the wall
  • A shelf or pot rail that needs hanging
Living room: what usually needs fixing
  • Holes left by old TV mounts and pictures
  • Cracks above doors and windows
  • Tired walls and ceilings ready for paint
  • Built-in shelving made to fit an alcove
Bathroom: what usually needs fixing
  • Water stains and flaking paint on the ceiling
  • Loose towel bars, mirrors and fittings
  • Soft or stained drywall near the fixtures
  • Mold spots on walls and trim
Bedroom: what usually needs fixing
  • Closet doors that rub or will not close
  • Scuffed paint behind the bed
  • Baseboard that has come loose
  • Custom shelves and built-in storage
