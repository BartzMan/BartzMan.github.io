Run 05 · Questions

Before you call.

Short answers to what people ask first.

AHow do I get a quote?

Call (904) 282-5585, or send the request form below with what is going wrong and where the job is. We look at the work and tell you what it involves. Anything talked through on the phone is an estimate until you accept the quote.

BWater is coming through, or I smell gas. What first?

Protect yourself first. For water, turn off the main shut-off valve if you can reach it safely and keep clear of anything electrical. For a gas smell, leave the building and call 911 or your gas company from outside. Do not wait to hear back from us.

CCan you plumb a kitchen or bathroom remodel?

Yes, remodel work is half of what is lettered on the van. Moving a fixture, running new supply and drain lines before the walls close, then setting the new fixtures at the end.

DWhich areas do you cover?

Middleburg, the rest of Clay County and around the Jacksonville area. Tell us where you are and we will say straight away whether we can take the job.

EWhat should I put in the request?

Your name, the best phone number, what the problem is, where it is in the house or building, and when is good to reach you.
