MODE C1 · COOL

Central air & outdoor condensers

The outdoor condenser and the indoor coil work as a pair to carry heat out of the house. When the air at the vents stops feeling cold, this pair is where to look.

  • Vents blowing warm or weak air
  • Outdoor unit humming, fan not turning
  • Ice on the copper lines
Call about cooling

MODE H2 · HEAT

Heating for the cold snaps

When a cold front reaches Katy, the furnace or heat strips have to wake up after months off. A dusty smell for a few minutes is normal; heat that never arrives is not.

  • No warm air when the thermostat calls for heat
  • A burning smell that does not fade
  • The breaker trips when the heat comes on
Call about heating

MODE C3 · COIL

Worn & corroded condenser coils

The coil is the radiator of the outdoor unit: thin fins wrapped around copper tubes. Once the fins crumble like this, the unit cannot shed heat and works harder all summer.

  • Fins flaking or missing behind the guard
  • Green or white crust on the copper
  • Long run times on hot afternoons
Call about a coil

MODE A4 · SHOP AIR

Air handlers in shops & metal buildings

Workshops, warehouses and steel buildings often hang the air handler from the roof framing, with long refrigerant lines and drain lines running along the walls.

  • Hot spots on the shop floor
  • Water dripping from a hung unit
  • Lines and drains run a long way
Call about a shop

MODE C5 · COLD ROOM

Walk-in coolers

A walk-in cooler is the insulated room behind a kitchen or shop. It only holds its cold when the door latch, hinges and gasket close tight and the cooling keeps up.

  • Door that will not pull shut
  • Frost building up around the frame
  • The box feels warmer than it should
Call about a cooler

Read it before you call.

Plain checks any homeowner can do first. Pick what you are seeing; the screen shows what to look at.

SYSTEM CHECK● READING

Warm air at the vents

  1. Thermostat on COOL, set below the room temperature.
  2. Pull the air filter. If it is grey and clogged, change it.
  3. Check the breaker for the outdoor unit.

Ice on the copper lines

  1. Switch the system OFF and the fan ON so the ice melts.
  2. Change the air filter.
  3. If it ices up again, turn it off and call a pro.

Outdoor unit clogged

  1. Keep grass and leaves about two feet clear of it.
  2. Power off at the disconnect, then rinse the fins gently with a garden hose.
  3. Fins crumbling away? That is coil trouble.

No heat on a cold day

  1. Thermostat on HEAT, set above the room temperature.
  2. Fresh batteries in the thermostat.
  3. Check the breaker for the furnace or air handler.
