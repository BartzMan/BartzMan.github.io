WHAT WE WORK ON

Two seasons, one crew

Cooling, heating, and the gear that runs all year — the card that matters right now is the one lit up.

Season: cool

Cooling

Condensers, air handlers and the ducts between them. We clean, test and repair — and when a system is done, we size the replacement to the house, not to the box on the truck.

  • AC repair & tune-ups — gauges, capacitors, coils
  • Air handlers & duct work — attic installs, sealed and strapped
  • Drain lines & condensate — cleared before they drip through the ceiling

Season: warm

Heating

Furnaces in attics and closets all over Oakland. We service gas furnaces, test venting, swap blowers and igniters, and catch the small stuff before it becomes a cold night.

  • Furnace repair & tune-ups — ignition, blowers, safety checks
  • Gas line & venting — tested, sealed, done to standard
  • Thermostat swaps — wired in, explained, set to your week

All year

The in-between

The gear that runs in both seasons, plus the plumbing side of the name. Filters, air quality, water heaters, drains — and the small leak you found on a Tuesday.

  • Air quality & filtration — right size, actually fitted
  • Water heaters & drains — the plumbing half of the name
  • Seasonal maintenance — one visit per season, notes kept

HOW A SERVICE CALL RUNS

Four steps, no mysteries

  1. 01

    The call

    (510) 692-5718. Say what's blowing, leaking, or refusing to start — a photo of the unit label helps more than a long story.

  2. 02

    The look

    Attic, closet, or side yard — we open it up, read the data plate, and find the actual problem instead of guessing at it.

  3. 03

    The number

    Parts and labor in writing before any work starts. If the job grows once we're inside, you hear it from us first, not after.

  4. 04

    The test

    Temps checked at the registers, drain tested, thermostat explained, and the space left cleaner than we found it.
