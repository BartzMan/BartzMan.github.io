Ridge to gutter

Every layer, top to bottom.

A roof is a stack of layers, lapped like the shingles themselves. These are the six places a roof check looks.

  1. 1Ridge capThe top line, where wind gets under worn caps first.
  2. 2Shingle coursesLifted, cracked, bruised or missing tabs.
  3. 3FlashingChimneys, walls and vents: where most leaks start.
  4. 4UnderlaymentThe layer under the shingles that backs them up.
  5. 5Deck and drip edgeSoft spots and the metal edge at the eaves.
  6. 6GutterWhere all that water has to go.

North Texas weather

Hail or high wind? Start on the ground.

DFW storms lift shingles and dent gutters without much showing from the street. Before anything else:

  1. 1

    Stay off the roof

    Wet or damaged shingles are slick. A fall is worse than any leak.

  2. 2

    Photograph from the ground

    Granules in the yard, dented gutters and vents, new stains on ceilings.

  3. 3

    Call for an inspection
