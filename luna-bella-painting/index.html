Gibbous · Before you pick a color

One color, four lights.

Paint never looks the same all day. Pick a time and watch the same three samples shift, then test yours the way you will live with them.

MorningCool, soft light. East-facing rooms look brightest now, and grays can lean blue.

MiddayStrong, neutral light. Colors look closest to the chip, and flat walls show every bump.

EveningLow, warm light. West-facing rooms glow orange, so warm whites can turn yellow.

Lamp lightBulbs decide. Warm bulbs pull colors toward yellow; cool bulbs flatten them. Look after dark too.

Roll out a big sample and look at it at all four times before you choose.

  • Warm white
  • Soft gray
  • Sage
