What we install.

Fixture work is its own trade. Each item below is a job we take on by itself or as part of a kitchen or bath update: remove the old unit, check the rough-in, set the new one, seal it, connect it and run water until it is right.

Drop-in · undermount · farmhouse

Kitchen faucets & sinks

Pull-down and bridge faucets, soap dispensers and sprayers, undermount sinks re-clipped to stone, basket strainers and new supply lines.

  • Pull-down & bridge faucets
  • Undermount re-clips
  • Strainers & supplies

Single-hole · centerset · widespread

Bathroom faucets & vanities

Single-hole, centerset and widespread faucets, pop-up drains, P-traps and vanity top sinks on new or existing cabinets.

Flange · wax or waxless

Toilets & bidet seats

Pulled, reset and sealed on a sound flange, new shutoff and supply, bidet seats with an outlet nearby, and comfort-height swaps.

Trim · mixing valve

Tubs, showers & valves

Tub spouts and diverters, shower heads and hand showers, trim kits, and pressure-balance valves behind tile or an access panel.

Garbage disposals

How a job runs

From your photo to running water.

Most fixture jobs can be sized up from a couple of photos and the model number on the box. Here is the order things happen in.

  1. 1

    Send a photo

    Text or email a picture of the old fixture, the space under it and the new unit if you already have one.

  2. 2

    Confirm the fit

    We check hole spacing, rough-in, valve type and shutoffs so nothing is a surprise on the day.

  3. 3

    Install & seal

    Old unit out, surfaces cleaned, new fixture set level, sealed and connected with fresh supplies.

  4. 4

    Test & tidy

    Water run hot and cold, every joint checked dry, the area wiped down and the old parts hauled out.
