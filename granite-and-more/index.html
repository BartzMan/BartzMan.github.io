Homeowner material guide

Three surfaces. Different care.

Start with how you cook and clean, then look at a full sample under your own light. Use a trivet for hot cookware with any of these surfaces.

Quartz

Engineered patterns are often consistent. Wipe spills promptly and follow the specific surface’s care instructions.

Granite

Patterns vary from slab to slab. Some granite benefits from a sealer to help resist staining.

Marble

Expect natural veining and more visible changes with use. Acids such as lemon juice can dull the finish, so wipe spills promptly.
