Before you decide

Repaint or replace
your cabinets?

Four quick checks. Look at your own kitchen, pick the answer that fits, and see which way it leans. It is general advice, not a verdict: a close look in person always settles it.

1 The boxes and doors

Solid wood or plywood takes paint well. Swollen, crumbling particle board usually doesn’t hold it.

2 The door style

Flat and shaker-style doors paint evenly. Peeling or plastic-wrapped doors are harder to get paint to stick to.

3 Hinges and drawers

If doors hang straight and drawers slide, paint and fresh hardware can freshen them. Sagging doors and broken runners need fixing either way.

4 The layout

If the layout suits how you cook and store things, painting is a simple refresh. If you keep wishing a cabinet were elsewhere, that points to replacing.
