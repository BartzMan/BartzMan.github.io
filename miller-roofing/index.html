Choose a roof part to see where water can get in and what a repair may address.

Parapet

The wall rises above the roof deck. Cracked joints or open masonry can let water in and may need masonry repair and a sealed junction.

Coping

The cap protects the top of the parapet. A loose joint or damaged cap may need resealing, repair or replacement.

Membrane

The continuous roof covering sheds water. A split or worn seam may need a compatible patch when the surrounding material is sound.

Drain

Runoff leaves at a drain or outlet. A blocked opening or a leak around the drain can leave water standing and needs a closer look.

Flashing

Flashing bridges changes in material or direction. A loose edge where the roof meets a wall may need a new sealed transition.
