Raw cedar

How it looks
Bright orange-tan with every knot and grain line showing. In Houston sun and humidity it weathers toward silver-gray within months, and shady boards can mildew.
Roughly how often
Nothing to redo, but nothing protects the wood either.

General guide only. Every product label has its own drying and recoat times, and new wood usually needs time to dry out before the first coat.

Painting a Houston house: when to paint, when to wait.

Humidity, rain and heat decide more than the brush does. Tap each one.

Paint

Drier days and afternoons. Many exterior paints want humidity under about 85%.

Wait

Muggy, foggy mornings. Paint dries slowly and can stay tacky or streak.

Paint

A dry forecast for the rest of the day, on siding and wood that have dried out.

Wait

Rain due within a few hours, or boards still damp after yesterday’s storm.

Paint

The shady side of the house, following the shade around as the day moves.

Wait

Walls baking in afternoon sun. Paint dries too fast and shows lap marks.

Paint

Mid-morning to mid-afternoon, so the coat sets before the evening dew.

Wait

Late-day starts on cool fall evenings. Dew can spot fresh paint.

The can always wins: every product lists its own temperature and humidity limits.
