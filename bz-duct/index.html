01 · Services

What we fabricate and install

Four lines of work, from sheet on the bench to the run in the ceiling.

  1. 01

    Custom HVAC ductwork fabrication

    Rectangular and round runs cut and folded to the measurements you send, then joined and sealed here in the shop.

  2. 02

    Sheet-metal fabrication

    Boxes, panels and transitions cut, folded and assembled from galvanized sheet, finished with flanges and joints ready to connect.

  3. 03

    Duct installation

    Runs connected and hung in place, trimmed to fit the space they are passing through and supported where they land.

  4. 04

    Fittings and transitions

    Offsets, elbows and transitions that join one section to the next and change the direction or the size of the run.

05 · Questions

Straight answers

01

How do I get an estimate?

Call the shop on (917) 815-5813 or send an email with the space, roughly how long the run is, and whether it is new work or a replacement. We read the measurements and the photos you send, then tell you what the work involves and what it costs.

02

What should I do if I smell gas or see smoke?

Leave the building, then call 911 and your gas utility. Do not wait for anyone from this website and do not use switches or flames inside.

03

Which areas do you work in?

Sunset Park and the rest of Brooklyn. Give us the building and we will tell you whether we can take the job on.

04

What should I send in a message?

The room or space, the rough length of the run, whether the duct is new or being replaced, and a photo of the existing ductwork or of the space. A photo of a tape measure held up to the opening tells us more than anything else.
