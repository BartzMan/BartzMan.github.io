Service request

Beforeyou call.

My AC is running but the air isn't cold. What now?

Check that the thermostat is set to Cool and the filter isn't clogged. If it still blows warm, call or text: it could be the outdoor unit, an electrical part or low refrigerant, and that needs a look on site.

When should I have the heat looked at?

Before the first cold night. Turn the heat on once early in the fall. If it won't come on, smells odd or keeps switching on and off, text a photo and a short note and book a visit while it's still mild.

How do I know if there's a refrigerant leak?

Common signs: the AC runs a long time without cooling, ice on the copper lines or the outdoor unit, or a hissing sound. Refrigerant doesn't get used up, so a low charge means a leak somewhere. It gets found and repaired before the system is recharged.

What should I text you first?

A photo of the label on the outdoor unit (the sticker with the model and serial number), a photo of the whole unit, and one line on what it's doing. Text it to (720) 710-7171.
