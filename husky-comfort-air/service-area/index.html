Town by town

Where the cable runs.

A short note on each place in the service area. The services are the same everywhere: all six are available across the list.

RICHMOND Home base

Richmond, TX

Richmond is where Husky Comfort Air is based, so it is the natural starting point for repair calls, new installs and maintenance visits. When you get in touch, mention what the system is doing and, if you can, send a photo of the unit and its data plate.

SUGAR LAND On the list

Sugar Land, TX

Sugar Land is on the service list. Homes here lean on central air through a long, humid summer, which is exactly when attic drain lines, weak capacitors and dirty coils tend to show their problems. Call or text with the symptoms and the city to get started.

KATY On the list

Katy, TX

Katy is on the list too. Whether it is a system that has started short cycling, a new install for an addition or a replacement for tired equipment, the process is the same: find out what the system needs, explain it, then do that job.

HOUSTON AREA On the list

Houston area, TX

The Houston area rounds out the list. Houses and flat commercial roofs both count, from an attic air handler that is dripping into a ceiling to a package unit on a roof that needs service or a crane lift.
