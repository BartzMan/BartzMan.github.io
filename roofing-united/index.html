How a roof goes on

Course by course.

A roof is built from the deck up, every layer lapping over the one below. This page is built the same way. Here is the order the crew works in.

  1. 01

    Course 01 · Tear-off

    Strip it to the deck.

    The old roof comes off and goes straight into the dumpster, so the decking can be seen before anything new goes on.

  2. 02

    Course 02 · Underlayment

    Dry it in.

    Synthetic underlayment rolls out across the deck, each run lapped over the one below, from the eaves up.

  3. 03

    Course 03 · Shingles

    Lay the courses.

    Shingles go on in straight, staggered rows, worked in around the chimney and every roof edge.

  4. 04

    Course 04 · Ridge & vents

    Let it breathe.

    Vents go in up top and the ridge gets capped, so hot attic air has somewhere to go.

Questions

Straight answers.

Q1Should my roof be repaired or replaced?

It depends on what is going on under the shingles. Text photos of the roof and the problem spot to (726) 777-4751 and we will tell you which way we would go, and why.

Q2What should I do after a storm?

Stay off the roof. From the ground, look for lifted or missing shingles, granules in the gutters and new stains on ceilings inside. Take photos and text them to (726) 777-4751.

Q3How long does a re-roof take?

Ask us. It depends on the size and pitch of the roof, what turns up at tear-off and the weather. We will give you a straight answer for your roof.

Q4What should I text you?

A few photos of the roof from the yard or driveway, a close-up of any damage, your neighborhood or ZIP code, and the best time to call.

Q5Where do you work?

San Antonio, TX and nearby. Not sure if you are in range? Text us your ZIP code.
