Zone 01 · drawing of a furnace

Zone 01 · Heating

Warm rooms through a Lake Erie winter.

From the first cold snap to the last frost, the furnace is the hardest-working thing in a Toledo basement. When it slows down, the whole house feels it.

  • Heating repair

    When the furnace won't start, blows cold air or keeps cycling on and off, a repair visit finds out why and fixes it.

  • Furnace installation & replacement

    When an old furnace is past fixing, a new one is sized for the house and put in where the old one stood.

  • Thermostats

    The small box on the wall that tells the furnace when to run. A new or rewired thermostat can be part of any heating visit.

Zone 02 · Air conditioning

Cool air when Ohio turns sticky.

Toledo summers come in humid off the lake. Central air pulls the heat and the damp out of the house, and the outdoor unit is where that heat goes.

  • AC repair

    Warm air from the vents, ice on the copper lines or an outdoor unit that hums and won't start: signs the AC needs a repair.

  • Central air installation

    A new outdoor unit and the indoor coil that goes with it, matched to each other and to the size of the house.

Zone 03 · drawing of a breaker panel

Zone 03 · Electrical

The wiring behind both.

A furnace and an air conditioner each run on their own circuit. The same hands that work on the equipment can work on the wiring in the rest of the house.

  • Electrical repairs

    Breakers that keep tripping, outlets that went dead and switches that stopped working: problems for an electrician, not a guess.

  • Outlets, switches & fixtures

    New outlets where the furniture actually goes, tired switches swapped out and light fixtures hung.

  • Heating & cooling circuits

    The circuits, disconnects and thermostat wiring that a furnace or AC needs, done as part of the same job.

Meter, wiring, outdoor unit. Three trades in one corner of the yard.

That is why one company doing all three makes sense: nothing gets lost between the electrician and the heating guy.
