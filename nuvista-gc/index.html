Planning a renovation in an older home? Leave room in the schedule for these four conversations.

Permit review

Ask the local building office what paperwork applies before you settle on a start date.

Hidden conditions

Keep some flexibility for repairs that only become apparent once walls or floors are opened.

Pre-1978 paint

Ask a qualified professional about lead paint testing before work that might disturb old paint.

Materials lead times

Check when specialty windows, cabinets and matching finishes can arrive before work depends on them.
