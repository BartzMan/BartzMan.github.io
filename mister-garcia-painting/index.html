Cabinets & built-ins

Colour that commits.

Deep navy and forest green look rich on cabinetry, and they show every brush mark, drip and missed edge. That is why cabinet work is careful work: doors and frames prepped, coats laid on evenly, shelves and inside edges finished the same as the fronts.

Forest Navy Gallery white Charcoal Oak

Colour families from their own photos, not paint products.

Or keep it bright.

Not every built-in wants a bold colour. A crisp white on shelves, cabinet doors and drawer fronts keeps a home office light, with the trim matched to the walls around it.

  • Kitchen cabinets, uppers and lowers
  • Built-in shelving, desks and window seats
  • Dining and living-room cabinet walls

Questions

Asked before the first coat.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Se habla español. Call or text (214) 837-6353 in English or Spanish.

Do you paint kitchen cabinets and built-ins?

Yes. Cabinet and built-in painting is one of the services: kitchen cabinets, built-in shelving, desks and dining-room cabinets like the green, navy and white ones on this page.

Do you take commercial painting jobs?

Yes. Commercial painting is one of the services, alongside residential painting. Describe the space, or send photos, and ask when you call.

Can the drywall be fixed before the paint goes on?

Yes. Drywall repair and tape and bed are both services, so damaged or new drywall can be made ready as part of the same job.

What should I send to get a price?

Call or text (214) 837-6353, or email the details with the form below. Photos of the rooms, cabinets or walls, rough sizes and the colours you have in mind all help.
