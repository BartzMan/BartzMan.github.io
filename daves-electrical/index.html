Sheet 02 · Repair or rebuild

Don't bin it. Rebuild it.

When an electrical unit quits, the easy answer is to throw it out and buy another. Rebuilding is the other answer: the unit comes apart, the parts that have worn are replaced, and the sound parts go back together with them.

It is the trade in the name on the plate. Electrical repair and rebuilding, from Wooster, Ohio.

Replace
The whole unit goes out, a new one comes in.
Rebuild
Only the worn parts go out. The rest is cleaned and kept.

Sheet 03 · On the bench

Three jobs. One bench.

Dave's Electrical Rebuilding works on electrical repair and rebuilding. Here is what each of those means, in plain words.

DER-R02

Electrical rebuilding

Taking a worn unit apart, replacing what has worn and putting it back together. Useful when the unit itself is worth keeping, or a replacement is hard to come by.

DER-R01

Electrical repair

Finding what stopped working and fixing it. Worth a call when something electrical has quit, keeps tripping a breaker or only works some of the time.

DER-R03

Troubleshooting

Tracing an electrical fault back to where it starts. For problems that come and go, or that have no obvious cause.
