USEFUL · FROM THE GROUND

After a Gulf storm: four checks, none of them on a ladder.

Walk the yard in this order. Every check below is reading the storm the way a crew does — from ground level.

Tab edges lift at hips and gables first. Stand across the lawn and compare the ridgeline against your memory of the good sky behind it — a lifted tab shows as a thin white crack.

Gutters, downspouts, and where the splash lands. Puddle rings on the walk or mud fanned off the splash stone both mean water came down hard — and tells you where the roof took it.

In good daylight, look at ceilings and top corners under every slope. A fresh stain starts pale and wide. Photograph any patch you find with something in frame for scale.

Scattered shingle flecks on the grass, dented the window seal on your hedge, struck leaf-loss high on the garden. The yard sees the storm the roof took.
