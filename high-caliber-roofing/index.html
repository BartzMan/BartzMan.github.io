6/12 How a roof goes on

Five marks on the tape. One roof at the end.

Every re-roof runs along the same tape, and you hear what we see at each mark. On Google, reviewers bring up a thorough job and clear explanations.

  1. Inspect

    A walk of the roof: shingles, flashing, vents, valleys and any decking you can see, explained in plain words.

  2. Tear off

    The old roofing comes off down to the deck, with scrap picked up as the work goes.

  3. Deck & underlayment

    Soft or damaged decking gets pointed out before anything covers it. New underlayment goes down over the deck.

  4. Shingles

    Chalk lines snapped, then shingles laid course by course up to the ridge, with vents and edges finished.

  5. Clean-up

    Debris off the roof and out of the yard, then a last walk around the house.

9/12 Leaks

Stain on the ceiling? Start here.

  1. Photograph it

    Take a photo of the stain and, if it is safe from the ground, of the roof above it.

  2. Bucket and towel

    Put a bucket and a towel under any drip, and move what you can out of the way.

  3. Keep clear of a sagging ceiling

    If the drywall is wet and bowing, keep people out from under it.

  4. Call or text

    Send the photos to (832) 433-5780 and say where the stain is.

7/12 FAQ

Questions from the ground.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in a text with a photo.

Text (832) 433-5780
01What are the signs a roof needs replacing?

Shingles that are curling, cracked or missing, granules collecting in the gutters, soft spots in the deck, and leaks that keep coming back in new places. A look at the roof itself tells you more than its age does.

02There's a leak after a storm. What should I do?

Photograph the stain, and the roof from the ground if it is safe to. Put a bucket and a towel under any drip, and keep clear of a ceiling that is wet and sagging. Then call or text (832) 433-5780 with the photos and say where the water shows up.

03Do you work on siding as well as roofs?

Yes. Google lists High Caliber Roofing as a siding contractor as well as a roofing contractor. Mention the siding when you call or text about the roof, or ask about it on its own.

04Can you help with the paperwork for an insurance claim?

We look at the roof and explain what we see; whether a claim is approved is the insurer's decision.

05What should I send in a text?

Photos of the roof from the ground, photos of any ceiling stains inside, your town, and a line about what you have noticed. Text them to (832) 433-5780.
