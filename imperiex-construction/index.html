Building owner checklist

Where leaks can start.

In a multi-storey building, the damp patch you see may be away from where water got in. Record where and when it appears.

Parapets

Note stains near the top of an outside wall, especially after wind-driven rain.

Terrace drains

Look for areas that stay wet around a terrace drain or threshold.

Window perimeters

Check which side of the opening shows dampness or discoloration.

Roof penetrations

Tell a professional if an indoor stain is near a vent, pipe or other roof opening.
