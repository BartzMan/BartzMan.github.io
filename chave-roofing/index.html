What we do

Four jobs, read like a gauge.

Level 1

Roof replacement

Replacement strips the old shingles back to the deck and puts a new roof on top. It suits roofs that are worn out across the board.

Level 2

Roof repair

Repair fixes the spot that is letting water in, such as a flashing, a vent or a handful of shingles, while the rest of the roof keeps working.

Level 3

Shingle roofs

Asphalt shingles are the common roof on Houston homes. They are laid in overlapping courses so water runs over each one and off the next.

Level 4

Storm damage repairs

After a hard storm, shingles can lift, crack or blow off. Storm repairs close those openings before the next rain finds them.

    5 IN

    Repair or replace

    Patch the leak, or start over.

    When one spot is the problem.

    A repair makes sense when the trouble is local: a flashing that has lifted, a vent boot that has cracked, or a few shingles damaged in a storm, with the rest of the roof still sound.

    • Flashing and vent boots
    • Missing, lifted or cracked shingles
    • Storm damage repairs

    When the whole roof is tired.

    A replacement makes sense when wear is everywhere: shingles curling or brittle across whole slopes, bare patches, or repairs that keep coming back. A new roof starts from the deck again.

    • Old shingles off, deck checked
    • New underlayment and flashing
    • New shingles, ridge and vents
