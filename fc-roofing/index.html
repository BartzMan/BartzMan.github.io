What it is · when you need it

Shingle roofs

A shingle roof is the overlapping cover that sheds rain off your house. You need a new one when shingles are curling, cracked or missing, or the roof has simply worn out.

Re-roofs

A re-roof strips the old roofing back to the deck and starts again with new materials. It is the answer when patching no longer keeps up with the leaks.

Underlayment

Underlayment is the water-resistant sheet rolled over the bare deck, under the shingles. It is a second line of defence when wind-driven rain gets past a shingle.

Sweep 02 · Before the season

Five checks
from the ground

Hurricane season runs June to November on the Gulf Coast. Before it starts, take ten minutes with binoculars and walk the yard. Mark each check as you go. You never need to climb up to look, and if anything worries you, a roofer can take a proper look.

1

Shingles

Scan every slope for missing, curled or cracked shingles and bare patches. Binoculars make it easy.

2

Metal and vents

Look at the metal around chimneys, vents and walls. Gaps, lifting edges or rust are worth noting.

3

Gutters

Gutters should be clear, firmly attached and draining away from the walls. Grit from shingles piling up in them is a sign of an ageing roof.

4

Trees

Branches hanging over the roof scrape shingles and drop limbs in high wind. Note any that reach the roof.

5

Ceilings inside

Look at upstairs ceilings and closets for brown stains or damp patches, especially a day after heavy rain.

Answer all five and the scope will tell you what to do next.
