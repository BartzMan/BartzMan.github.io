Read the boards

Walk around the house and look at the siding from the ground. Old paint tells you what it needs. Tap a board to read it.

The board says

Peeling

Paint lifting in flakes, usually from moisture getting in behind it.

What to do: find and fix the moisture first, scrape the loose paint, prime bare spots, then repaint.

General advice. Look from the ground or a window; leave ladders to the painter.
