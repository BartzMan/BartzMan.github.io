DFW heatwave checklist

Before you call.

Mode

Check that the thermostat is set to cooling and that its setpoint is below the room reading.

Filter

If your filter is accessible and due for a change, use the type and size listed for your system.

Airflow

Check that furniture, curtains and rugs leave indoor supply vents and returns clear.

Sun

Close blinds where direct afternoon sun heats the room and keep exterior doors closed.

What changed?

Note which rooms are warm and when the change started. If it persists, share those details when you call.
