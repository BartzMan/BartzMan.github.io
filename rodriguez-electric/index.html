The five on the van

Five jobs, one electrician.

New construction

What it isWiring a new home from bare framing: boxes, circuits, panel and lighting laid out before the walls close.

When you need itYou are building a house, and the electrical has to be right the first time because it disappears behind drywall.

Additions and remodels

What it isNew circuits, outlets and lighting for a room you are adding or a space you are reworking.

When you need itA bigger kitchen, a finished garage or a new bedroom means more load than the old wiring was planned for.

Service and panel upgrades

What it isReplacing or enlarging the meter, main panel and service so the whole house is fed safely.

When you need itBreakers trip often, the panel is full, or you are adding big loads such as a spa or an addition.

Indoor and outdoor lighting

What it isFixtures, switches and wiring for rooms, garages, porches, paths and gardens.

When you need itA space is too dim, an old fixture has failed, or you want the yard to be usable after dark.

Hot tubs and spas

What it isThe dedicated circuit, outdoor disconnect and wiring a spa needs to run safely.

When you need itYou are buying a hot tub, or moving one, and the yard needs power run to the right spot.
