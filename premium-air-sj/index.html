A year of
care.

Choose a season for one thing to look at before the next change in weather.

Cooling check

Before warmer weather, note whether cooling reaches the rooms you use most and whether the outdoor unit is clear of loose debris.

Filter review

Check the filter label and look for visible buildup. Note any change in airflow on days when the system runs longer.

Heating check

Try the heat as the weather cools. Note any rooms that stay cool or changes in the sound as the system starts.

Watch for changes

Note repeated short cycles, uneven temperatures or new sounds. A record of when they happen helps you describe the problem.

Cooling

Cooling service can help identify why an air conditioner is running without bringing rooms down to the setting you expect.

Heating

Heating service addresses problems such as weak output, uneven warmth or a system that does not start when the thermostat calls for heat.

Know your
filter.

Filter schedules vary by model and household. Choose a factor and check the instructions for your system.

What changes the schedule?

Start with the label or equipment manual. Thin filters and thicker media can have different replacement intervals.

Check the filter more often if there is shedding or visible buildup. Note how quickly it collects dust during regular use.

Check the filter regularly and ask what filtration your equipment supports. A filter that restricts airflow can affect how the system runs.
