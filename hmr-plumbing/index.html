Water heater:3 signs to look at it.

Tap each sign to read it. This is general home advice, so if you see water pooling around the tank, turn off the water and call a plumber.

Call (330) 763-3760
1The hot water runs out fast

If showers turn lukewarm sooner than they used to, or the water never gets as hot as it did, the tank may be struggling to keep up.

2The tank rumbles or pops

Banging, rumbling or popping from the tank is often a sign of mineral build-up inside it. It is worth having someone take a look.

3Damp, rust or drips at the base

Look around the bottom of the tank and the pipe connections. A wet floor, rust streaks or slow drips mean it is time to call, and to shut off the water to the tank while you wait.
