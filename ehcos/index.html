Questions from a Katy house.

The AC is running but the house is not cooling.

Say whether every room is warm or only some, and whether the outdoor unit is running. A photo of the outdoor unit and of the model plate helps.

How do I book a visit?

Call or text (832) 902-5492, or use the message form. We read what you send and write back about a time.

What should I send?

Photos of the unit and of the model plate, your town, and what the house is doing: cool air, warm air, no air, or a noise.

I smell gas.

Leave the house. Call 911 or the gas company. Do not look for the leak yourself. A text to EHCOS is not the call to make for a gas smell.
