LABEL 1 · THE ROOF IN THE SACK

Four layers between you and the sky.

A roof is more than shingles. Tap a layer to see a tear-off photo and what that layer does in Houston heat and storms.

1 · Deck

The plywood deck is what everything else nails into. Houston humidity can leave it soft, so spongy or stained patches are worth replacing before any new roofing goes on.

LABEL 2 · WHAT WE DO

Shingle or tile.

LOT 01

Roof replacement

The old roof comes off down to the deck, the deck is checked, and a new roof goes on. It makes sense when repairs keep coming back or the shingles are worn through.

LOT 02

Roof repair

A fix for a leak, lifted shingles or damaged flashing, without replacing the whole roof.

LOT 03

Storm damage repairs

Wind and falling limbs can lift or crack roofing. Storm repairs close up the damage so the next rain stays outside.

LOT 04

Tile roof repairs

Clay and concrete tile roofs are common on Gulf-style homes. Cracked or slipped tiles get replaced and the underlayment beneath is checked.

GOOD TO KNOW

Why tile starts underneath

Tile sheds the first of the water, but the underlayment below it does the sealing. That is why a tile repair looks at both.
