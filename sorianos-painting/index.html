How a job goes

First call to last coat.

No surprises: you know the plan, the colours and the rooms before anyone opens a can.

  1. 01

    Call, text or email

    Say what needs painting or fixing. Photos of the rooms, walls, cabinets or doors help.

  2. 02

    Colours & scope

    Talk through the rooms, the colours and any drywall or tile work, before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Prep, then paint

    Floors and furniture covered, walls patched and taped, then the coats go on.

  4. 04

    Clean up & walk through

    Tape off, drop cloths up, the room back in order. Reviewers mention cleanliness for a reason.

Questions

Before the first brushstroke.

Do you paint both inside and outside?

Yes. Interior and exterior painting, plus cabinets, doors and trim.

Can drywall damage be fixed before painting?

Yes. Holes, dents and cracks are patched and smoothed so the new coat goes on a clean wall.

Do you do tile and remodeling too?

Yes. Tile work and remodeling come up often in Google reviews, alongside the painting.

What should I send to get started?

Photos of the rooms, walls, cabinets or doors, the colours you are thinking about, and where you are in Katy or west Houston. Call or text (281) 682-3412, or use the email form below.
