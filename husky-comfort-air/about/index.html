Who we are

Repair, service and installation.

Husky Comfort Air describes its own work in four words: HVAC repair, service and installation. In practice that means air conditioning and heating for residential homes, plus rooftop and package units on commercial roofs, across Richmond, Sugar Land, Katy and the Houston area.

The work covers both halves of a system. Outside, that is condensers on their pads and units up on the roof. Inside, it is air handlers, coils, drain pans and ductwork in the attic. The approach is the same in every spot: work out what the system actually needs, explain it in plain words, then do that job properly.
