How a stucco wall is built

Lath. Scratch. Brown. Finish.

Traditional stucco goes on in layers, each one left to set before the next. What you see from the street is only the last one. This is what sits under it.

  1. 01

    Lath

    Wire lath over building paper, fastened to the framing, gives the plaster something to grab.

  2. 02

    Scratch coat

    The first coat of cement plaster, pushed into the lath and scored with lines so the next coat bonds.

  3. 03

    Brown coat

    Builds the wall out flat and true. It is left to cure before anything goes over it.

  4. 04

    Finish coat

    The colour and texture you live with: a sand finish, a smooth look or a synthetic finish.

What is a sand finish?

A fine, even texture on the last coat of stucco, floated with sand in the mix. It reads smooth from the street and shows a soft grain up close.

Can you fix a crack or a hole in my stucco?

Stucco repairs are on JL Stucco’s list. Send a close photo of the damage and a wider photo of the wall, so the texture around it can be seen.
