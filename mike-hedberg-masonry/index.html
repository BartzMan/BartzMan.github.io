Small jobs

No job
too small.

A cracked brick, a capstone that has worked loose, a tree ring that has lifted at one edge and is catching the mower. Small repairs are the work, not an exception to it.

Send a photograph and a sentence about what is wrong. Most of the time that is enough to tell you whether it is a repair or a rebuild.

Text (817) 271-4716
Q1Can you fix one brick, or is it a whole wall?

One brick is usually one brick, along with the joint it sits in and whatever caused it. A crack that runs through a whole panel, or a wall that has started to lean, is a different job, and the photograph usually settles which one it is before anybody drives out.

Q2Do you build new mailboxes, or only repair them?

Both. A new mailbox is a pier, a cap and a number plate set into the face, going in on a footing that suits the soil it is standing in. A repair starts by working out why it moved in the first place, because a pier that has heaved will do it again.

Q3What should I send with the photograph?

The whole thing, standing back far enough to show what it is part of, and a sentence on what changed: when it started, whether it moves, and whether water gets to it. On a brick job the weather usually tells you more than the crack does.
