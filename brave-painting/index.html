Tower 2 · Before you paint

Read the flags before an outdoor paint day.

Lifeguards fly flags for the water. These are for the weather on painting day: general advice for any home. Tap a flag to raise it.

Green flag

Dry, mild, a light breeze

A good day for outside paint. Surfaces are dry, and the coat has time to dry before evening.

Yellow flag

Morning dew

Wait until walls and decks are dry to the touch, and stop early enough that the paint dries before the evening dew.

Red flag

Rain on the way

Fresh exterior paint needs dry hours to set. Check the can's label for how long, and move the day if rain is close.

Purple flag

Hot sun on the wall

Paint on a wall baking in direct sun dries too fast and can show lap marks. Follow the shade around the house.
