The job

What AC repair actually covers.

A central air conditioner is two halves working together: the outdoor condenser with its compressor and fan, and the indoor coil and blower, usually in the attic or a closet. A repair visit looks at both, along with the thermostat, the wiring between them and the refrigerant lines that connect them.

Most breakdowns come down to a short list of parts: a capacitor that has weakened, a contactor with burned points, a fan motor that has seized, a refrigerant leak or a coil choked with dirt. Each one shows up a little differently, which is why measuring comes before replacing anything.

Check list

Signs it needs a look

  • Vents blow warmAir at room temperature or warmer
  • Outdoor fan stoppedUnit runs but the blade sits still
  • Hum, click, nothingStarts to turn on, then gives up
  • Short cyclingOn and off every few minutes
  • Ice on the linesFrost on the copper or the coil
  • Breaker tripsPower cuts out as the AC starts

The visit

How a repair visit goes.

  1. Step 01

    Tell us the symptoms

    Call or text what the system is doing and when it started. A photo of the thermostat and the outdoor unit's data plate helps the visit start with the right idea.

  2. Step 02

    Test, don't guess

    Electrical readings on the capacitor and motors, refrigerant pressures on the lines, and temperatures at the supply and return show where the problem actually sits.

  3. Step 03

    Plain-words options

    You hear what failed, why it likely failed and what the choices are, so you decide what gets fixed before any work starts.

  4. Step 04

    Repair and recheck

    The failed part is repaired or replaced, then the system runs while it is checked again to confirm it is cooling the way it should.

What happens next

After the repair, no loose ends.

Once the system is running again, you'll know what was replaced and whether anything else looked worn. If a part is close to the end of its life, you'll hear that too, so it can be planned for instead of failing on the next hot afternoon.

When a repair no longer makes sense for an older system, the conversation turns to replacement, with the reasons laid out side by side so the choice is yours.
