Outside, stucco to beams.

Exterior and stucco painting, from the walls and trim of a two-story house to the beams of a courtyard pergola.

Why outside paint peels

Hard Texas sun, heat and humid air work on exterior paint every day. Peeling usually starts where moisture gets in behind the paint, so scraping, patching and priming the bare spots matter as much as the color on top.
