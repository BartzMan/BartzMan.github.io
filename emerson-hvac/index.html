32° The Geauga year

Four readings
a year.

Up here the weather sets the schedule. This is roughly how a year with your heating and cooling goes, and when to call.

Oct – Nov

Before the first band

Have the furnace looked at before lake-effect snow starts piling up and everyone needs heat at once.

Dec – Mar

The long stretch

Keep outdoor vent pipes clear of drifts, change filters, and call at the first strange noise or cold room.

Apr – May

The switch-over

Heat in the morning, cooling by afternoon. A good time to test the air conditioner before you need it.

Jun – Sep

Lake-humid summer

Cooling and dehumidifying in muggy weeks. If it runs and runs without catching up, let us look.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call or text (440) 742-2889, or use the request form on this page. Tell us what the system is doing, roughly how old it is and where the building is.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the building straight away, do not switch anything on or off, and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Call us once everyone is safe.

The furnace vent pipes outside are buried in snow. What now?

Many high-efficiency furnaces breathe through plastic pipes on an outside wall. Gently clear snow and ice away from them. If the furnace has shut off and will not restart, call us.

Which area do you cover?

Chardon and Geauga County in north-east Ohio. If you are just outside it, call and ask.

Do you work on air conditioning too?

Yes. Heating, air conditioning and the ductwork that connects them.
