BLOCK A, LOT 2 · New build

The first roof a home gets

Once the framing is up and the deck is on, underlayment, flashing and shingles go over it so the house is closed in. Ridge, valleys and edges are checked before the builder's walk-through.

BLOCK B, LOT 6 · Reroof

Old shingles off, new roof on

When a roof is worn through, curling or leaking, the old layer is taken off, the deck underneath is looked over, and new underlayment, flashing and shingles go on.

BLOCK D, LOT 1 · Repair

Fix the spot, not the whole roof

A few lifted, cracked or missing shingles, or a worn seal around a vent or wall, can often be put right without replacing everything around them.

BLOCK C, LOT 1 · Storm damage

Find what the storm moved

Wind and hail can lift shingles, dent vents and loosen flashing. The work starts with a careful look at what changed, then repairs or a replacement as needed.
