01The membrane

One white sheet. Every seam welded.

TPO is a single-ply roofing membrane. Wide white sheets roll out across a flat or low-slope deck, overlap at the edges, and are joined with hot air, so each seam fuses into one continuous surface instead of a glued joint.

In Austin the color matters as much as the seams: a white roof faces the Texas sun instead of soaking it up. That is the whole idea behind Castillo Roofing TPO.
