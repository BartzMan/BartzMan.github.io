PLATE 8Storm log

After hail in Austin: 5 things to do in the first week

Open each note as you work through the list. None of it means climbing on the roof.

  1. Take photos of dents on gutters, vents and cars. Your phone keeps the date for you.

  2. Look for loose shingle granules, like coarse dark sand, where downspouts empty onto the ground.

  3. Check ceilings and the edges of the attic for new stains or damp spots.

  4. Note the day and time the hail fell, and how big it looked next to a coin or a ball.

  5. Have a roofer look at it before the next rain, so small problems stay small.
