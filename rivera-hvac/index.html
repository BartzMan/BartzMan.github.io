Which areas do you work in?

Spring and north Houston, Texas. Tell us your neighborhood or ZIP when you call, text or email.

What should I have ready when I call?

What the system is doing (no cooling, no heat, a noise, water where it shouldn't be), roughly how old it is, and if you can, a photo of the label on the outdoor unit. You can text the photo to (832) 452-5772.

Can you replace an old system?

Yes. Sales and system replacement is one of the services. Call and we can talk through the options for your home.

Yes. Refrigeration is part of the business: the full name is Rivera Heating, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration.
