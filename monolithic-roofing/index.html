THREE JOBS,ONE SURFACE.

Seam reinforcement

Seams and joints are where flat roofs tend to open first. Reinforcing fabric is bedded over them and brushed in, so the weakest line on the roof becomes part of one strong layer.

When you need it: open laps, splitting seams, or leaks that keep returning to the same line.

White reflective coatings

A white liquid coating goes over the whole roof surface. It reflects sunlight instead of soaking it up, and it cures with no joins of its own.

When you need it: the roof is sound but dark, hot and ageing on top.

Commercial flat-roof restoration

For commercial roofs that are tired but still sound: clean, repair, reinforce and coat, rather than tearing everything off and starting again.

When you need it: warehouses, shops and offices with a big flat roof that is ageing but not finished.

FOUR PASSES.ONE ROOF.

Pass 1 of 4

Clean

Dirt, debris and loose material come off first, so repairs and coatings have a clean surface to bond to.

Pass 2 of 4

Repair

Blisters, splits and open laps are dealt with before anything goes on top, so the roof is flat and sound under the coating.

Pass 3 of 4

Reinforce the seams

Fabric goes over every seam and joint and is brushed into the base layer, so the weakest lines end up the strongest ones.

Pass 4 of 4

Coat

The white coating is sprayed or rolled over the entire surface and runs up and around details, so it dries as one continuous layer.
