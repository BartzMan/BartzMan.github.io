What are the signs a house needs a 100 to 200 amp upgrade?

Breakers trip when the kitchen and the air conditioner run together, lights dip when something big starts, there is no open space left in the panel for a new circuit, or a remodel or addition is about to add load. Send a photo of the panel and the meter and ask whether an upgrade makes sense for the house.

A breaker keeps tripping. What should I do?

A breaker that keeps tripping is doing its job: something on that circuit is drawing too much or there is a fault. Do not keep resetting it and never tape it on. Note what was running when it tripped, then call or text (720) 462-1010 so the circuit can be checked.

Can lighting be added during a kitchen remodel?

Yes. Lighting installation and kitchen and remodel electrical are both on the list. The easiest time is while walls and ceilings are open, before drywall and paint, so boxes and runs go exactly where the plan needs them.

What should I send first?

A clear photo of the open panel showing the breakers and the label inside the door, a photo of the meter outside, and one line about what you want to add or fix. Text it to (720) 462-1010 or attach it to an email to nikelectric11@gmail.com.
