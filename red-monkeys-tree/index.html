The North Texas tree

Live oaks

Live oaks are a defining part of North Texas yards. They spread wide, grow long, heavy limbs and often reach out over roofs, driveways and fences. Good trimming keeps them healthy, tidy and looking their best around homes across Grand Prairie and DFW.

  • Limbs cleared back from roofs and drives
  • Crossing and crowded branches thinned
  • Dead wood taken out of the canopy

Live oak trimming is one of the topics people mention in our Google reviews.

Our services

  1. Tree trimming

    Branches cut back from roofs, drives and fences, and crowns tidied up for shade and light.

  2. Live oak trimming

    Careful trimming for the wide, heavy limbs of North Texas live oaks.

  3. Tree removal

    Taking down trees that need to come out, near homes, fences and driveways.

  4. Stump grinding

    Stumps ground down after a removal, so the spot can go back to lawn or beds.

  5. Brush chipping

    Branches and brush from the job fed through the chipper.
