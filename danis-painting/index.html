Siding

Start with a clean, dry surface. Deal with loose paint and damaged areas, then choose an exterior coating made for the siding material.

Trim

Look for open joints and failed paint at the edges. Prepare bare spots and choose a trim coating suited to the material.

Shutters

Check how the old coating is holding. Match the primer and finish to the shutter material and the exposure.

Front door

Clean the surface and check edges and hardware. Choose a finish that suits the door material and its exposure.

Interior room

Fill small surface defects and prepare the walls. Compare how matte and higher-sheen finishes look in the room’s light.

Long Island homeowner guide

Wait for dry weather.

Choose a mild stretch

Start with mild stretches in late spring or early fall. May, June and September are reasonable months to look at first on Long Island.

Check nights and mornings

Check overnight lows as well as afternoon highs. Let morning dew dry before painting, and follow the temperature range on the paint label.

Allow time to dry

Choose a dry surface and a clear forecast. Leave the time the label calls for before rain or heavy dew, and avoid hot surfaces in direct sun.
