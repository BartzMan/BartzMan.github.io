Desert tree notes

Built for 110° summers and monsoon nights.

A tree in Mesa has to handle heat, wind and the occasional dust storm. Here's what we tell homeowners before storm season.

Read our tree guides

Trim before the storms

Dense, top-heavy canopies catch the wind. Lightening them before monsoon season (June 15 to September 30) makes limbs less likely to fail.

Palms: dead, not green

Dead, brown and hazardous fronds are the usual targets. Healthy green fronds feed the palm, so be careful how much comes off.

Water deep, not daily

Frequent shallow watering keeps desert tree roots near the surface, which can make them less stable in a storm.

Check before you remove

Some removals in Mesa may need a permit, and digging needs an Arizona 811 call first. Ask before anything comes out.
