Privacy policy

SK2 Home Inspections And Electric · Lebanon, Ohio · Updated October 2026

This policy explains what happens to the details you share with SK2 Home Inspections And Electric ("we", "us") through this website, by phone, by text or by email.

01What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you. If you call, text or email us, we see your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to tell us about the job or the property.

02How we use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange electrical work or a home inspection.
  • To keep a record of the work or inspection we did for you.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for advertising.

03Who sees it

Only us, and the services we use to run the business (for example email, phone and website hosting providers), which handle it on our behalf. We share details with others only when the law requires it or when you ask us to, for example sending an inspection report to someone you name.

04How long we keep it

We keep requests and job records only as long as we need them for the reasons above, then delete them.

05Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

06Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Call (513) 314-9423 or email stevekramer67@gmail.com.
