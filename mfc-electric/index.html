01Services

What MFC Electric takes on.

Residential and light commercial electrical work across the west side, from a single dead outlet to a full panel change in an older Sunset house.

01

Panels, subpanels and service upgrades

Old fuse boxes and undersized panels in Sunset and Parkside homes get swapped for modern breaker panels sized for today's kitchens, heat pumps and car chargers.

  • Main panel replacement
  • Subpanels for garages and units
  • Breaker and bus faults
  • Load calculations for new appliances
Often the first step before a heat pump, induction range or EV charger
02

Rewiring and circuit additions

Knob-and-tube and brittle cloth wiring in pre-war flats, aluminum branch circuits, and new dedicated runs for laundry, ovens and workshops.

  • Whole-house and partial rewires
  • Dedicated 20A and 240V circuits
  • Added rooms brought up to code
  • Garage and workshop power
Knob-and-tube still turns up in west side attics and walls
03

Lighting, outlets and switches

Recessed and under-cabinet lighting, dimmers, GFCI and AFCI protection in kitchens and baths, USB outlets, and exterior and garage lighting.

  • Recessed and track lighting
  • GFCI, AFCI and tamper-resistant outlets
  • Smart switches and dimmers
  • Porch, garage and yard lights
Kitchens, baths, garages and the outside of the house
04

Troubleshooting and repairs

Breakers that trip, outlets that went dead, lights that dip when the heater kicks on. We trace the fault back along the circuit, explain it in plain words and fix it.

  • Tripping and nuisance breakers
  • Dead or warm outlets
  • Flicker, buzz and burning smells
  • Loose connections and failed devices
You hear what failed and why before any work starts
05

EV chargers, solar tie-ins and inverters

Level 2 charger circuits in garages and driveways, inverter and battery hookups, and the panel work that makes them possible in older houses.

  • Level 2 EV charger circuits
  • Inverter and battery connections
  • Generator inlets and transfer switches
  • Panel capacity checks first
Garage and driveway charging for west side homes
