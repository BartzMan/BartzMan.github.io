Before you pick a colour

Four looks before you decide.

Tap each card. General advice for any home, whoever does the roof.

Hold the chip up to your brick and trim

Stand back at the kerb and look at the wall, trim and door together. The roof is the largest colour on the front of the house.

Ask your HOA before you order

Many neighbourhoods set the shingle colours you may use. A quick email to the association can save choosing a colour you then cannot have.

Lighter colours reflect more sun

Dark roofs soak up more heat in a Texas summer, lighter ones reflect more. Neither is wrong; just choose it knowing your attic and your cooling bill.

See a sample outdoors, morning and evening

A chip looks different in a showroom than on your roof. Lean a full-size sample against the house and check it at a few times of day.
