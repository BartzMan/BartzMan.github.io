Field
notes.

N-01What does a cracked heat exchanger mean?

The heat exchanger keeps the flame and its exhaust apart from the air that goes round your house. Once it cracks, that separation is gone, so the furnace should be checked before it is run again. Fig. 1 above is one we found during a replacement.

N-02What is an ERV?

An energy recovery ventilator. It brings fresh outdoor air in and sends stale indoor air out, passing heat and moisture between the two streams on the way, so the house gets fresh air without throwing away all the heat.

N-03Can you make a fitting that is not standard?

Yes. Custom sheet metal is part of the job here: transitions, offsets, takeoffs and plenums made to fit the run in your house.

N-04Do you work on boilers and hot water tanks?

Yes. Along with furnaces and central air, we work on boilers and hot water tanks.

N-05What should I send with a quote request?

What the equipment is, what it is doing (or not doing), and your town. If you can, text a photo of the unit and its label to (440) 488-6748.
