Summer questions

Asked on a hot afternoon.

01The AC is running but blowing warm air. What do I do?

Check that the thermostat is set to cool and the filter is not clogged, then check the breaker. If it still blows warm, turn the system off so the compressor is not straining, and call or text (623) 313-5657 with what you are seeing.

02The rooftop unit is louder than it used to be.

A new rattle, squeal or hum from a rooftop package unit usually means something has changed: a loose panel, a worn blower motor or a failing part. It is worth having it looked at before a small part takes a bigger one with it.

03How do I book a visit?

Call or text (623) 313-5657, or use the email form below. Say where you are in Phoenix or the West Valley and what the system is doing.

04What should I send with my message?

A photo of the unit and its label, where it sits (roof, side yard, closet or wall) and what it is doing: blowing warm, making noise, not turning on, or cycling on and off.
