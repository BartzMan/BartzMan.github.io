03 · Drawing first

The plan,before the hole.

A reflected ceiling plan is drawn before anything is cut. It puts every can, fan and fixture on the same ceiling at the same time, so the spacing can be seen instead of guessed at room by room.

Can positions are laid out from the walls and the cabinets in the room. Fan and fixture positions are set where nobody walks under them. The holes get cut to the plan, not to whichever joist happens to be nearest.

  • RL-1–6Six cans, spaced even across the open floor.
  • CF-7Fan on the centre line, clear of the walk path.
  • FX-8Fixture where the room needs light in one place.
  • SOne switch leg, drawn dashed, tying the room together.

05 · Lanai season

Outside lightfor Tampa lanais.

A lanai ceiling is outdoors but wired like a room, and it is usually the ceiling most people forget. Fans and downlights go up over stained tongue-and-groove boards, onto framing that has to carry the weight, in air that is damp most of the year.

Outdoor and lanai fans and lighting is one of the five things ASJ Electric does in Tampa. Send a photo of the ceiling and the framing if it is visible, and the plan for the fan and the lights can be talked through before anything is cut.

  • Wet-location fans and housings for a ceiling in damp air
  • Brackets landed on framing, never on the boards
  • Fans and downlights planned together, not added later

Q-01How many recessed lights does a room need?

It depends on the room, the height of the ceiling and how much daylight the space already gets. The useful answer comes from laying the cans out on the ceiling first and spacing them even, instead of counting them off the size of the floor. A photo of the room from a corner, with the ceiling in frame, is enough to talk the layout through.

Q-02Can a ceiling fan be replaced with a fan-light?

Usually. A fan-light hangs from the same box as the fan it replaces, so where the existing box carries the weight of the old fan the swap is straightforward. If there is no box, or the old fan was hung from a brace or a joist instead, the new fixture needs support of its own. A photo of the existing fan and of the ceiling above it helps.

Q-03Which fans go on a lanai?

A fan over a lanai or a covered patio sits in damp air, so it takes a wet-location fan and a box that holds the weight. Most lanai ceilings are tongue-and-groove boards over framing, so the bracket has to land on something solid rather than on the boards. Outdoor fans and the downlights over the same ceiling are planned together instead of being added later.

Q-04Why do the lights flicker?

The usual causes are a loose connection at a fixture or inside a box, a dimmer that does not suit the lamps fitted to it, and a can that is not seated properly in the ceiling. Sometimes it is simply a lamp near the end of its life. Where lights flicker in more than one room, the panel is worth looking at before the fixtures are.

Q-05What should I send with my message?

A photo of the ceiling you are asking about, a photo of the fixture or fan that is going up, and the part of town or the ZIP. For anything at the panel, a photo of it with the cover off. That is usually enough to say what the job involves before anyone climbs a ladder.
