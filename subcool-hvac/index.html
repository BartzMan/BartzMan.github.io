What does “subcool” actually mean?

It's a technician's word. Inside an air conditioner, refrigerant runs around a closed copper loop: hot gas on one side, cold liquid on the other.

Superheat

How many degrees the vapor leaving the indoor coil sits above the temperature at which it boils.

Subcool

How many degrees the liquid leaving the outdoor coil has cooled below the temperature at which it condenses.

Read together on gauges and thermometers, the two numbers help show whether a system holds the right amount of refrigerant and is working the way it should. It's the kind of detail the company is named after.

Questions

Straight answers, no jargon.

Why is my AC running but blowing warm air?

Common reasons include a clogged filter, a frozen indoor coil, an outdoor unit that isn't running, an electrical part that has failed, or a refrigerant problem. Check the filter and the thermostat setting first. If it still blows warm, call or text (832) 943-9884.

Why is there ice on my AC lines?

Ice on the copper line or the indoor coil usually points to an airflow problem or a refrigerant charge problem. Switch the system off, or to fan only, so it can thaw, and have it checked before running it hard again.

Do you work on heating as well as AC?
