The work, course by course

A roof goes up the way this page reads: one course over another. Tap a course to open it.

We come up, we measure, we photograph. You get the condition of the deck, the flashings and the gutters in plain words — plus the sample board for your table.

Charcoal, slate, weathered wood; cream, cape gray, bay blue. We hold the chips against your brick and your neighbour's roofline until the choice is easy.

Tidy tear-off, deck boards replaced where they've had it, underlayment rolled out. The yard gets tarped; the neighbours get nothing to complain about.

Shingles lapped tight, ridges capped, flashings folded, gutters running true. We walk the job with you before the ladder comes down.
