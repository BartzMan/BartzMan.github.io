Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how A & J Roofing and Siding ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit this website, request a quote, or contact us by phone, text or email.

1. Who we are

A & J Roofing and Siding, Canfield, Ohio 44406. Phone: (234) 830-4511. Email: ajroofingohio@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Project details you give us: roof or siding work needed, the town or address, and anything you tell us in a message or send as a photo.
  • Technical information our website host may record automatically, such as IP address, browser, device and pages requested, to run and secure the site.

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising and does not use analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

3. The request form

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

4. How we use information

  • To answer your questions and quote requests.
  • To schedule, carry out and follow up on roofing and siding work.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records, and to operate and secure this website.

5. Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of receiving service.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example website hosting and email), when the law requires it, or with a successor if the business is sold.

7. Third-party services

This site links to services such as Google Maps and Facebook. They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information as long as we need it to respond to you, complete work and meet business, tax and legal obligations, then delete it or keep it only in a form that does not identify you.

9. Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by calling, texting or emailing us. We will answer within a reasonable time.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
