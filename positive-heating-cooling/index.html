What to note

Select a symptom to see a next step.

  • Hot rooms. Note which rooms warm up and when. Compare sunny rooms with rooms on the shaded side.
  • Unusual noise. Note when the sound happens: at startup, while running or when the system stops.
  • Higher bills. Compare similar weather and billing periods. Note any recent changes in thermostat settings.
  • More dust. Check when you last replaced the filter. Take note of rooms that collect dust faster.
  • Older system. If you can see it without removing any panels, photograph the model label and note recurring problems.

Tune-up season.

A heating and cooling check usually starts with how the system runs. Here are three topics to ask about.

Filter and airflow

Ask which filter fits your system and whether airflow looks even at the vents and returns.

Controls and operation

A check can compare the thermostat setting with room temperature and observe how the system starts and stops.

Drainage and equipment condition

For cooling equipment, ask whether the condensate drain is clear and whether there are visible signs of wear or buildup.
