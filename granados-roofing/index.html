Hail or high wind?

North Texas storms lift shingles and dent gutters without much showing from the street. Before anything else:

  1. Stay off the roof

    Wet or damaged shingles are slick. A fall is worse than any leak.

  2. Photograph from the ground

    Granules in the yard, dented gutters and vents, stains on ceilings.

  3. Call for an inspection

    Text your photos to (940) 595-4894 and we'll look at the roof itself.
