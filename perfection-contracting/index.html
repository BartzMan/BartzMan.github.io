Sheet 04The system

How it fits
together.

Air goes out, air comes back, and one unit outside does the heavy lifting. Scroll and the drawing builds itself.

Fig. C · Section through a home00%
  1. ASupply airCarries conditioned air out to each register.
  2. BReturn airBrings house air back to the unit.
  3. CFurnaceThe indoor unit where air is heated.
  4. DThermostatThe wall control where you set 72.
  5. EOutdoor unitSits outside and does the cooling work.
  6. FLine setJoins the indoor side to the outdoor side.

01Air comes back

House air is drawn back to the unit through the return, to be warmed or cooled again.

02Air goes out

Supply ducts carry it to every register, quietly and evenly.

03Outside does the work

The outdoor unit and its line set connect to the indoor side. Fitted properly, they never get a thought.
