The pattern

Six jobs,
laid in a pattern.

Hardscape divides up cleanly. Either you need new ground laid, or you need something done to ground that is already there. The bricks below are in the order a job usually runs: dig, lay, edge, joint, seal, repair.

01

Paver driveways

Front of house, side of house, or all the way round to the garage. Base, edge restraint, pavers, jointing sand.

02

Patios

A patio has to sit level with a door, a pool coping and a grill pad. Two out of those three are set by something you cannot move.

03

Pool decks

The surface you actually stand on in the sun. It takes the weight, the water and the years, so the base matters more than the pattern.

04

Paver sealing

A sealer goes on after the pavers are down and clean. It darkens them, and it makes them easier to wash.

05

Retaining walls

Block, cap stone, and the drainage behind it. The wall that fails is the one with no gravel behind it.

06

Paver repairs

Settling, weeds in the joints, a strip lifted by a root, a driveway somebody has already patched twice.

Seal it

Why pavers
get sealed.

Pavers are porous. That is the whole point of them: they take water, they take salt, and they take the sand that washes out of the joints. A sealer closes the surface so dirt stops going in and the colour stays where it is instead of fading unevenly.

It is a maintenance decision rather than a repair, and it belongs on the same maintenance schedule as the roof paint and the gutters.

One honest note: sealing a surface that was never laid properly just seals the problem and makes it harder to see.
