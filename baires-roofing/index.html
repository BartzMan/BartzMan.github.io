What we do

Up top, four ways.

Roof replacement

A whole new roof, from the old shingles to the new. You need it when wear shows across the whole roof, not in one spot.

Roof repair

Fixing what is failing without redoing the rest: a leak, lifted shingles, worn flashing. It fits when the rest of the roof is sound.

Valleys, flashing & metal details

Valleys and flashing carry the water where roof planes meet. You need attention here when a leak follows a seam or chimney edge.

Shingle roofs

Shingles come in many colours and textures. A good choice sits well with the brick, trim and what is on the street.

Which job is it?

Replace or repair?

Replacing makes sense when…

  • shingles are curling, cracked or bare across whole slopes
  • leaks keep turning up in different places
  • repairs have been needed again and again
  • you are changing the look of the house and want a new colour

A new roof is also the moment to choose a shingle that suits the brick. Use the guide above.

Repairing makes sense when…

  • a few shingles are lifted, cracked or missing
  • one leak traces to a valley, vent or flashing
  • the damage is in one area
  • the rest of the roof still lies flat and even

Fixing the failing detail keeps the roof you already have working.

Choosing a shingle colour for a brick home: 5 tips.

Tap a brick in the wall to read a tip. Colour is easy to get wrong on a small sample, so take your time.

Tip 1

Look at the brick in daylight

Brick can look redder in sun and browner in shade. Judge the colour outdoors, at different times of day, before you pick a shingle.
