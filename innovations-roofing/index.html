A202The firm

Two trades, one set of drawings.

Innovations Roofing & Remodeling works on both halves of a Houston house: the roof that keeps the weather out, and the rooms underneath it. One number to call for either.

B103Roofing

Roofing, in section.

A roof is a stack of layers. Tap a layer on the drawing to see what it does and why it matters on a replacement.

Detail B1 · Roof edge to ridge · illustration

Shingles. Laid in overlapping courses from the eave up, each one covering the nails of the one below it, so rain runs over and off.

Service 01

Roof replacement

The old roof comes off down to the decking, damaged boards are replaced, and every layer in Detail B1 goes back on new. You need it when a roof is worn out, leaking in several places or badly storm-damaged.

Service 02

Roof repair

Fixing one problem area instead of the whole roof: missing or lifted shingles, a cracked boot around a vent pipe, loose flashing. You need it when a leak or damage is limited to one spot.

C104Remodeling · Service 03

Remodeling, drawn as a plan.

Remodeling means changing a room you already have: a kitchen, a bath or a whole room, with new layout, finishes and lighting. You need it when a room no longer works the way you live. Tap a spot on the plan for the decision made there.

Island. Leave enough walkway around an island for two people to pass and for the dishwasher and oven doors to open all the way.
