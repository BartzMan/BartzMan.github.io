Services

Three kinds of work

  1. 01

    Split-unit (mini-split) air conditioning installation

    One indoor unit on the wall inside, one outdoor unit outside, and the line run between them. We install into a single room, into a room that already has a unit, and into rooms where the outdoor unit has to go on a pad or onto a siding wall.

  2. 02

    Air conditioning repair

    For split systems that stop cooling, warm unevenly, drip water, keep resetting, or show an error code on the indoor unit. Send a photo of the display and we start from there.

  3. 03

    Multi-zone systems

    One outdoor unit feeding several indoor units, each with its own control, instead of a single thermostat for the whole floor. Send a photo of the rooms you want on it and we will tell you what the layout can take.

How it works

Three steps, and nothing automatic about them

One

You call or text

Which room, which system, what it is doing, and when you noticed. A photo of the indoor unit and the outdoor unit is worth more than a description.

Two

We take a look

We look at the equipment, at the space it has to fit into, and at where an outdoor unit could sit. Then we talk through what the job actually involves.

Three

You decide

You get our read on it before anything is agreed. If the sensible answer is to leave it alone or replace a part, that is what we will say.

Questions

Four things people ask first

How do I get an estimate for a mini-split?

Call or text us with the room, what is already in place and what you want done. We will talk through the job and what it involves. Any figures we give before you approve a written scope are an estimate only.

What should I do if the air conditioning stops?

If the system quits and the rooms get hot, call or text us and tell us what the unit is showing. If you smell gas, suspect a carbon monoxide problem or see smoke, leave the building, call 911, then call your gas utility. Do not rely on a website in an emergency.

Which areas do you work in?

Astoria, Queens. If you are just outside the neighbourhood, ask anyway. We will tell you straight away whether we can take the job.

What should I put in a message?

Your name, a phone number that reaches you, the street you are on, the type of unit if you can see the nameplate, and what the system is doing. Photos of the indoor unit and the outdoor unit help more than a description.
