Notes on the sheet

  1. 01
    No hot water. Cold at one faucet or at every faucet, and is the heat in the house still running? A photo of the water heater or boiler plate helps.
  2. 02
    A leak. Where is the water showing up: under a sink, at a toilet, or on a ceiling? Which floor? A photo of the wet spot helps.
  3. 03
    Radiators and heat. A radiator that stays cold, or a room that never warms. Which rooms, and does the thermostat click when you turn it up?
  4. 04
    What to send. Your name, a phone number, the town in Bergen County, and a photo of the fixture or the equipment plate. You do not need to take anything apart.
