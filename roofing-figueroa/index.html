Do I need a repair or a whole new roof?

It depends on what is going on up there. A leak around one vent or a few lost shingles is often a repair. Shingles worn or damaged across several roof planes usually points to a replacement. Send us photos and we will tell you plainly which one we think your roof needs.

Hail came through. Can you check my roof?

Yes. Call or message us and we will set up a time to look at it. If you can safely take a few photos from the ground first, send them on WhatsApp so we know what to expect.

How long does a re-roof take?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof and on the weather, so ask us. Once we have seen your house we will give you a straight answer for your roof.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

Roof photos. A wide shot of each side of the house, close-ups of anything that worries you (missing shingles, a stained ceiling, a loose vent) and the area of Dallas you are in.

Do you also build fences and remodel?

Yes. Roofing comes first, but we also install fences and remodel bathrooms and kitchens.
