Shingle repair

Not every roof needs to start over.

Wind flips a course, a shingle goes missing, a patch slides. The rest of the roof can be perfectly good. We pull the bad section, weave new shingles into the courses around it, and leave the rest alone.

  • Wind-liftedCourses that lifted and won't lie flat again.
  • MissingBare felt and nail heads showing through.
  • DamagedCracked or broken shingles in an otherwise sound roof.
