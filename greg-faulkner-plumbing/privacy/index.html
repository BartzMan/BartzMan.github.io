Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to personal details on the website of Greg Faulkner Plumbing Company, a plumbing business in Middleburg, Florida (“we”, “us”).

The request form

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

We use those details only to answer your request, to talk with you about the plumbing work you asked about, and to keep a simple record of the job if you go ahead. We do not sell them and we do not use them for advertising.

Calls, texts and email

If you call, text or email us, your phone number or email address and what you tell us reach us directly through your own phone or email provider. We use them only to deal with your enquiry or job.

What this site does not do

  • It does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • It does not ask for payment details.
  • Fonts and images are served from this website itself, not from third-party services.

The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Links to other sites

This site links to our Facebook page. Facebook has its own privacy policy, which applies when you visit it.

How long we keep details

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to answer you and, if you go ahead, for the job and our ordinary business records.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Call (904) 282-5585 or email gfaulknerplumbing@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
