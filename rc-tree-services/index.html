02 · The yard plan

One yard. Five jobs.

Tap a tree or a fence run
Your yard

What happens where?

Five spots on this plan, five jobs. Tap one to see the work and a photo of it.

1 · Tree trimming

The spreading oak

Taking dead or crowding limbs off a healthy tree opens up light and keeps branches off the roof and fence line. It is easier on the tree when limbs come off before they rub, droop or hang over.

2 · Tree removal

The tall pine

When a tree is dead, leaning or too close to the house, taking it down in sections is the usual way in a tight yard. Each piece is lowered rather than dropped.

3 · Landscaping and cleanup

The brush and cut rounds

After limbs and trunks come down, the rounds and brush still have to leave. Hauling them off and tidying the lawn is what makes the yard usable again.

4 · Wood fence building

The back run

A new wood fence is planned as one line: posts set first so everything stays straight, then rails, then pickets. It marks the edge of the yard and gives it privacy.

5 · Wood fence building

The gate

A gate is planned with the fence, not added later. Its width decides what can come through, from a mower to a trailer, and its posts are set the same way as the rest.
