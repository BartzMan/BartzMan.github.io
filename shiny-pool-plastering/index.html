Shell and surface prep

Getting the surface ready

A drained pool shows its bare shell. The surface is cleaned and prepared so the new finish has something sound to bond to.

Plaster finish

The plaster finish

The smooth coat that lines the pool and gives the water its colour, troweled across the walls, floor and steps.

Waterline tile

Waterline tile

A band of glass or ceramic mosaic tile along the waterline, and tile accents on steps and spas.

Spa

Spas

Round and shaped spas with their own plaster finish and tile border, often raised beside the pool.

Steps and benches

Steps and benches

Entry steps and seats finished in the same plaster as the pool, often edged with a tile line.

Coping

Coping at the edge

The cap along the top of the pool wall, in brick, stone or tile, where the water meets the deck.

Questions

Common questions, in plain words.

How do I know if my pool surface needs attention?
Look for a rough texture, stains, chips, cracks or patched spots on the walls and floor. If you are not sure what you are seeing, send close-up photos and a photo of the whole pool and we can take a look.
What does the tile at the waterline do?
Waterline tile gives the pool a clean, decorative band where the water meets the wall, and it is easy to wipe down. Mosaic tile is also used as a border on steps and around spas.
Do you work on spas as well as pools?
The business's photos include a round spa with a tile ring as well as full-size pools. Call or message to ask about your own spa.
What should I send before you come out?
A photo of the whole pool, a few close-ups of the surface and tile, and a line about what you want done. Message them to (832) 546-3571 on WhatsApp or call (832) 546-3571.
