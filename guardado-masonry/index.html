Brick Repair

When a section of brick wall is loose, bowed or has pulled away from the house, it can be taken down and rebuilt. The loose brick comes out, new brick ties go in to connect the brick to the wall behind it, and the section is laid back up to match the wall around it as closely as the brick allows.

  • Loose or separated sections taken down
  • New brick ties set into the mortar joints
  • Rebuilt to match the existing wall
