My AC is running but not cooling. What should I check?

Make sure the thermostat is on cool and set below the room temperature, and that the air filter isn't clogged. Then look at the outdoor unit. If its fan isn't spinning, or the large copper line is covered in ice, switch the system off and call. Running it while it's frozen can damage the compressor.

Water is dripping from the attic unit. What do I do?

Attic air handlers sit over a drain pan with a drain line. When the line clogs or the pan rusts through, the water ends up in your ceiling. Turn the system off at the thermostat, put a bucket under the drip, and call or text us a photo of the unit and the pan.

How do I book a visit?

Call or text (832) 455-1055, email huskycomfortair@gmail.com, or message huskycomfortair on WeChat. Tell us your city and what the system is doing.

What should I send before you come out?

A photo of the data plate on the outdoor unit and on the indoor unit, which shows the model and age, a photo of the thermostat screen, and a line about what's happening.
