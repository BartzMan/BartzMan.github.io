Three materials, one trade

Brick. Rock. Block.

The three words on the business's own Facebook page. Each one is laid differently, and a repair has to sit with what is already there.

01 · Brick

Fired clay, in courses

Each brick sits over the joint below it, half a brick along. The joints are filled and struck so the water runs off.

02 · Rock

Natural stone, by hand

No two pieces match. Each one is picked, turned and fitted to its neighbours before it is set.

03 · Block

Concrete units, for structure

Larger hollow units for walls and backing, laid level and plumb so the face work has something true to sit on.

How a job goes

Four courses, bottom to top.

Every wall starts at the first course. So does every job.

Do you take small repairs?

Yes. A few loose bricks, a cracked mortar joint or a tree ring with a corner knocked out are all worth a call. Text a photo first so the job can be talked through.

Can you build a new mailbox, or only repair one?

Both. Brick and stone mailboxes are built new, and existing ones are repaired: a loose cap, a cracked face or a number plate that needs setting.

Do you work with rock and block as well as brick?

Yes. Brick, rock and block are all part of the trade, for repairs, remodels and new custom homes.
