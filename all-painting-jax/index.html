Before you call

Four questions.

1How long does an exterior repaint take?

It depends on the size of the house, how much prep the walls and trim need, and the weather. Ask us: send a few photos or call, and we will talk it through for your house.

2Should the house be pressure washed before painting?

Pressure washing is one of our services, and paint goes on best over a clean surface. Ask us whether your siding, stucco or block needs a wash first and we will tell you what we see.

3Do you paint commercial buildings?

Yes. Commercial and residential painting are both part of what we do. The block building with the lime stripe on this page is one of ours.

4What should I send for a quote?

A few photos of the house or building, which parts you want painted or washed, and roughly where in Jacksonville it is. Use the form, call or text (904) 236-9511.

Another question about your house or building?

Text a photo of it and ask. That is usually the quickest way to a straight answer.
