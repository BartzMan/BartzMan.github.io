Privacy policy

This policy explains how Fitzwell Plumbing of Medina, Ohio ("we", "us") handles the information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

If you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to tell us, in the normal way.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and talk about the work you need.
  • To arrange a visit and keep a simple record of the job.

We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with anyone except where needed to answer your request or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to deal with your request and keep ordinary business records.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. The hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run the site securely.

Links to other sites

Links to Google and Facebook take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details you have sent us, email fitzwellplumbing@aol.com or call (216) 633-8020.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
