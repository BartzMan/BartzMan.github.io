Privacy policy

This policy covers the website of Zadnik Heating and Air Conditioning (Zadnik LLC), Mentor, Ohio 44060. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you.

If you call, text or email us at (440) 488-6748 or zadnikhvac@gmail.com, we receive what you choose to send, such as your number, your message and any photos.

How we use it

Only to answer your request, arrange a visit, give you a quote and carry out and follow up on the work you ask for. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

The people at Zadnik who handle your request, and the service providers that carry our email, phone and website traffic, only as far as they need to.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to deal with your request and any work that follows, and to keep normal business records.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete them, by calling (440) 488-6748 or emailing zadnikhvac@gmail.com.

Changes
