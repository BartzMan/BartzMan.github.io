I The Leak Repairs ✦ ✦ ✦ turn

Repairs — the small faults with loud consequences

A dripping supply line, a slow drain, a toilet that runs at night. We find the source instead of the symptom, fix it with proper materials, and test it with the water on and the drains running.

  • Leak location and repair, supply and drain side
  • Fixture repair and replacement
  • Pressure problems traced to the cause

II The Current Drains & sewer ✦ ✦ ✦ turn

Drains & sewer — the line that carries everything away

Repeat clogs, gurgling fixtures, a cleanout that backs up after rain. We camera the line, mark the trouble on paper, and quote the fix before the shovel moves: root cutting, a spot repair, or a full replacement run.

  • Camera inspection and locate
  • Root removal, descaling, hydro jetting where the line allows
  • Spot repairs and full lateral replacement

Call or text about this: (650) 796-1411

III The Flame Water heaters ✦ ✦ ✦ turn

Water heaters — hot water without the surprise

Rusty water, a pilot that won't stay lit, a heater at the end of its run — we assess the unit honestly and say whether a repair buys years or weeks. When it's time, we swap heaters, bring venting and lines up to current layout, and haul the old one away.

  • Repairs and honest stay-or-replace reads
  • Tank swaps and tankless installs
  • Earthquake strapping and proper venting on every change-out

Call or text about this: (650) 796-1411

IV The Vein Repipes & underground ✦ ✦ ✦ turn

Repipes & underground — whole lines, done once

Galvanized supply throttled to a trickle, a lateral with a belly, a main leaking at the fitting — some jobs deserve a whole new run. We trench clean, install modern pipe, pressure-test everything, and backfill so the surface reads like we were never there.

  • Whole-house repipes in copper or PEX
  • Water main and sewer lateral replacement, trench or tunnel
  • Pressure test and camera verify before backfill — every time

THE READING · HOW A JOB GOES

The positions we lay for every job

Four positions, laid in order. The house asks; we answer with what's actually in the lines.

    The question
