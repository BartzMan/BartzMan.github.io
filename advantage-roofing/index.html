TAB 02HOW A STORM CLAIM RUNS

Five pages.
One file.

Tap a page to open it. Every storm claim moves through the same five stages.

1

The storm

Hail or wind comes through. First, stay safe and stay off the roof. Then walk the yard, take dated photos of what you can see from the ground, and call us.

Page 1 of 5 · Notes: the date, the weather, what you saw.

2

The inspection

A roofer goes over the roof and the property: shingles, vents, flashing, gutters. Anything the storm touched gets marked, like the circle on this page.

Page 2 of 5 · Marked: what, and where.

3

The documentation

Every finding is photographed up close and wide, then written down: where it is and what it is. That file is what your insurance carrier reads.

Page 3 of 5 · Photos, notes, dates, in order.

4

The carrier meeting

We work with your carrier, not against it. The adjuster and the roofer look at the same photos and the same notes, so everyone is talking about the same roof.

Page 4 of 5 · Attached: the file from page 3.

5

The rebuild

Once the plan is agreed, the old roof comes off and the new one goes on: deck checked, underlayment, shingles, ridge, then the yard cleaned up.

Page 5 of 5 · Closed: a finished roof.
