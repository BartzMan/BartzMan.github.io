Exterior painting

The coat that keeps Katy sun and rain off your stucco, siding, trim and fascia; it’s time when the paint chalks on your hand, fades, or starts to peel.

Interior painting

Pressure washing & cleaning

Water under pressure lifts dirt, mildew and chalky old paint off walls, walks and patios; you need it before any repaint, or whenever green and black spots show up on the shady side.
