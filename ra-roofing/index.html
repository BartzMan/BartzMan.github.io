Sheet A-3 Before you call

Ice dams: read the signs from inside.

What it is

Heat escaping into the attic melts snow on the upper roof. The water runs down to the cold edge and freezes into a ridge of ice. More water backs up behind it.

Signs from inside

Brown stains or damp patches on ceilings and upper walls near an outside wall. Peeling paint along the top of a wall. Wet, flattened insulation if you look in from the attic hatch.

Signs from outside

Look from the ground: thick ice along the eaves, long icicles, or snow that melts unevenly across the roof. Stay off the roof and off ladders in winter.

What to do

Keep gutters clear in fall. Do not chip at the ice. If water is coming in, set out a bucket, take photos and call a roofer.

Seeing a stain? Take a dated photo, then call or text.
