East Texas homeowner guide

Porch or pergola?

Start with when you want to sit outside and what gets in the way. Either structure can make part of the yard more useful.

Mosquitoes

A screened porch gives you a barrier when the screen panels and door are closed.

Shade

A pergola’s open top can give partial shade. Notice where the afternoon sun falls before choosing a location.

Rain

A roofed porch gives overhead cover. An open pergola lets rain through.

Upkeep

Check screen mesh and framing periodically. Keep wood and finishes clean and follow their care instructions.
