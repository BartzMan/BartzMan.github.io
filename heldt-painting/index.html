Ask about a job →

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors, inside. You need it when a room looks tired, a colour has dated, or patches and old nail holes show through.

Exterior & trim

Siding, trim, garage doors and porches. It is time when paint peels, chalks off on your hand, or bare wood starts to show at the edges.

Fences & decks

Stain or paint for decks, steps, railings and fences. Wood outdoors turns grey and soaks up water once its coat wears through, and a fresh one keeps it from drying out and splitting.

Basements & floors

Basement rooms, stairs and the floors under them, finished together. Useful when a lower level is about to become a family room, a guest room or just somewhere you want to be.

Home improvements

The other jobs on the list: kitchen and room updates, repairs and the fixes that come before paint. Worth a call when a project is more than one trade's worth of work.
