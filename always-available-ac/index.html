Inset B · part 1

The indoor unit

The slim unit high on the wall. It draws in room air, cools it over a coil and blows it back into the room.

Inset B · part 2

The line set

Two copper lines, a cable and a drain run through a small hole in the wall, usually under a cover on the outside.

Inset B · part 3

The outdoor unit

It sits outside on feet or a pad and moves the heat from indoors out into the open air, so it needs room to breathe.

Inset B · part 4

The remote

Sets the mode, temperature and fan speed for that room's unit. Many live in a holder on the wall.

Before you call · general advice for any home

Take a bearing first.

Pick what the system is doing. The needle swings to a few simple checks anyone can make, and to the kind of help it points to.

Bearing 000° · warm air

Warm air from the vents

  1. Check the thermostat is set to Cool, and a few degrees below the room.
  2. Look at the air filter. A clogged one chokes the airflow.
Still warm? That points to AC repair →

Bearing 090° · water inside

Water near the indoor unit

  1. Switch the system off at the thermostat.
  2. Dry the area and keep things clear of the drip, then call.
That points to AC repair →

Bearing 180° · silent outside

Outdoor unit is silent

  1. Check that the breaker for the AC hasn't tripped.
  2. Clear leaves and plants from around the outdoor unit.
Still silent? That points to AC repair →

Bearing 270° · ice

Ice on the pipes

  1. Set the system to Fan only, or off, so the ice can melt.
  2. Don't chip at it. Change a dirty filter and call a pro.
That points to a check-up →

If you smell burning or see sparks, switch the system off at the breaker and call a pro.
