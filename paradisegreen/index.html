Common questions

Will you follow my HOA guidelines?

Yes. Send the packet and we will match rock color and the approved plant list before we order anything.

Can you fix a burnt lawn?

Sometimes overseeding is enough. If the soil is gone, replacing the lawn is cheaper than another season of seed that fails in May. We’ll tell you which it is after we look.

How soon can you start?

Call (602) 421-1175. West Phoenix jobs can often start within the week if the calendar is open.

Do you haul debris?

Yes. We take green waste and leftover rock with us.

Do you install tropical plants?

We plant for Phoenix heat. If a species won’t live through June here, we will suggest something that will.

What do you need from me?

The street address, a photo from the curb, and whether you want the front, the back, or both. A phone number is enough to start.
