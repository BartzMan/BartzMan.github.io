The panel map.

Older Queens two-family homes tend to have the same few weak spots. Tap a breaker to see what usually goes wrong on that circuit and which service fixes it. Our service here: panel upgrades and replacements.

Tap a breaker. Never open a panel cover yourself.

1Kitchen circuit

Toaster, kettle and microwave at once.

What usually goes wrong: in an older home several kitchen appliances share one circuit, and the breaker trips whenever they run together.

Service: wiring and outlet work to spread the load; a panel upgrade if there is no room left.

2Laundry circuit

A dryer on a tired line.

What usually goes wrong: an old or shared circuit for the dryer or washer, with plugs that run warm or a breaker that keeps tripping.

Service: a circuit of its own for the machine and a look at the panel.

3Bath circuit

The outlet by the water that won’t reset.

What usually goes wrong: old outlets near the sink that stop working, or trip the breaker when a hair dryer runs.

Service: outlet replacement and a check of the circuit.

4Lights circuit

Lights that flicker or dim.

What usually goes wrong: a loose connection at a switch or fixture, or a switch that feels warm.

Service: lighting and switch work, smart thermostats included.

5A/C circuit

The air conditioner that trips on hot days.

What usually goes wrong: a circuit that is shared or too small for the unit, or no disconnect near the unit.

Service: a dedicated circuit and a disconnect beside the equipment.

6Outlets circuit

Loose, two-prong or dead outlets.

What usually goes wrong: old outlets that no longer grip a plug, or a run of outlets that has lost power.

Service: replacing outlets and rewiring the run.

1This station’s servicePanel upgrades and replacements

Service notice

Breaker keeps tripping in an older Queens home? 4 checks.

Think about what was running: a space heater, the microwave, a dryer. Often the breaker is only telling you the circuit was asked to carry too much.

Note which breaker tripped and which rooms or appliances its label names. Write it down: it helps whoever comes to look.

Unplug what was on that circuit, then reset the breaker once.

If it trips again, or anything smells hot, stop and call. Never open the panel cover.
