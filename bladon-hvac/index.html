❦ Table III · the season log

Three jobs for each season.

Plain checks any Toledo homeowner can do, quarter by quarter. Pick a season on the wheel.

❄ Winter

  1. Check the furnace filter every month while the heat runs hard; swap it when it's grey.
  2. Keep snow and ice off outdoor vents and the plastic intake pipes of high-efficiency furnaces.
  3. Hold the thermostat steady; big swings make the furnace work harder.

❅ Spring

  1. Put in a fresh filter as the heating season ends.
  2. Clear leaves and winter debris from around the outdoor AC unit.
  3. Before the first hot day, switch to cool and make sure cold air comes out.

☀ Summer

  1. Check the filter every month or two while the AC runs.
  2. Keep shrubs trimmed back from the outdoor unit so it can breathe.
  3. Close the blinds on the sunny side of the house in the afternoon.

❦ Autumn

  1. Before the first cold night, switch to heat and make sure warm air comes out.
  2. Fit a new filter for the heating season.
  3. Test the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms when the clocks change.

Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas company from outside.
