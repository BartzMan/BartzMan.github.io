06° Before you call

Good questions.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call (740) 383-5012 or use the request form. Tell us what the equipment is doing, what it is if you know, and which town you are in, and we will get back to you to talk it through.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Do not switch anything on or off. Leave the house, then call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Call us about the equipment once the utility says it is safe.

When should I book a furnace or AC check?

Book the furnace check in early fall, before the first cold snap, and the air conditioning check in spring, before the first hot, humid week. That way each system is looked at before it has to work hard.

What should I put in the request?

Your name, a phone number and email, what the system is doing, any make or model details from the label, and your town. Please leave out payment details and other sensitive information.
