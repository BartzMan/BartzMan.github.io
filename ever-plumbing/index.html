0–2 FT · TOPSOIL

The easy layer. The problem is rarely here.

Most plumbing calls start at the surface — a faucet, a drain, a water heater in the garage. We fix those like anyone would. What makes this crew different is everything under the topsoil: the lines that run under yards, driveways and slabs, where a wrong guess costs a lawn.

  • Water main — the line that feeds the house from the street
  • Sewer lateral — the line that carries everything away
  • Drain and supply lines — under slab, in walls, behind finishes
2–5 FT · CLAY

The cutaway: your yard, opened up

This is the map we work from. Pick a line and trace it — the water coming in, or the waste going out.

Pick a line above. The blue one brings water in from the street. The terracotta one carries waste out. Every underground job starts by knowing exactly which of these is the problem — and where.

The water main. It runs from the city tap, under the yard, and up into the house — the line every faucet and the water heater depend on. Pressure that sags, a meter that spins with everything off, or a soft spot in the lawn: that's this line telling on itself. We locate it, expose it cleanly, and replace or repair the run that's failed.

The sewer lateral. It slopes from the house, under the yard, out to the street — quietly doing the least glamorous job on the property. Roots find its joints; ground settles break its grade. We camera and locate the line first, so the dig happens in one right place instead of three wrong ones.

5–8 FT · GRAVEL

Work strata: what we're called out for

Four layers of the trade, from the tap to the trench. Open a plate for the details.

Leaks, clogs, and things that shouldn't be dripping

A dripping supply line, a slow drain, a toilet that runs at 2 a.m. — small faults with loud consequences. We find the actual source instead of patching the symptom, fix it with proper materials, and test it with the water on and the drains running.

  • Leak location and repair — supply and drain side
  • Fixture repair and replacement, done to the studs if needed
  • Pressure problems traced to their cause

The line that carries everything away

Repeat clogs, gurgling fixtures, a cleanout that backs up after heavy rain — the lateral is telling you something. We camera the line, mark the trouble on paper, and quote the fix before the shovel goes in: root cutting, a spot repair, or a full replacement run.

  • Camera inspection and locate, so the dig is in the right spot
  • Root removal, descaling and hydro jetting where the line allows
  • Spot repairs and full lateral replacement

Hot water, without the surprise

Rusty water, a pilot that won't stay lit, a heater that's old enough to vote — we assess the unit honestly and tell you whether a repair buys you years or weeks. When it's time, we swap heaters, upgrade venting and lines to current layout, and haul the old one away.

  • Repairs and honest stay-or-replace reads
  • Tank swaps and tankless installs, code-current connections
  • Earthquake strapping and proper venting on every change-out

Whole-line work, above and below grade

Galvanized supply that's throttled to a trickle, a lateral with a belly, a main that leaks at the fitting — some jobs deserve a whole new run. We trench clean, install modern pipe, pressure-test everything, and backfill so the surface reads like we were never there.

  • Whole-house repipes in copper or PEX, walls respected
  • Water main and sewer lateral replacement, trench or tunnel
  • Pressure test and camera verify before backfill — every time
