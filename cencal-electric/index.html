Q1Do you install EV chargers at home?

Yes. EV charger installation is one of CenCal Electric's five main services: the charger on the wall and the circuit that feeds it. Text a photo of your garage or the wall where you want it, and of your panel.

Q2Can you work on panels and meters?

Yes. Panels and meters are on the list, along with the breakers inside them and the wiring between them. The two open panels in the photos on this page are CenCal Electric's own work photos.

Q3Is a single outlet too small a job?

No. Outlet and switch repair is one of the five services, and small jobs are welcome. Tell us which outlet or switch it is and what it is doing.

Q4What should I put in my text?

Your name, your town or ZIP, what is going on, and a photo if you can: the outlet, the panel, the light or the spot for the charger. A photo helps us understand the job before we call you back.

Q5Where do you work, and when can I call?

CenCal Electric is based in Richmond, California and works across the East Bay. Hours as listed on Google: 7 AM to 6 PM, seven days a week.
