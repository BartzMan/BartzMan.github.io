Settle five decisions before anyone picks tile. Tap a tread to open it. Having answers makes every quote easier to compare.

General home advice only.

  1. Keeping the tub, toilet and vanity where they are is the simpler path. Moving them means opening floors and walls, so decide this first.

  2. Think about who uses the room every day. A walk-in shower saves space; a tub suits a house with small children.

  3. Count towels, toiletries and cleaning supplies. Drawers, a tall linen cabinet or a recessed niche all make room.

  4. Plan light at the mirror, overhead and in the shower, so faces and tile look right at any time of day.

  5. A bathroom needs a fan that moves damp air outside. Pick one sized for the room and have it vented outdoors, not into the attic.
