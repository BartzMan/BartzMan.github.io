Section 01 · scope of work

On the ticket.

Sales, service and installation on the same truck. Every call starts the same way: tell us what stopped working, we put it on the ticket and quote it straight.

  1. HTG · 01

    Heating

    Furnace sales, service and installation: new units set in closets and basements, venting run clean, the whole system started up and checked before we pull the ladder down.

  2. AC · 02

    Air conditioning

    Condensers and split systems: the pad set, the unit level, the lineset and gauge work done clean, and the charge checked before the crew rolls off.

  3. MS · 03

    Ductless mini-splits

    Heads for the rooms the ductwork never reached: additions, converted attics, sunrooms. Mounted, line-set and tested.

  4. COM · 04

    Commercial & refrigeration

    Packaged units set on slabs beside shops and metal buildings, refrigeration service for kitchens and coolers. Commercial calls run on the same ticket as the house next door.

  5. APL · 05

    Household appliances

    Washer, dryer, range, dishwasher and fridge repair. The same visit can cover the HVAC call and the appliance that quit the same week.
