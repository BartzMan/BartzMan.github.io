Plate IVBefore you call anyone

Planning a paver patio or driveway in Florida? Five things to decide.

Where the water will go

Walk the area after a heavy rain. Water should run away from the house and off the pavers, not pool against a wall. A slope you can barely see is enough.

How deep the base is

Cars and feet need different bases. A driveway sits on a deeper compacted layer than a patio. Ask whoever lays it what depth they use, and why.

What the border looks like

Decide the edge early: a row in a contrast colour, or pavers cut to follow a curve. The border also holds the edge of the area in place.

Colour in full sun

Dark pavers soak up Florida sun. Lighter tones reflect more heat, which matters on a pool deck or a patio where people go barefoot. Look at samples outdoors.

Sand in the joints

Pavers are held together by sand swept into the joints. Heavy rain can wash some out over time, so expect to top it up now and then.
