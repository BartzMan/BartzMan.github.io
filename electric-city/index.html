Questions, answered.

WCan one company handle plumbing, electrical and HVAC?

Yes. Electric City covers plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and gas lines and meters, so one call can cover a job that touches more than one trade.

EWhat are TECL 31382 and M-41269?

They are the Texas trade numbers shown on Electric City's Facebook page: TECL 31382 for electrical work and M-41269 for plumbing. Texas electrical numbers are listed by TDLR and plumbing numbers by the State Board of Plumbing Examiners, and both can be looked up online.

GDo you work on gas lines and meters?

Yes. Gas lines and meters are one of the services, like the new gas meter and piping in the photo at the top of this page.

WWhen can I call?

The Google listing shows 7 AM to 7 PM, every day of the week. You can also send a text with photos of the job.

EWhat should I put in a text?

Your name, where the job is, which line it touches (plumbing, electrical, HVAC or gas) and a photo or two of the problem.
