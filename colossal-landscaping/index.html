Before you top up the bed

Mulch, with room to breathe.

Mulch can help soil hold moisture and make weeds easier to manage. Start by checking what is already in the bed.

Check the depth

For many garden beds, about two to four inches of organic mulch is enough. Measure what is left before adding more.

Define the edge

Clear weeds at the border and keep loose mulch off paths and lawns.

Keep the trunk clear

Leave a gap around stems and tree trunks so the mulch stays on the soil.
