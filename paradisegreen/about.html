About Paradise Green

Landscaping, lawn, and pavers for west Phoenix homes.

Paradise Green Landscaping is based at 3346 W Monterosa Street in Alhambra. We do front-yard and backyard work for homes in Phoenix, Maryvale, Encanto, North Phoenix, and Glendale.

A lot of yards we see were installed for a cool weekend and then cooked by June. Gravel spreads, lawn edges burn, and plants that belong in a milder climate don’t make it. We use desert plants that already grow on these streets — palo verde, Texas sage, red yucca, cactus — and we set irrigation so they last.

If you have HOA rules, send them. We match rock color and the plant list before we order materials.
