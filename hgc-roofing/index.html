LAYER 01

Roof deck

The plywood or boards fixed over the rafters. Every other layer is fastened to it, so it has to be flat and solid.

Sign it is failingSagging between rafters, or dark damp patches on the underside of the boards when you look from the attic.

Covered byDecking

LAYER 01 · DECK UP

What gets built,
layer by layer.

Open a card to see what to look for and where it sits in the roof.

L3+4

Roof replacement

Taking the old roof down to the deck and building a new one up, for when repairs no longer keep up.

Look for Shingles curling or missing across several slopes, or granules collecting at the downspouts.

L4

Roof repair

Fixing a leak, lifted shingles or damaged flashing when the rest of the roof still has life in it.

Look for A stain on a ceiling after rain, or a few shingles out of line when seen from the yard.

L1

Roof decking

Replacing soft or damaged boards and plywood underneath, so the new roof has a solid base.

Look for A dip or sag in the roofline, or darkened wood seen from inside the attic.

L2

Underlayment

The water-resistant sheet laid over the deck before the shingles go on, a second line of defence if rain gets past them.

Look for Water marks on the underside of the deck when you look from the attic after rain.

L5

Ridge, vents and flashing

The details at the peak, around chimneys and where roof meets wall, where leaks and trapped heat tend to start.

Look for An attic that stays very hot and stuffy, or broken ridge caps seen from the ground.
