Sheet 06 · Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Before you call: have handy

  1. What you see or hear
  2. Where in the house it is
  3. How old the fixture or system is
  4. A photo you can email
Email the photo
01There is no hot water. What should I tell you?

Call (201) 206-3164 and say what you see: no hot water at all, only lukewarm, or it runs out fast. Mention how old the water heater or boiler is if you know, and send a photo of its label by email.

02I have a leak. What do I do first?

If you can find the shut-off valve under the sink or at the main, close it to stop the water, then call. A photo of the leak and where it shows up (ceiling stain, puddle, wet wall) helps a lot.

03Do you work on radiators and heat?

Yes. Heating is half the business name. Cold radiators, banging pipes, heat that will not come on, and the thermostat that runs it are all part of the job.

04What should I put in an email?

Your name, your town, a phone number, what is going on, and photos: the leak, the fixture, the radiator, the label on the equipment. Email fcianfrone@live.com.

05Where do you work, and when can I call?

Fred's Plumbing & Heating is based in Bogota, New Jersey (07603) and works across Bergen County. Hours as listed on Google: Monday to Saturday 8 AM to 5 PM, closed Sunday.
