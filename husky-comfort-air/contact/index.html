Before the visit

Four things worth sending.

None of these are required, but each one makes the first conversation quicker. Text them to (832) 455-1055 or attach them to the quote email.

  1. 01

    Outdoor data plate

    The label on the side of the condenser lists the model and serial, which tell us its size and roughly how old it is.

  2. 02

    Indoor data plate

    The same label on the air handler or furnace, usually in the attic or a closet, if it is safe to reach.

  3. 03

    Thermostat screen

    A photo of the display shows the mode, the set point and the room temperature at the moment the problem is happening.

  4. 04

    One line on the problem

    When it started and what you notice: warm air, water, a noise, ice, or a unit that won't come on at all.
