How a cooling system works

Two sides.
One cold room.

74

That number on the thermostat is the easy part. Behind it, every air conditioner runs on two sides.

The low side

Cold refrigerant picks up the heat and humidity inside your home or business. This is the air you feel.

The high side

The condenser outside pushes that heat out into the Miami air. On a hot afternoon, it works hard.

When one side is off, the whole system struggles. That is where Pacific starts looking.

Before you call

Good questions.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call (305) 633-6722, email pacrefcorp@gmail.com or use the request form on this page. Tell us what the system is doing, the type of property and how to reach you.

Do you work on commercial systems?

Yes. Pacific works on residential and commercial air conditioning, as well as systems for large developments in Miami and South Florida.

Which areas do you cover?

Homes and businesses throughout South Florida, in Miami-Dade and Broward County. Tell us where the property is when you get in touch.

What should I include in my request?

The type of system if you know it, what it is doing (or not doing), the type of property and a good time to call. Please do not send payment details or ID numbers.

What if I smell gas or see smoke?

Leave the building first. If you smell gas, see smoke or suspect carbon monoxide, call 911 from outside, then contact your utility.
