Good questions

Before you call.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (718) 331-4822 or email info@iceagemechanical.net with a short description of what's happening, and a photo of the equipment if you can. We'll tell you the next step.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the building right away. From outside, call 911 and your gas utility, and don't use light switches, phones or flames inside. Call us once it's safe.

Do you work on mini-splits and rooftop units?

Yes. Tell us what you have, how old it is if you know, and what it's doing, and send a photo of the unit and its label.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Sunset Park and work across Brooklyn. Call with your address to confirm.
