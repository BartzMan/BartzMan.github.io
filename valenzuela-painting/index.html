Choosing an exterior colour on the Peninsula.

Test, light and HOA checks before you commit to a whole-house colour.

1
Test on the wall

Paint a sample board and look at it at different times of day — Peninsula fog and sun change a colour completely.

2
Watch the light

Coastal light is soft and blue-shifted; inland Peninsula sun is stronger. A chip that reads warm inland can look cool by the water.

3
Check the HOA

Many Peninsula neighbourhoods restrict exterior colours — confirm your shade is approved before ordering paint.

4
Prep first

Clean, sand and prime before colour goes on — prep is what makes a repaint last.
