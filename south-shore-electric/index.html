Electrical panel guide

Know the panel.

Pick a part to see what it does. Use the diagram as a guide without opening your panel.

Main breaker

It controls power to the panel as a whole. If the entire property keeps losing power and neighbors are unaffected, describe what happened to an electrician.

Branch breakers

Each branch breaker serves a circuit. If the same one trips repeatedly, note what stopped working and call an electrician.

GFCI protection

A GFCI detects some current leaks. An outlet that repeatedly loses power or will not reset needs attention.

AFCI protection

An AFCI detects patterns associated with electrical arcing. Repeated trips need an electrician to investigate.

Surge protection

A panel surge protector helps limit brief voltage spikes. If its status indicator changes, ask an electrician to check what it means.

Homeowner checklist

When it keeps tripping.

Notice what went off

A single room or group of outlets often points to one circuit. Write down what lost power and when it happened.

Note what was running

A trip may happen when several devices run at once, or when one device is switched on. Make a note of that pattern.

Call if it keeps happening

Leave a repeatedly tripping circuit off and call an electrician. If you notice heat, a burning smell or damage, turn off power if safe and call a pro.
