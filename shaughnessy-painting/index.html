Exteriors

Outside, the sun does the testing.

In Mesa and the East Valley the south and west walls take the full afternoon sun. Over the years paint fades, stucco picks up hairline cracks and trim starts to lift. These are the usual signs an exterior is ready for a new coat:

  • Fading colourWalls going pale or patchy on the sunny sides.
  • Hairline cracksThin cracks running through the stucco.
  • Peeling trimPaint lifting on fascia, trim and doors.
  • Chalky paintA powder that rubs off on your hand.

Questions, answered.

Do you paint both inside and outside?

Yes. Interior and exterior painting for homes, plus commercial painting, drywall work and accent walls, across Mesa and the East Valley.

Can drywall be fixed before the painting starts?

Yes. Drywall work is one of the services, so holes, dents and cracks can be repaired as part of the same job. Describe the damage or send a photo.

What should I send to get a price?

Call or text (602) 574-5881, or email the details with the form on this page. Photos of the walls or rooms, rough sizes and the colours you have in mind all help.

What does AZ ROC 350903 mean?

It is Shaughnessy Painting's number with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). Arizona contractors show their ROC number in their advertising, and anyone can look a number up on the ROC website.

Do you paint patterned accent walls?

Yes. Accent walls are one of the services, from a single bold colour to a pattern like the diamond wall shown on this page.
