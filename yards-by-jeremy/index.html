Palm season

Skinning a palm is a cut, not a guess.

The pineapple cut is the Arizona look: dead fronds off, seed pods cleared, and the old frond bases trimmed back so the top of the trunk shows a clean pattern of diamonds. Tall palms are worked from a ladder with a pole saw, and everything that comes down is hauled away.

How often does routine yard maintenance happen?

On a schedule you agree on together. Many yards do well on a regular repeating visit that keeps grass cut, edges trimmed and beds cleared. Call or text to talk through what your yard needs.

When should palms be trimmed in Arizona?

Many Valley homeowners have palms trimmed in late spring or early summer, once the cold nights are over, so dead fronds and seed pods are off before the hottest months. A palm can be cleaned up at other times of year too.

Can you plant shrubs into an existing desert gravel bed?

Yes. Shrubs and plants can be set into the gravel bed you already have: the plants are placed, holes dug, planted and watered in, and the gravel raked back around them.

What happens to the cut wood and green waste?

Branches, fronds, cut wood and green waste from the job are loaded up and hauled off, so nothing is left in a pile at the curb.

What should I send when I ask about a job?

A few phone photos of the yard, the palms or beds that need work, and how to get in (gate width, side yard, tight spots), plus the part of town you are in. Text them to (602) 332-1875 or attach them to an email.
