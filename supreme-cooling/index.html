Ductless or ducted.

The ductless mini-split

One outdoor unit connects by a slim line to a head on the wall inside. No ducts needed, so it suits rooms the old system never reached.

The central AC condenser

The outdoor box sends cooling to a coil in the furnace or air handler, and the air travels through ducts to every room on one thermostat.

Sheet 04 · Worth knowing

How many heads does a home need?

One head for each room you want to control on its own. Open plans, like a kitchen that flows into a living room, can often share one. And bigger is not better: an oversized head cools fast, shuts off, and leaves the air clammy. A site visit and a room-by-room load calculation settle the exact sizes.
