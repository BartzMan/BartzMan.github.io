Common questions

Do you install mini-splits?

Yes. Mini-split installation is one of the services Father & Sons AC & Heating offers. Tell us which room or rooms you want to cool and send a photo of the wall.

Can you replace an air handler?

Yes, air handler replacement is one of our services. A photo of the air handler and its label helps us understand what is there now.
