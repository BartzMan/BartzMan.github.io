What gets painted

Inside the house, outside the house.

Pick a side of the house. Each job starts the same way: the surface is made ready, and the lines are taped, before any colour goes on.

01

Interior painting

Walls and ceilings repainted room by room, so the colour you picked is the colour you end up living with.

02

Trim, doors & cabinets

Baseboards, door frames and cabinet faces are touched all day, so they need a smooth, well-prepped surface to hold a finish.

03

Drywall patching before paint

Nail holes, dings and cracks get filled and sanded flat first, because paint shows every bump underneath it.

Patch. Sand. Caulk. Prime. Mask.

That is the order a painter works in before a roller touches the wall. The colour is the last step, not the first.

01

Exterior painting

Siding, eaves and trim repainted to protect the surface and freshen the look of the house from the street.

02

Doors & outside trim

Front doors, frames and porch trim sit in sun and Gulf humidity, so they are cleaned, dried and primed where bare before they are painted.

Dry days only.

Exterior paint needs a dry surface and settled weather. In Houston that sometimes means waiting for the right few days, and that is part of doing it properly.
