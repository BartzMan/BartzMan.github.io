Good to know

Before any tree or palm work, check these.

A few things worth knowing in California before a saw comes out.

Check for nests first

Nesting birds are protected in California, and spring and summer are prime nesting time. If you see a nest in a tree or palm, tell us before we start.

Palms: leave the green

Dead and hazardous fronds are the target. Healthy green fronds feed the palm, so a good trim doesn't strip them.

Check before you remove

Many cities regulate street trees and protected natives like oaks, and some HOAs have rules. Check first, and call 811 before any digging.

Power lines: stay clear

Trees touching power lines are a job for the utility or a certified line-clearance crew. Don't touch them, and call the utility.
