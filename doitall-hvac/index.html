08Pre-flight checks

Before
you call.

CHK 1What do you work on?

Heating and cooling systems for homes: furnaces, central AC condensers, and the duct work, venting and connections between them. Both installation and service.

CHK 2My AC stopped working. What should I tell you?

What it is doing (no air, warm air, outdoor unit silent, ice on the copper lines), roughly how old the system is if you know, and your address. A photo of the unit's label helps too.

CHK 3Do you only replace equipment?

No. Service and repair are half the name. If a repair is the sensible answer, that is the answer you get.

CHK 4Where do you work?

Do It All is based in Beavercreek, Ohio. If you are elsewhere around Dayton, call or text with your street to check.

CHK 5I smell gas. What do I do?

Leave the house first, then call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not switch lights or appliances on or off on the way out. Do not use this website for anything urgent.
