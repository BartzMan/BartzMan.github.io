Which sheen for which room?

Sheen is how shiny paint dries. Pick a room and see the usual choice. A rule of thumb homeowners use everywhere; the label on the can has the final say.

Ceilings sit in raking light from windows and lamps, so flat hides the most.

Walls that get touched now and then: eggshell looks soft and still wipes clean.

Steam and splashes: satin stands up to moisture and a wet cloth.

Hands, shoes and vacuums hit them most: semi-gloss is the tough one.

Opened every day: satin or semi-gloss, in a paint made for cabinets.

Flat / matte

Hides bumps and patches, no glare. Hardest to scrub.

Eggshell

A soft glow that wipes clean better than flat.

Satin

Handles steam, splashes and scrubbing.

Semi-gloss

Tough and shiny: crisp edges, easy to wipe.
