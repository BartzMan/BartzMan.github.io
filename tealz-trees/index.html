Ring 02 · Services

Pick a ring.See the job.

Five kinds of tree work, laid out like the rings of a cut trunk. Tap a ring, or a name, to see what the job is and when it is worth a call.

Tap a ring

01

Trimming & pruning

Cutting back limbs that hang over the roof, the drive or the sidewalk, and thinning out crowded branches.

When to call: limbs scrape the house, block the street or drop leaves into the gutters.

Ask about trimming

02

Bucket-truck canopy work

A bucket truck lifts the cutter up into the canopy, for limbs too high or too far out to reach from a ladder.

When to call: the branches that need cutting are high over a road, a roof or a busy yard.

Ask about canopy work

03

Tree removal

Taking a whole tree down, piece by piece from the top, and cutting the trunk into rounds that can be moved.

When to call: the tree is dead, leaning toward the house or has simply outgrown its spot.

Ask about a removal

04

Storm-damaged trees

Cutting apart limbs and trunks that wind has split or brought down onto a house, a fence or the yard.

When to call: after a storm, once everyone is safe and well clear of any fallen wires.

Ask about storm damage

05

Clean-up

Gathering up the branches, leaves and cut rounds after trimming or a removal, so the yard can be used again.

When to ask: when you call about the tree work, say what you would like done with the wood.

Ask about clean-up

Ring 04 · Yard check

One ring.One year.

A tree adds one ring a year. Here is what to look at in a Florida yard as that year goes round, all from the ground. Tap a season.

SeasonJun–Aug

Leaf-out

Once the trees leaf out, dead limbs stand out bare against the green. Note any that hang over the roof, the drive or the fence.

Storm season starts

Atlantic hurricane season starts on June 1. Look for limbs resting on the roof, long branches over the house and cracks where big limbs join the trunk.

After a storm

Stay well away from fallen wires and hanging limbs. From a safe distance, look for leaning trunks, lifted soil at the base and split limbs, and take photos.

Cooler months

A calmer time to plan trimming. Walk round each trunk and look at the base for mushrooms, soft wood or hollows, which can point to decay.

Anything touching a power line is a job for the utility company. Never touch it.
