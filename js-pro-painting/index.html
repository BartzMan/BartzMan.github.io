SECTION 01 · THE DECK

Tap one open.

Exterior, interior, cabinets, pressure washing and drywall. Each chip says in one line what it is and when you need it.

Exterior painting

Fresh paint for the outside of your house: siding, trim, doors and the details around them. It protects and it changes how the house looks from the street.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings and trim inside the house. The quickest way to change how a room feels without moving a single piece of furniture.

Cabinet painting

Kitchen cabinets painted instead of replaced, in a color picked to suit your counters and floor. Often the biggest change for the least mess.

Pressure washing

A high-pressure rinse for driveways, siding, fences and patios. Done before painting so the paint has clean ground to grip, or just to freshen a tired surface.

Drywall

Holes, dings and cracked seams patched and smoothed in the wall itself, so the paint that follows goes on flat and clean.
