05 / Field notes

Questions, answered.

Q1We just had a storm. Should someone look at the roof?

If you can see missing or lifted shingles, granules collecting in the gutters, a dented vent or a new stain on a ceiling, it's worth a look. Stay off the roof yourself. Take photos from the ground and text them to (281) 760-8706 so we can see what you're seeing.

Q2How long does a roof replacement take?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the materials and the Houston weather. Once we've seen your roof, we'll tell you what to expect for your house before any work starts.

Q3Do you work on gutters too?

Yes. Gutters are part of what we do, along with siding and exterior painting, so the roof and the rest of the outside of the house can be handled together.

Q4What should I send when I get in touch?

Your name, the part of Houston you're in (a ZIP code is enough), what you're seeing, and a few photos if you can take them safely from the ground. A text or an email both work.

Q5Where do you work?

3RS Construction is based in northwest Houston (77064) and works on homes across Houston.
