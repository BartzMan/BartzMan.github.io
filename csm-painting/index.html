The jobscope.

Commercial exterior painting

Panel, stucco and block facades. Needed when a new building is ready for its final colour, or a storefront has gone tired.

B-2

Steel and metal painting

Canopies, stairs, gates and fences. Bare or rusty steel is cleaned, primed and coated so it holds a finish.

B-3

Interior painting

Open floors, offices and fit-outs. Walls and ceilings are coated once the other trades are clear.

AREA C / SITE PLAN

Walk thejob.

Tap an area on the plan. Each one shows a photo of that kind of work and, in general terms, what it takes: prep, primer, coating.

AREA 1 / STOREFRONT FACADE

Panel, stucco and block walls

Prep
Wash off dirt and chalk, scrape anything loose, patch cracks and seal the joints.
Primer
A primer matched to the surface: panel, stucco or masonry.
Coating
Exterior-grade coating in two coats, with glass, awnings and ground masked off.

AREA 2 / STEEL CANOPY

Beams, posts and soffits

Prep
Brush or grind off rust and loose coating, then wipe the steel clean.
Primer
A rust-inhibiting primer on bare steel, welds and edges.
Coating
A durable metal enamel, usually sprayed so the ribs and corners are covered.

AREA 3 / STEEL STAIRS

Treads, stringers and rails

Prep
Remove flaking paint and rust, scuff what is sound, and mask the brick around it.
Primer
Metal primer on every bare spot, including the underside of the treads.
Coating
Two coats of enamel, brushed into the corners where water sits.

AREA 4 / BLOCK WALL

Masonry and block

Prep
Clean and dry the wall, fill holes, and cover the fence, ground and fittings.
Primer
A block filler or masonry primer to seal the porous surface.
Coating
Masonry coating in two coats, rolled or sprayed from a lift when the wall is tall.

AREA 5 / STEEL GATE

Gates and fences

Prep
Degrease and sand the steel, and clean around hinges and latches.
Primer
A bonding metal primer, with extra on welds and edges.
Coating
Enamel in a dark finish that hides marks, applied on both faces.

AREA 6 / INTERIOR FIT-OUT

Walls, ceilings and trim

Prep
Patch and sand, clear the floor, and protect finished areas and fixtures.
Primer
A primer on new drywall and repairs, so the colour goes on evenly.
Coating
Ceilings first, then walls and trim, with a last walk-round for touch-ups.
