The prep · fine wood work

Paint is the last step. The prep is the job.

Old wood tells you everything. These garage doors were stripped back, the rotten boards cut out and patched with new wood, before a drop of paint went on. That is the fine wood work Five Towns Painting talks about on its Facebook page.

  1. 1
    Cover and protect

    Drop cloths, paper and tape over floors, furniture and anything that should stay clean.

  2. 2
    Strip and scrape

    Loose paint, old wallpaper and failing finish come off first.

  3. 3
    Patch and sand

    Holes, cracks and soft wood are filled or replaced, then sanded smooth.

  4. 4
    Prime, paint, clean up

    Primer where it is needed, the finish coats, and the room left tidy. Cleanup is one of the things reviewers mention.

Old doors, trim or railings? Text a photo to (917) 573-5935 and say what you have in mind.

Questions

Before you call.

Short answers to what people ask first. Anything else, call or text.

Q1Do you paint inside and outside?

Yes. Interior painting (walls, ceilings, trim, closets) and exterior painting, plus fine wood work on doors, trim and railings.

Q2Can you take down old wallpaper first?

Yes. Wallpaper removal is one of the services, and one of the jobs people mention in their Google reviews. The walls are prepared after the paper comes off so the new paint goes onto a clean surface.

Q3Do you paint offices and stores?

Yes. Five Towns Painting is a house painting and commercial painting company, so offices, shops and other commercial spaces are part of the work.

Q4Where do you work?

Based in Hewlett, Long Island, working across the Five Towns and Nassau County.

Q5What should I send to get a price?

A few phone photos of the rooms or the outside of the house, your town, and roughly what you want painted. Call or text (917) 573-5935, or use the email form below.
