How a roof is built

Take it apart.

Tap a layer to isolate it.

321

The finished roof

Shingles or metal panels. This is the layer you see, and it sheds rain, sun and hail so the layers beneath stay dry.

The underlayment

A water-resistant sheet over the deck. If wind pushes rain past the surface, this is the backup that keeps it out of the house.

The deck

The wood sheathing everything is fastened to. It needs to be flat and sound, or the layers above it never sit right.

Shingle or metal?

Shingle

What it is
Overlapping asphalt shingles in rows, nailed through to the deck.
How it looks
Textured and layered, in browns, greys and blacks that suit brick homes.
East Texas weather
Sheds rain and shrugs off sun. Hail and high wind can lift or bruise them.

Metal

What it is
Long steel panels that lock together in raised seams, fastened to the deck.
How it looks
Clean vertical lines in a flat or satin finish, like the grey roof above.
East Texas weather
Sheds rain fast and many finishes reflect heat. Big hail can dent panels.

Before you decide

Metal or shingles for an East Texas home: an honest comparison.

Pick what matters most to you. No roof wins everything.

Shingle

Dark shingles absorb heat. Good attic ventilation and lighter colours help.

Metal

Many finishes reflect heat, so the roof surface runs cooler in summer sun.
