I

Four strokes.

Four kinds of paint work. Tap a scroll to unroll it and read what it covers.

The rooms you live in: walls and ceilings in one even colour, with straight lines where wall meets ceiling and trim.

  • Scuffs and marks that no longer wipe off
  • Old patches showing through the colour
  • A colour you have stopped liking
Call about a room

Fronts, trim and doors on the outside of the house, where weather and sun wear paint down first.

  • Peeling or flaking on window trim
  • Faded, chalky colour on the sunny side
  • A front door that looks tired from the street
Call about the outside

Baseboards, door and window casings, doors and mantels: the edges that frame a room and take the most knocks.

  • Chipped or yellowed baseboards
  • Fingerprints around door frames
  • Trim that no longer matches fresh walls
Call about trim

Holes, dents and hairline cracks filled, sanded and primed before colour, because paint shows what is underneath.

  • Holes from shelves and TV mounts
  • Cracks above doors and windows
  • Water stains on a ceiling
Call about prep

Quiet walls.Sharp edges.

The look everyone wants from a painted room: even colour, no lap marks, and lines that stop exactly where they should.
