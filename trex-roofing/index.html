Up top · Flat roofs & leaks

Flat roofs, seam by seam.

Most of Brooklyn lives under a flat roof: rolls of black bitumen, white coatings and rubber, lapped at every seam and turned up at the parapet. That is the roof T-REX lays new, and the roof T-REX fixes when it starts to let water in.

01

Flat roof installation and repair

New flat roofs laid from the deck up, and tired ones put right: open seams closed, blisters cut out, edges and parapet turn-ups sealed again.

  • Torch-down
  • Rubber
  • Coatings
  • Parapets

02

Roof leak repairs

A stain on the ceiling usually starts somewhere else on the roof: a vent pipe, a lapped seam, a drain, the flashing at a chimney. The leak gets traced back to where it starts and patched there, like the vent pipe in the photo.

  • Seams
  • Flashing
  • Drains

The edge · Shingles & gutters

Shingles on top, gutters below.

Not every Brooklyn roof is flat. Where it pitches, it is usually asphalt shingle, and everything a shingle roof sheds lands in the gutter at the eave. T-REX does both halves of that job.

03

Shingle roofing

Shingle roofs put on and patched, course by course, and tied into the flat roof next door. The joint where a pitched roof meets a flat one gets its own care.

  • Courses
  • Ridges
  • Valleys
  • Flat-to-pitch joints
04

Gutters and gutter guards

Gutters hung to run toward the leader, and guards fitted over them so leaves from Brooklyn’s street trees stay out and the water keeps moving.

  • Leaders
