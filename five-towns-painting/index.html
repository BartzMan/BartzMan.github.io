Inside and out

Two sides
of the same job.

Interior

Walls, ceilings, trim, and the small woodwork that decides whether a room looks finished. Closets get painted like rooms, not like cupboards.

Exterior

Siding, trim, shutters and the parts of a house only a ladder reaches. A chimney is a different job from a wall and it gets treated like one.

The prep

Most of a paint job happens before the paint arrives.

Wallpaper comes off, loose paint gets scraped and sanded, and anything that has failed underneath gets made good before a single coat goes on. It is the least interesting part of the job and the only part that decides whether the last part looks right.

Fine wood work sits in the middle of it: doors, casings, mouldings and the built-in trim that makes a centre-hall house what it is. On a house like that, the woodwork is the reason you hired a painter.
