Palms / frond by frond

Leave
the green.

A palm trim is not a haircut. The dead and hanging fronds come off, the seed pods and flower stalks come off, and the green crown that feeds the palm stays on. That is what a palm looks like when it is ready for the season.

  • Green fronds stay on
  • Dead, brown and hanging fronds off
  • Seed pods and flower stalks off

Questions / 4

Before
you call.

01When should I get my palms trimmed?

Before the season is the smart time. The Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so spring is when most people in Hialeah and Miami get dead fronds and seed pods taken off. Call or text and we will set a day.

02I have a tree leaning toward the house. Can you take it down?

Tree removal is one of the four jobs we do. Send a photo of the whole tree and where it is leaning, and we will tell you how we would take it down and when we can come.

03What happens to the stump after a removal?

Ask for stump grinding with the removal. The grinder cuts the stump down into chips and soil so the spot can be levelled and the grass can come back.

04What should I text you for a quote?

A photo of the tree from far enough back to see all of it, one closer photo, your city or ZIP, and one line on what you want done: trim, palm cleanup, removal or stump.
