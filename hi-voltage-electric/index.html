Questions, switched on

Can you add recessed lights to a ceiling that's already finished?

Often, yes. Recessed lights (hi-hats) can usually go into an existing ceiling through the openings for the lights themselves, depending on the joists and what's above. Send a photo of the room and how many lights you have in mind.

Do you do outdoor and landscape lighting?

Yes: step lights, path and garden lights, and lighting for outdoor kitchens and bars. Outdoor work uses weather-rated fixtures on protected outdoor circuits.

How do I know if my panel needs work?

Breakers that trip often, no space left for new circuits, an old fuse box, or plans for a big new load like a pool or an addition are all reasons to have it looked at. Send a photo of the panel with the door open.

What should I send with my message?

Photos of the area, the fixture you want changed or the panel with its door open, plus your town. Text them to (917) 439-7985 or email hivoltage65@gmail.com.
