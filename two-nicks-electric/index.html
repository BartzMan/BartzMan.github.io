CKT 01

Standby generators

A new unit set on a pad and wired through its transfer switch and meter, so the house picks up where the utility leaves off. No extension cords across the driveway.

CKT 02

Electrical panels

New panels and panel work, mounted square with clean knockouts and doors that close the way they should. Every circuit labelled so nobody guesses in the dark.

CKT 03

Service masts & meters

Mast, meter and disconnect replacements on brick and siding: riser strapped straight, seals watertight, the service path from the street to the panel rebuilt where it has aged out.

CKT 04

High-bay lighting

High-bay LED layouts for shops, garages and warehouses, row spacing planned from the floor so the light lands where people and machines actually work.

CKT 05

Kitchen & recessed

Under-cabinet strips, recessed lights and dimmers placed where the work happens (counters, islands, sinks) and wired so every switch lands within reach of the door.

CKT 06

Dock & pavilion

Dock lights, pavilion fans and recessed wood ceilings: the outdoor rooms people gather in after dark, wired and switched from the house.
