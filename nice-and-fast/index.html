What we do

Five lanes.Pick yours.

Hover or tap the plus on a lane to read more. Whichever one your roof is stuck in, call and tell us what it is doing.

01

Shingle roofs

Leaks, worn shingles and whole roofs on Brooklyn's rowhouses and two-families.

Tell us where the water shows up and we start from there. Photos of the ceiling or the roof help.

Own photo: house with ladders up
02

Flat roofs

The flat roofs that cover so much of the borough, and the seams and edges where water gets in.

Flat roofs are all about the details: seams, edges, curbs and drains.

Illustrative stock photo
03

Skylights

Skylights set into flat and pitched roofs, and the flashing that keeps them dry.

A skylight is only as good as the roof around it. We look at both.

Own photo: skylight on a flat roof
04

Drains & flashing

Roof drains and the flashing around them, along vents and up walls: small parts that cause big stains.

If water sits or stains show up under a wall or a vent, this is usually where we look first.

Own photo: working at a roof drain
05

Gutters & downspouts

Gutters, leaders and downspouts that carry water away from the wall and off the sidewalk.

Where the water goes after it leaves the roof matters just as much.

Own photo: downspout at the back of a house
