North Texas homeowner checklist

After the hail passes.

Once the storm has passed, stay on the ground and make a short record of what you can see.

Gutters and downspouts

Look for fresh dents, bends or sections that have pulled away.

Vents and roof edges

Note visible dents on metal vents or trim.

Granules on the ground

Look for unusual piles of shingle grit near the outlet of a downspout.

Dated photos

Take a wide photo and a closer view from the ground. Keep the date and location of each observation.
