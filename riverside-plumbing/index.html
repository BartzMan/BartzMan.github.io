Before the freeze, look at these.

Tap each one to open it. General home advice for cold Ohio nights. If a pipe does freeze or burst, shut off the water at the main and call a plumber.

1Find your main shut-off

Know where the main water shut-off valve is, and make sure you can reach it. If a pipe ever bursts, closing it fast limits the damage.

2Unhook the garden hoses

Disconnect hoses from outdoor spigots and let them drain before the first hard freeze, so water does not sit in the pipe behind the wall.

3Check pipes in cold spots

Look at pipes in unheated places like crawl spaces, garages and outside walls. Wrapping them in pipe insulation helps keep them from freezing.

4Open the cabinet doors

On a very cold night, opening the cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls lets warm room air reach the pipes.

5Let a faucet drip

When it is bitterly cold, a slow drip from a faucet on an outside wall can help keep water moving through the pipe.
