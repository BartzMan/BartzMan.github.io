Drips, wobbles and worn handles.

Copper lines, joined back up.

Find your main shut-off before you need it

Look for the wall where the water line comes into the house. It is often in a basement or utility room, on the side facing the street. The water meter is usually close by.

Follow the pipe from the meter. The main shut-off valve is usually just inside the wall, with a round wheel handle or a straight lever.

Turn it a little and make sure it moves, then hang a tag on it so anyone in the house can find it. If a pipe is leaking, shut it off and call a plumber.
