How do I ask for an estimate?

Call or send a message with the issue, the equipment involved and your neighborhood. Ask what information is needed to discuss an estimate.

What if I smell gas or have an urgent problem?

If you smell gas, suspect carbon monoxide or see smoke, leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. For a leak or loss of heat, avoid hazards and seek appropriate help.

Which area can I ask about?

Ask about service in Red Hook, Brooklyn. Contact the business to confirm the location and scope of your request.

What should I put in a message?

Include your name, a way to reach you, your neighborhood and a short description of the issue. You can also text us a clear photo of the equipment. Avoid sensitive personal information.
