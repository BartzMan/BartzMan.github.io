Questions

Before we talk.

How do I ask for a quote?

Call (404) 237-5556 or use the request form with your name, phone, email and a short description of the job. Say whether it is plumbing, heating or air conditioning.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Yes. Tuxedo handles residential and commercial plumbing, heating and air conditioning: installation, repair and maintenance.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the building straight away, do not switch anything on or off, and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Call a contractor once the utility says it is safe.

Which area do you cover?

Tuxedo is based in Chamblee (30341), north-east Atlanta. Ask about your address when you call or send the form.

What should I have ready?

The kind of fixture or system, what you have noticed, roughly when it started, and a good time to reach you. A photo of the equipment label helps with heating and cooling questions.
