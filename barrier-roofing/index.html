BAY 2 SERVICES

BAY 2

Who is the roof for?

You own the building, and the roof is what keeps everything else under it dry. It pays to know what the roof needs.

01

Roof coatings

White reflective coating systems

A white reflective coating system is applied over a flat roof and seals the surface. It is one way to deal with an aging flat roof.

02

Commercial flat roofs

Shops, offices and warehouses usually have flat or low-slope roofs. They drain slowly, so water and seams are what to watch.

03

Metal building roofs

Metal roofs on warehouses and workshops are tough, but fasteners, laps and sealant still need a look from time to time.

04

Roof repair and leak repair

A leak shows up inside long after it started outside. Repair means finding the real way in, then sealing it.
