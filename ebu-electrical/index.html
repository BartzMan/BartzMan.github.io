Before you call

Breaker keeps tripping?
3 things to know.

Circuit 7Kitchen
ONTRIPPEDOFF

Unplug first.

A breaker trips when a circuit is asked to carry more than it should. Unplug or switch off what is running on that circuit. Space heaters, kettles, hair dryers and microwaves sharing one line are common causes.

Reset once.

Push the handle all the way to OFF, then back to ON. If it holds, note what was running and use less on that circuit. If it trips again right away, stop and leave it off.

Then call.

A breaker that keeps tripping, feels hot, buzzes, smells burnt or shows scorch marks needs an electrician. Leave it off and do not open the panel cover yourself. Smoke or a burning smell: leave the building and call 911.
