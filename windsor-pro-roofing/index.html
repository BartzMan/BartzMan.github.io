before you choose

Shingle, metal or flat: which roof suits which building?

Roofs are chosen by the building, not the other way round. Pick the kind of building you have, and read the plain pros and cons.

Usually a fit

Shingle roof

Shingles are the usual choice on a pitched roof: plenty of colours, and a section can be repaired without redoing the whole roof.

Also consider
Metal is also an option on a steep roof and sheds rain quickly.
Keep in mind
Check any HOA rules and ask how the attic will be ventilated before choosing a colour.

Usually a fit

Flat roof membrane

A single-ply membrane is made for roofs that are almost level, with the seams welded or sealed.

Also consider
Shingles need a real slope to shed water, so they are not the answer here.
Keep in mind
Water has to drain somewhere. After rain, look for ponds that stay and seams that have opened.

Usually a fit

Metal roof

Long metal panels can run in one piece from the eave to the ridge, which suits big, simple roof planes.

Also consider
Shingles work too if the pitch is steep enough and the roof is not very large.
Keep in mind
Ask about how the panels handle heat expanding them and how rain will sound inside.

Usually a fit

Patio cover

A patio cover turns the patio into a room: shade in summer and shelter when it rains.

Also consider
Skylights let light in so the space under it does not feel dark.
Keep in mind
Make sure rain runs off toward a gutter, not onto the house wall, and that it matches the main roof.
