The playbook

Four plays we run every week around San Jose. Tap a play — the routes draw themselves and the details open up.

Outlets dead, breakers tripping, lights acting up

We trace the line to the actual fault instead of swapping parts until something holds. When we're done we test it in front of you, so you see the play work before we pack up.

  • Dead outlets and switches
  • Breakers that trip and won't reset
  • Flickering you can't explain
  • A faceplate that runs warm or smells burnt

An old panel is a bottleneck on every circuit you own

If the board is warm, crowded, or cobbled together out of doubled-up breakers, it's holding the whole house back. We clear the drive: a clean, modern layout with room for the circuits you actually use.

  • A panel that's warm to the touch
  • Doubled-up breakers and open slots
  • No room for the new circuits you want
  • Flicker that moves around the house

Rooms that read, porches that glow, fans that hold still

Fixtures, recessed cans, dimmers, ceiling fans on pitched patio roofs — mounted straight, switched where your hand expects it, and balanced so nothing wobbles or hums at night.

  • Recessed and surface fixtures, inside and out
  • Ceiling fans — new mounts and wobble fixes
  • Dimmers, timers and three-way switches
  • Under-cabinet and landscape lighting runs

New circuits and new outlets, from the panel out

Remodel wiring, dedicated circuits, outlets where the furniture actually sits, hookups for the heavy appliances. We pull the runs clean, label the panel, and leave the walls ready for whoever closes them up.

  • New outlets and dedicated circuits
  • Wiring for remodels and additions
  • Appliance and equipment hookups
  • Panel labeling so the next visit is quick
