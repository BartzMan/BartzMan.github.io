10:00 PMQuestions

Asked after hours.

Four things people tend to wonder about once the lights are off. Anything else: call or text during the day.

01The same breaker keeps tripping. What now?

A breaker that trips again and again is telling you something: too much on one circuit, a worn breaker, or a fault somewhere on the line. Resetting it over and over is not the fix. Note which outlets and lights go dead, then call or text and describe it.

02Can recessed lights go into a room that is already finished?

Often, yes. Recessed and kitchen lighting can be added to rooms that are already drywalled, with the layout planned around the joists and the switch where you want it. A photo of the ceiling and the room helps.

03What do outdoor timers actually do?

A timer box on the outside wall switches an outdoor circuit on and off by itself, for landscape lights, a pool area or a fountain. If the timer has stopped keeping time or the circuit behind it acts up, that is part of the job list.

04What should I send when I text?

A photo of the panel with the door open, a photo of the outlet, light or box in question, and your town: Holiday, or wherever you are in Pasco or Pinellas.
