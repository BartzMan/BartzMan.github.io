Privacy policy.

This page explains what happens to information you share with Tuxedo Mechanical & Plumbing through this website, by phone, by text or by email.

What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, the details you enter (your name, contact details and message) are sent to us so we can reply to you. If you call, text or email, we receive your number or address and whatever you choose to tell us about the job.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer your request, discuss plumbing, heating or air conditioning work, arrange a visit and keep a record of the job. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Your details are seen by the people at Tuxedo who handle your request. They may also pass through the services that run this website and our phone and email, which process them on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job records only as long as we need them for the work, for follow-up questions and for our normal business records, then delete them.

Cookies and visit counts

This website does not use advertising cookies. A simple visit counter may record that the page was opened (time zone, device type and the referring site), without names or contact details.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at (404) 237-5556 or whuckabee@bellsouth.net.

Please do not send

Please do not put payment card numbers, passwords or other sensitive personal information in the request form or in messages.
