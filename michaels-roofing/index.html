Seasonal check

Gutters: a 2-minute look

  • Leaves or debris sticking up out of the gutter
  • Sagging sections or gaps at the seams
  • Water marks or stains on the siding below
  • Downspouts clear and pointing away from the house
  • Shingle grit collecting in the gutter
  • Ice hanging at the eaves in winter

05 / Field notes

Questions, answered.

Q1We just had a storm. What should I look for on the roof?

Missing or lifted shingles, granules collecting in the gutters, a dented vent or a new stain on a ceiling are all worth a look. Stay off the roof yourself. Take photos from the ground and text them to (614) 563-0822.

Q2How long does a roof replacement take?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the materials and the weather. Ask about your own roof when you get in touch.

Q3Do you work on gutters too?

Yes. Gutters are part of the name and part of the work, along with roofing and copper flashing, so the roof and the roof edge can be handled together.

Q4What should I send when I get in touch?

Your name, what you're seeing, and a few photos if you can take them safely from the ground. A text or an email both work.

Q5Where do you work?

Michael's Roofing & Gutter is based in Centerburg, Ohio (43011).
