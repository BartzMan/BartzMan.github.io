Short answers to the things people ask first. Anything else, send it on WhatsApp.

Q1My breaker keeps tripping. What does that mean?

A breaker switches off when the circuit behind it is asked for more than it should carry, or when something on that circuit has a fault. Note which breaker it is and what was running when it went off, then send that to us and we'll take a look.

Q2Can you add outlets where I need them?

Yes. Adding outlets is electrical installation work, and that's one of our services. Tell us the room and roughly where you want them. A photo of the wall helps.

Q3Do you do commercial electrical work?

Yes, commercial electrical work is one of our five services. Send us the kind of building, the part of town and what you need, and we can talk it through.

Q4What should I send on WhatsApp?

A photo of your breaker panel with the door open, a photo of the problem (the outlet, the light, the spot on the wall), your neighborhood, and one line about what's happening.

Q5Where do you work?

We're in south-east Houston and work in Houston and nearby. Not sure you're close enough? Just ask.
