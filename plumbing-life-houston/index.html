Before you call

Hidden leak? The meter test.

Stop every tap.

Turn off every tap and make sure the washing machine, dishwasher and ice maker are not running. Ask everyone in the house to hold off on flushing.

Find and read the meter.

The meter box is usually set in the ground near the street. Lift the lid and look; keep your fingers out. Note the numbers, and find the small dial or triangle that spins when any water moves.

Wait. Then look again.

Give it thirty minutes without using water, then check the meter again. Did the numbers change, or is the small dial still turning? Choose what you see.

Good sign: no hidden water movement showed up in this test. If you still see damp spots, stains or a drip, say so when you call.
Water is moving somewhere. If you can find the main shut-off valve at the house, turn it off. Then call a plumber. (281) 961-5718
