Air starts at the unit, travels the ducts and comes out of a register in your room. When a room is too hot or too cold, the fault is somewhere along that line. These are the parts Weber Air works on.

Texas summer: why one room is always hot.

Five common causes and what fixes them. Work through the list; tick each one you’ve ruled out.

0 of 5 ruled out

  1. A clogged filter chokes airflow to every room, and the room at the end of the longest run feels it first.

    Fix: replace it on schedule.

  2. A rug, a sofa or a shut louver stops the air in the last foot of the run.

    Fix: open it fully and clear the space in front.

  3. West-facing windows can heat a room faster than the system cools it.

    Fix: blinds or curtains through the afternoon.

  4. Air that can’t get back to the unit slows down coming in.

    Fix: test a day with the door open; a pro can add a return path.

  5. A kinked flex run or a loose boot in the attic spills the air before it reaches the room.

    Fix: have the run checked by an HVAC tech.
