New construction wiring

What it is The wiring, outlets, lighting circuits and panel for a house that is being built or taken back to the studs.

When you need it Before the walls are closed up, so the layout of outlets and lights is decided early.

Panel upgrades

What it is Replacing the main electrical panel, the breaker box, with a newer and often larger one.

When you need it When the panel is old, full, uses fuses, or you are adding big loads such as a heat pump.

Meter & service

What it is The meter socket and the service cable that carry power from the street to your panel.

When you need it When a bigger panel needs a bigger service, or the meter box or mast is worn or loose.

Lighting

What it is Recessed lights, switches, dimmers and fixtures, placed where you actually use the room.

When you need it During a remodel, or when a room is too dark or a fixture flickers.

EV-ready circuits

What it is A dedicated circuit and a spot for a car charger, after checking the panel has room for it.

When you need it Before you buy or collect an electric car.
