Inside the building.

Tap a numbered fixture to see what it does and where it fits.

Window unit

A window unit can cool one room where the opening, support and building rules allow it.

Ductless head

A wall-mounted indoor unit can serve a room without existing ductwork. The outdoor unit and drain route still need a suitable location.

Steam radiator

Steam radiators receive heat from a boiler system. In an apartment, note whether other rooms or neighbors have similar symptoms before speaking with building management.

Thermostat

A thermostat reads the temperature where it is mounted. One room can feel different if the thermostat is in a warmer or cooler part of the home.

Heating service looks at why equipment does not start, does not produce enough heat or leaves some rooms cooler.

When rooms hold heat

Air conditioning service looks at weak airflow, uneven temperatures or equipment that runs without cooling the room.

Before planning ductless AC

Ask the
building first.

Before choosing a spot for an indoor unit, ask management or the owner how alterations are handled.

What needs permission?

Ask what paperwork the building requires for equipment, wall openings or work in shared areas.

Where can the outdoor unit go?

Check which exterior locations and routes the building allows. Ask how exterior access is handled.

Where can the lines and drain run?

Share the proposed path for the lines, wiring and drain before work is planned, especially if it crosses a shared area.
