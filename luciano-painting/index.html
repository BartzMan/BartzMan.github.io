Homeowner prep guide

A finish starts underneath.

Check the surface

Dust, chalk and grime get between paint and the wall. Start by noting the material and any loose or damaged areas.

Match cleaning to the wall

Different surfaces need different cleaning. Stucco and older coatings can be damaged by too much pressure.

Let it dry

Paint adheres better to a clean, dry surface. Give the wall time to dry and check what the coating calls for before painting.

Painting and washing

Pick the surface.

Interior and exterior painting

Interior paint changes the color and finish of rooms. Exterior paint renews the coating on outdoor surfaces.

Pressure washing

Pressure washing removes built-up grime from suitable exterior surfaces before refinishing or as a standalone cleaning task.
