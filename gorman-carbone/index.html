Four ways to keep dry.

A house meets the weather in four places. Here is what each job is, and when you need it.

Roofing

Tear-offs and new roofs. You need one when shingles curl, crack or go missing, or when the ceiling shows a stain after rain.

Siding

The outside wall of the house. New siding is worth a look when boards are loose, warped or letting rain in behind them.

Windows

Replacement windows for panes that fog, frames that rot or seals that let a draught through.

Gutters

The channels that carry rain off the roof and away from the walls. Look at them when water spills over the edge or the fascia looks stained.

Roof

The roof takes the sun, the rain and the snow, so it is the part of the house that tires first. Dormers, valleys and edges are where water likes to get in, so that is where a roofer looks first.

Tear-off

The old shingles come off down to the wood deck. The deck gets a look, repairs where it needs them, then new underlayment and shingles go on. It is the cleanest way to see what is under there.
