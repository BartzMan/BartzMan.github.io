Four irons. One finish.

Every ranch has a set of irons, one for each mark. These are the four jobs in Ranchero Painting’s own photos. Pick an iron to see the work it stands for.

Not sure which iron? Read the wall first: five checks from the ground.

Exterior walls & stucco

The outside of the house: stucco, block and siding, brought back to one even colour. Worth a call when the sunny side has faded, the wall leaves chalk on your hand, or paint has started to lift.

  • Stucco and block walls
  • Chimney chases and garage fronts
  • Colour changes, light to dark or back
Ask about exterior walls

Trim, fascia & accents

The frames around doors and windows, the fascia boards under the roof edge, and the bands painted in a second colour. Small areas, but they are what make a plain wall look finished.

  • Window and garage surrounds
  • Fascia and roof-edge boards
  • Contrast colours that tie the house together
Ask about trim work

Interior rooms

Walls, ceilings and trim inside the house. In this dining room the table, chairs and cabinet were wrapped in plastic and taped down before a brush came out, so the room stays usable around the work.

  • Living, dining and bedrooms
  • Ceilings and baseboards
  • Furniture wrapped, floors covered
Ask about a room

Fences, gates & wood

Wood fences, gates and enclosures, painted or stained. Desert sun turns bare wood grey and splits it; a fresh coat brings the colour back and keeps the boards from drying out as fast.

  • Plank fences and gates
  • Trash and equipment enclosures
  • Wood beams and pergola parts
