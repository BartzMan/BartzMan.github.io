Homeowner checklist

Vinyl siding wear signs.

Walk around the house in daylight and compare sides. These four clues can help you describe what you’re seeing before discussing replacement.

Warping

Look along the siding from the ground. Buckled sections or rippling that interrupts otherwise straight rows are worth pointing out.

Loose panels

Look for edges that have come apart or rows that no longer meet evenly.

Uneven fading

Compare sunny and shaded walls. Fading alone is usually cosmetic.

Signs of moisture

Persistent staining or softness around openings warrants a closer look by a professional.
