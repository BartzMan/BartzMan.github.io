What happens on a roof visit,
sorted into four stops.

Every visit runs the same route, and you can read it from the ground: what gets looked at, in what order, and why. Tap a stop to open its card.

POSTCARD · THE WALL UNDER THE ROOF · READ FROM THE YARD

PARCEL 1 OF 4 · FROM THE GROUND ONLY

The address

We read the roof from the ground first: slopes, tree shade, and where the water goes after rain. Nothing needs a climb to spot the obvious.

PARCEL 2 OF 4 · THE SEAMS

The sort

Pipes, vents, flashing and valleys get sorted one by one. Those are the seams where most leaks start, so each one gets its own look.

PARCEL 3 OF 4 · ROW BY ROW

The window

Seams, shingles and fasteners get checked row by row from the ridge down. Loose, lifted or missing pieces show up here before they show up inside.

PARCEL 4 OF 4 · IN PLAIN WORDS

Handle with care

What we saw gets written down in plain words with photos, so you can see the same spots. Then you decide what to do next — no pressure either way.
