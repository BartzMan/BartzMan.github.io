RTU-1

A rooftop unit is a self-contained heating and cooling box that sits on the roof. It frees up floor space inside, and the air travels down to the rooms through ductwork.

Type
Rooftop unit
Where
On the roof, on a curb
Does
Heats and cools a whole space

CU-1–12

A condenser is the outdoor half of a cooling system, the part with the big fan that gets rid of heat. On a larger building they sit side by side in a row, so each zone can have its own.

Type
Condenser
Where
Outdoors, in a row
Does
Sheds heat from the cooling cycle

DUCT-A

Ductwork is the sheet metal that carries cooled or heated air from the unit to where it is needed. Sealed joints mean the air you paid to condition ends up in the room, not in the ceiling.

Type
Sheet metal duct
Where
Roof, ceiling, chase
Does
Moves air from unit to room

SG-1

A supply grille is where conditioned air enters a room. The adjustable louvres let you aim the air along the ceiling or wall instead of straight at people.

Type
Supply grille
Where
High on a wall
Does
Delivers and aims the air

SG-2

A ceiling diffuser throws air out in several directions at once. That mixes the air in the room evenly and avoids a cold draught in one spot.

Type
Ceiling diffuser
Where
In the ceiling
Does
Spreads air across the room

Sheet M-201 · Scope of work

What goes
on the plan

Sheet2 of 5
ScaleNTS
RTU-1

Rooftop unit installs

One box on the roof heats and cools a whole floor or shop. You need one when a building outgrows window units or wants the equipment out of the way.

CU-1–12

Multi-unit condensers

Several outdoor units set in a neat row, one for each zone. Useful for apartment buildings and offices with many separate spaces.

DUCT-A

Sheet metal ductwork

Custom-formed metal duct that carries air from the unit to the room. You need it when a new unit has to connect to the space it serves.

SG-1

Residential air distribution

The grilles and registers that deliver air to each room. If one room is always warmer or colder than the rest, this is usually where to look.

DUCT-A

Ductwork for homes

The metal runs inside walls and ceilings that connect the system to every room. It matters when you add, move or replace equipment.

SG-2

Grilles and diffusers

The visible faces of the system, from wall grilles to ceiling diffusers. They decide how the air feels in the room.
