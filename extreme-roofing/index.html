Fix it.
Or start over.

Two ways a roof gets work, and one way to know which. Pick a side to see what each job is and when you need it.

R-01 · REPAIR

Roof repair

Fixing the part that is hurting: lifted or missing shingles, a leak that keeps coming back, flashing that has let go. Reach for it when the roof is mostly sound but one spot is not.

R-02 · STORM

Storm damage repairs

When wind or a fallen limb has torn into the roof. You need it after a big blow, when shingles are in the yard or water is coming through.

R-03 · INSPECT

Roof inspections

A close look at the whole roof before you decide anything. You need one after a storm, before you buy or sell, or whenever you simply do not know what shape the roof is in.

P-01 · REPLACE

Roof replacement

The whole roof comes off and a new one goes on. It is for roofs that are worn out across the board, where patching one spot would only chase the next.

P-02 · DECIDE

Roof inspections

Repair or replace? An inspection is how you find out, before spending on either.

    03 · Useful, whoever you call

    After a Jacksonville storm:
    5 roof checks from the ground.

    Once the wind drops, you can learn a lot without leaving the yard. Walk around the house, look up, and tick off what you see. Binoculars help. Everything here is from the ground: never climb onto a wet roof.

    Tick each check as you do it and the wind on the gauge falls to calm. Spotted something? Take a photo, keep off the roof and call a roofer.
