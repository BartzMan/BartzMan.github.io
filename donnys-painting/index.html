Exterior paint needs the right weather to dry and last. Tap a month to see how it looks in the Hudson Valley. Inside work can happen in any month.

The window opens

Mild days and drying surfaces. Late spring is a prime time to start outside.

Dew point, in plain words

When the surface is within about 5°F of the dew point, moisture can form on it and spoil fresh paint. Wait for the morning dew to burn off before you start, and stop early enough that the paint can set before the evening dew.
