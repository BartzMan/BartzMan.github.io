05 Questions

Asked from the ground.

Q1How often do palms need trimming?

It depends on the kind of palm and how fast it is growing. Send us a photo of yours and we will tell you whether it is due now or can wait.

Q2Can you take down a dead tree?

Tree removal is one of our services. Send a photo that shows the whole tree and what is around it, like the house, a wall or wires, and we will talk it through with you.

Q3Is clean-up part of the job?

Yard clean-up is one of our services. Mention it when you message us so it is part of your quote from the start.

Q4What should I send to get a quote?

A photo of the tree, taken from far enough back to show it from the ground to the top, plus your part of Las Vegas. That is usually enough to get started.
