THE INFINITY RUN — ONE LOOP, EVERY CIRCUIT BACK TO THE PANEL

TUBE 01 · PANELS & BREAKERS

The panel runs the whole wall

Every circuit in the house reports to one board. When it's undersized or unlabeled, everything downstream misbehaves. We size panels for the load you actually carry, swap tired breakers, and add sub-panels so the garage stops borrowing from the kitchen.

  • Service panels & upgrades — room for the real load
  • Breaker work — swaps, additions, labels you can read
  • Sub-panels — power where the house grew, not where it used to be

TUBE 02 · WIRING & REWIRING

Old runs out, clean runs in

Brooklyn houses keep their history in the walls — and some of it hums. We replace worn-out runs, wire remodels and additions end to end, and trace the dead or buzzing circuits nobody else wants to chase.

  • Whole-home & partial rewires — planned around your rooms, not through them
  • Remodel & addition wiring — roughed in, trimmed out, tested under load
  • Repairs — the dead outlet, the buzzing switch, the breaker that won't stay on

TUBE 03 · LIGHTING & FIXTURES

Light where you actually stand

A fixture hung crooked or a dimmer that buzzes gets noticed every single day. We hang and wire fixtures level, run recessed and under-cabinet circuits, and put switches where your hand expects them.

  • Fixtures — chandeliers, pendants, fans: hung level, wired right
  • Recessed & under-cabinet runs — even light, no hot spots
  • Dimmers & switches — quiet ones, in the right spots

TUBE 04 · OUTLETS, EV & NEW CIRCUITS

Power where the car parks

The newest loads in an old city: an EV that charges overnight, a dryer that needs its own line, outlets that don't spark. We run dedicated circuits from the panel to the point of use and leave room for the next one.

  • EV charger circuits — dedicated, run to where the car actually parks
  • Outlets & receptacles — added, moved, replaced
  • Dedicated circuits — sized for dryers, ranges and whatever you bring home next
