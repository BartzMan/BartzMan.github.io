How to read this panel

01
Circuit number. Odd numbers run down the left side of a panel, even down the right.
20A
Breaker size in amps: how much the circuit can carry before it trips.
1P / 2P
Single or double pole. Big loads like a range or a meter feed take two.
SPARE
A position with nothing on it. Flip the switch to see which circuits go live for buildings.

  1. 0120A · 1P

    Panels & breakers

    The panel is where every circuit in your home starts. It needs attention when breakers trip often, feel warm, or the panel is old and out of spaces.

    SPARE

  2. 0315A · 1P

    Wiring & rewiring

    Older apartments often still run on their original wiring. Rewiring means opening the walls in neat lines to run new cable to outlets and lights.

    SPARE

  3. 0515A · 1P

    Chandeliers & fixtures

    A heavy chandelier or a new fixture needs a box rated for its weight and solid support above the ceiling, so it hangs straight and stays put.

    SPAREHomes & apartments

  4. 0720A · GFCI

    Outlets, switches & dimmers

    Swapping two-prong outlets, adding GFCI protection in kitchens and baths, or putting the dining-room light on a dimmer.

    SPAREHomes & apartments

  1. 02100A · 2P

    Meters & service equipment

    The meter socket and disconnect are where power enters the house. They need work when you add load, or when old equipment is worn or damaged.

    SPARE

  2. 0420A · 1P

    Office & commercial lighting

    Linear LED and recessed lights for offices, conference rooms and stores, laid out to light the work below and not just the ceiling.

    SPAREBuildings & businesses

  3. 0615A · 1P

    Exterior & entrance lighting

    Porch, stoop and backyard lights on switches, timers or motion sensors, so you can see the steps and the lock at night.

    SPARE

  4. 08TEST

    Dead circuits & troubleshooting

    When an outlet or a whole room goes dead and resetting the breaker does nothing, the fault has to be traced along the circuit.

    SPARE

Which breaker is it?

Tap the symptom.

Pick what you’re seeing at home. The circuit it usually lives on lights up, with what it tends to mean, what’s safe to check yourself and when to call. General advice only: every panel is wired a little differently.

CKT 05Living-room lights

Usually means
One lamp flickering is usually the bulb or a worn switch. Lights flickering in several rooms at once can point to a loose connection in the panel or where power comes in.
Safe to check
Tighten or swap the bulb. Notice whether it happens when something big starts up, like the A/C or the microwave.
Call when
It happens in more than one room, lights dim hard when appliances start, or you hear buzzing from a switch or the panel.
