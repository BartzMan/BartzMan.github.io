New construction & remodels

Rough-in. Trim-out. Lights on.

On a new build or a remodel, the wiring goes in twice: once while the walls are open, and again after paint, when the devices and fixtures go on. Plan the first visit well and the second one is the easy part.

  1. 01

    Rough-in

    Boxes, cable and circuits go in while the studs are bare, before insulation and drywall.

  2. 02

    Trim-out

    After the walls are finished and painted: outlets, switches, cover plates and light fixtures go on.

  3. 03

    Lights on

    The wire disappears behind the trim. What is left is a room that works: lights, fans and outlets where you use them.

Remodel or new build? Call before the drywall goes up.
