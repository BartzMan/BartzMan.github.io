01 · What we do

The whole tree, top to root.

Everything on this list is done by the same crew with the same truck. One call covers the climb, the cut, the stump and the cleanup.

01

Tree trimming & pruning

Deadwood out, limbs lifted off the roof and the driveway, canopy shaped so the tree stays strong through Gulf-Coast wind.

02

Tree removal

Dead, leaning or in-the-way trees taken down in sections with ropes and rigging, including tight spots between houses and fences.

03

Stump grinding

Stump ground below grade and the chips raked flat, so you can lay sod or plant again where the tree stood.

04

Storm cleanup

After a Houston storm: broken limbs cut off the house, cracked trees made safe, and the yard cleared of debris.

05

Crown raising & thinning

More light into the yard and the house, less weight on long limbs, better airflow through the canopy.

06

Debris chipping & hauling

Brush goes through the chipper, logs go on the truck. Your lawn is raked when we leave.

07

Lot & fence-line clearing

Overgrown lots, fence lines and back corners cleared of small trees, vines and brush.

08

Palm trimming

Dead fronds and seed pods removed from palms, with the trunk cleaned up and tidy.

02 · How a job goes

Four steps, no surprises.

From your first call to the last rake stroke, this is what to expect.

1

Call or send details

Tell us what the tree is doing and where it stands. A photo from your phone helps.

2

We walk the yard

We look at the tree, the drop zone, the fences and the lines, then give you an estimate in writing.

3

Crew day

Ropes, rigging and saws. Limbs are lowered, not dropped, when there is something underneath.

4

Chip, haul, rake

Brush is chipped, wood is loaded, the lawn is raked and the driveway is blown clean.

05 · How we cut

Rope it down, never drop it.

Houston lots are tight: fences, pools, AC units and the neighbor's roof are all in the drop zone. So we climb, rig and lower limbs in sections instead of felling trees whole.

  • Climbers with ropes and rigging for tight spaces
  • Proper collar cuts so the tree heals clean
  • Chipper on site, so brush never piles up at the curb
  • Lawn raked and driveway blown before the truck leaves
