Before you choose

Metal roof in North Texas: 6 questions to ask.

Good questions for any metal roof, whoever puts it on. Tap a panel to open it, then tick it off.

0 of 6 asked

  1. Standing seam, stone-coated steel or R-panel. Each suits a different roof shape and house style.

  2. Exposed screws go through the panel face. Hidden systems clip the panel down under the seam. Ask which you’re getting.

  3. Ask what goes under the metal. It’s the layer that sheds any water that gets past the panels.

  4. Lighter, reflective finishes take on less heat in a Texas summer. Ask how your colour behaves in the sun.

  5. North Texas gets hail. Ask how the panel you like handles impact, and how a dent would show on that finish.

  6. Ask who handles gutters, drip edge, flashing and trim, and whether they’ll match the panel colour.
