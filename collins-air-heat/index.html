System map

Seven spots where the trouble starts.

A typical Richmond home runs a split system: an outdoor unit on the pad, an air handler in the attic, and ducts that carry the air to every room. Here is where a call usually begins.

Before you call: three quick checks

  • ThermostatSet to Cool or Heat, a few degrees past the room, with fresh batteries.
  • BreakersIndoor and outdoor units both on. If one trips again, leave it off and call.
  • FilterNot caked with dust. A choked filter can ice up a coil.
  1. ThermostatBlank screen, the wrong reading, or a system that ignores a new setting.
  2. Air handler and coilIce on the coil, weak airflow, a blower that hums or won't start.
  3. Supply ductsRooms that never cool, crushed or loose flex runs in a hot attic.
  4. Return and filterClogged filters, a whistling return grille, dust that builds up fast.
  5. Refrigerant linesFrost on the lines, a hiss at the unit, warm air at the vents.
  6. Outdoor unitFan not spinning, clicking with no start, a unit that runs and runs.
  7. Condensate drainWater in the pan, a wet spot on the ceiling, a float switch that shuts it all down.
