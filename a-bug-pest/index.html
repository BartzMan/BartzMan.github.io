Questions

Good to know before the visit.

1What should I do before a visit?

When you book, say where you've been seeing them: kitchen, bathroom, garage or yard. Clear the space under the sink and around those spots so they can be reached. Anything else for your home, we'll tell you on the phone.

2What about kids and pets during a treatment?

Ask us when you book. We'll tell you what the visit involves for your home and what to do before and after, including where kids and pets should be.

3The roaches keep coming back. What now?

Call or text and tell us where and when you see them. We look at how they're getting in and what's drawing them, treat those spots, and tell you the next step in plain words.

4How do I book a visit?

Call or text (210) 533-2284, the number painted on the truck as 533-ABUG. You can also send the form on this page, which opens your own email app, or message A-Bug on Facebook.

5What should I put in a text?

Your ZIP or part of San Antonio, what you're seeing and where, a photo if you can get one, and a good time to call you back.
