Paradise Green is a Phoenix landscaping crew working out of 3346 W Monterosa St. We are not a bid mill. We take the yards we can finish in the week we promised.

West Phoenix heat cooks cheap installs. The gravel migrates, the turf burns at the edges, the cactus leans. We plant and set for 110-degree Junes, not for a pretty Saturday photo.

If you want a tropical lawn that drinks like a swimming pool, we will tell you no. If you want the street view to look settled — decomposed granite, the right palo verde, turf where feet actually go — that is the job.
