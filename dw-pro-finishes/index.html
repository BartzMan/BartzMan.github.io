A finisher keeps a wall of sample boards, one for every look. Tap a board to read what the finish is, where it suits, and see their photo of a piece in a similar finish.

No. 01

Natural stain

Stain soaks into the wood and lets the grain show through, in a shade anywhere from honey to deep walnut. A clear topcoat then protects it.

Where it suits
  • Kitchen cabinets
  • Built-ins
  • Accent pieces
No. 02

Lacquer white

Lacquer paint dries to a smooth, hard, even surface. White is the classic kitchen look, and the same finish can be tinted to almost any colour.

Where it suits
  • Kitchen cabinets
  • Built-ins
No. 03

Faux marble

A faux finish is painted by hand to imitate another material. Faux marble builds soft veins over a pale base, so a panel or piece reads like carved stone. This photo shows their dark finish on built-ins.

Where it suits
  • Accent pieces
  • Built-ins
  • Antique pieces
No. 04

Distressed

Distressing softens a finish on purpose: glaze settles in the corners and edges wear lightly, so a piece looks like it has been loved for years.

Where it suits
  • Antique pieces
  • Accent pieces
  • Built-ins
No. 05

Gold leaf

Gold leaf is wafer-thin metal laid by hand over a prepared surface for a bright, warm gleam. It is used for trim lines, edges and carved details.

Where it suits
  • Antique pieces
  • Accent pieces

The catalogueCabinets, pieces & built-ins

Open a drawer to see the work: kitchens in one, furniture and built-ins in the other. Each card says what it is and when you need it.

Cabinet painting and refinishing

Existing cabinets are repainted or refinished, so you get a new look without replacing the boxes.

You need it when cabinets look tired but are still sound.

Custom staining

Stain brings out wood grain and sets the colour, from honey to deep walnut, under a clear protective coat.

You need it when you want wood to look like wood.

Faux and distressed finishes

Hand-painted effects that imitate stone or give a piece a gently aged look.

You need it when plain paint is too flat for the piece.

Built-ins and display cabinets

Entertainment units, shelving and display cabinets finished to match the room.

You need it when a big piece sets the tone of a room.

Antique restoration and gold leaf

Old pieces are cleaned, repaired and refinished, with gold leaf for edges and carved details.

You need it when a piece is worth saving.

Accent pieces and touch up

Dark console tables, shelves and small repairs where a finish has chipped or worn.

You need it when one spot is spoiling a good piece.
