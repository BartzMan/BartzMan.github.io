Before the climb

Asked atthe base.

Can you take down a tree close to my house?
That is the job climbing and rigging is for. Instead of dropping the whole tree, a climber works down from the top and the pieces are lowered on a line. Send a photo that shows the tree and the house together.
What about a dead tree?
Dead trees are part of tree removal, including tall and high-risk trees. A photo from the ground of the whole tree, plus whatever is under it, is a good place to start.
Do you remove storm-damaged trees?
Yes. Storm-damaged tree removal is one of the services. Tell us what came down or what is hanging, and what it is resting on.
What should I send first?
A photo of the whole tree, a photo of what is under it (a roof, fence, deck or driveway) and your town. Call or text (470) 400-2546, or use the request form.
