Four kinds of roof work, each in plain English, so you know which one you are looking at before you call.

01 Roof replacement

Taking the old roof off down to the deck and putting a new one on. It is the job you need when leaks keep coming back or the shingles are worn through.

02 Roof repair

Fixing a leak, a few lifted shingles or damaged metal edging without replacing the whole roof. It suits damage that sits in one spot.

03 Vents & flashing

The vents, pipes and metal strips that seal every opening in a roof. Worth a look when a stain shows up on a ceiling right under one.

04 Sheds & additions

Workshops, sheds and room additions have roofs too, and they can get the same shingles as the main house.
