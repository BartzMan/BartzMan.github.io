JP-100 · the services

Four chips, one painter.

Each service is a chip: the swatch on top is the work, the strip below says what it is and when you need it.

JP-101 · Mantel Grey

Interior painting

Walls and ceilings inside the house, rooms cleared and covered first. You need it when a room looks tired, a colour has to change, or before moving in or selling.

JP-102 · Sawhorse Walnut

Front door refinishing

A wood front door taken back to bare wood, stained and sealed again. You need it when the finish is peeling, faded by the sun or patchy.

JP-103 · Fresh White

Walls and trim

The details that make a room look finished: baseboards, door frames, window trim and mantels, cut in with a steady line. You need it when trim is chipped or yellowed, or the walls change colour.

JP-104 · Prairie Sky

Exterior painting

Siding, trim, eaves and doors on the outside of the house, prepped and painted for the Texas sun and storms. You need it when paint is peeling, chalky or faded.

JP-300 · before you call

Which finish for which room?

Colour gets the attention, but the sheen decides how a wall wears. Pick a room to see the finish most painters reach for, and why.

FlatMatteEggshellSatinSemi-gloss

Flat

Flat hides bumps and patch marks overhead, where nobody scrubs.

General guidance: the shinier the finish, the easier it wipes clean, and the more it shows every dent and roller mark.
