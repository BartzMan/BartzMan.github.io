Questions

Common pad problems, what they tend to mean.

My pool pump runs but won't prime. What's going on?
Usually air is getting in on the suction side: a low water level, a loose or cracked pump lid, a clogged skimmer or pump basket, or a leaking valve. Check the water level and empty both baskets first. If it still won't catch, call or text and tell us what you see through the pump lid.
Why won't my pool heater or heat pump warm the water?
Heaters need steady water flow before they switch on, so a dirty filter or a pump problem can keep them off. If the pump is running and the filter is clean, write down any error code on the display and send it with a photo of the unit's label.
My timer lost its schedule. Can it be fixed?
Arizona skips daylight saving, so the clock doesn't jump twice a year, but power outages, worn trippers and controller resets can still throw a schedule off. We can set the schedule again, replace a worn timer, or set up automation and controls.
What should I send before you come out?
A clear photo of the label on the equipment, which shows the make and model, a photo of the whole pad, and a line about what it's doing. Text them to (480) 369-3625 or email beyondbluepoolservice@gmail.com.
