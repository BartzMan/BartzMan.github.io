118°My AC is running but blowing warm air. What should I check first?

Check that the thermostat is set to cool and below room temperature, then look at the air filter, and check that the outdoor unit is running. If the filter is clean, the setting is right and the air is still warm, switch the system off and message us with what you see and hear. Running it hard while something is wrong can make the problem bigger.

112°How often should an AC be serviced in Arizona heat?

Systems here run for months at a time, so a yearly check before the long hot stretch is the usual rhythm. Rooftop units and older systems can be worth a look more often. Tell us what you have and we will suggest a schedule.

98°What is the difference between a rooftop unit and a split system?

A rooftop package unit keeps everything in one cabinet on the roof and sends cooled air down into the house. A split system has a condenser outside on the ground and an air handler indoors, joined by refrigerant lines. We work on both.
