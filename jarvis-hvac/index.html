The system · follow the air

One loop. Five stops.

Forced-air heating and cooling is one big loop. Air comes back through the return, gets filtered, heated or cooled, and goes out the supply to every room. Step through it, winter or summer.

Stop 1 of 5 · return side

Return grille

Air from the rooms comes back here, usually through one or two big grilles. A couch or a pile of boxes in front of it starves the whole system of air.

Air check · before you call

What is the air telling you?

Pick what your house is doing. Each one comes with a few things any homeowner can check first. If they don't sort it, that's the time to call.

One room is always too hot or too cold

  • Make sure that room's supply register is fully open and not under a rug or behind a couch.
  • Leave the door open a crack so air can get back to the return.
  • Still off? The duct run to that room may need a look.

Weak air from every vent

  • Pull the filter. If you can't see light through it, replace it.
  • Make sure the big return grille isn't blocked.
  • Blower running but the air still weak? Time to call.

It runs, but won't heat or cool

  • Check the thermostat mode, the setting and its batteries.
  • Check the breaker and the furnace power switch. Reset a tripped breaker once only.
  • In summer, clear leaves and grass from around the outdoor unit.

Dusty or stale air

  • Change the filter on schedule; the size is printed on its edge.
  • Vacuum the return grille and the supply registers.
  • Keep the space around the furnace clear of boxes and dust.

Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas company from outside.
