Sheet 02 · Spec sheet

Three trades inside. One straight edge.

Going by the work Marksmen Painting has posted: walls and ceilings, crown and trim, and layered faux finishes. Pick a line on the sheet to see it.

Walls, ceilings and door casings in living rooms, halls and bedrooms.

When you need itMarks, patches or a dated color, or a move in or out is coming.

  • Fill and sand
  • Cut in at ceilings and trim
  • Two finish coats

Baseboards, crown molding, casings, doors and built-in millwork in a harder, wipeable finish.

When you need itTrim is chipped, yellowed or scuffed, or the line where trim meets wall looks wavy.

  • Caulk the joints
  • Sand between coats
  • One straight line at the wall

Sponged, ragged and layered wall finishes, like the soft blue in this bathroom and the tan in the hallway.

When you need itYou want depth on a plain wall, or an older faux finish needs a refresh or a clean cover.

  • Base coat first
  • Glaze layered by hand
  • Edges kept off the trim

Sheet 03 · Exterior

Outside, the lines show from the street.

Stucco, siding, trim, garage doors and front doors on the outside of the house.

When you need itPaint fades, chalks or peels, or the trim lines have gone soft and blurry.

  • Wash and scrape
  • Caulk and prime bare spots
  • Body first, trim last
