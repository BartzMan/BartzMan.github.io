Stratum 05 · Bedding · Before anyone digs

Read the
paint.

Before a dig in Ohio, the call goes to 811 and the utilities come out to mark their lines on the lawn. Every colour means something. Here is the code you will see in the grass.

  • RedElectric power lines
  • YellowGas, oil and steam
  • OrangeCommunication cables
  • BlueDrinking water
  • GreenSewer and drain lines
  • PurpleReclaimed water
  • PinkTemporary survey marks
  • WhiteWhere the dig is planned

The colours follow the uniform colour code used for utility locates across the US.

Stratum 07 · Before you call

Good
questions.

Got a photo?Text it to(513) 904-3730
Do you dig, or only do the plumbing?

Both. Excavation and sewer services are part of the work, so the same people who find the problem can dig to the line, replace it and backfill.

My drains keep backing up. Is it roots?

It often is in older lines, but not always. The line gets cleared and the cause found first; if roots are getting in through a broken section, you will hear the options for that section.

Tank or tankless water heater?

Both are installed and repaired. Tell them how many people are in the house, where the current heater sits and whether it is gas or electric, and the choice gets a lot easier.

What should I put in a quote request?

What is happening, where in the house or yard, roughly how long it has been going on, and the best number to reach you. A photo helps: you can text one to (513) 904-3730.
