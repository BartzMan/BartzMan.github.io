TB 04 · How a job goes

Clean lines start before the paint.

A sharp line between two colors is mostly prep. Here is the usual order of a painting job, from the first text to the last coat.

  1. 1

    Call or text

    Say what needs painting. A photo of the room, wall or outside of the house helps.

  2. 2

    Talk colors and rooms

    Which rooms, which surfaces, and the colors you have in mind.

  3. 3

    Cover, patch and prep

    Floors and fixtures covered, drywall holes and cracks patched, edges masked.

  4. 4

    Paint and tidy up

    Cut in, roll out, then the coverings come off and the room is yours again.
