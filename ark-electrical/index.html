My breaker keeps tripping. What should I do?

Stop resetting it over and over. Note what was running when it tripped and whether it goes right away or after a while, then call or text ARK at (281) 507-8181. If you smell burning or see scorch marks, turn off the main breaker if it is safe to do so and call 911.

What size generator and transfer switch do I need?

It depends on what you want to run when the power is out: a few circuits or the whole house. Send a photo of your panel with the door open and a list of the circuits you can't do without, and ARK will look at it and talk it through with you.

Do you build meter banks for builders?

Meter banks and service for new multi-unit builds are part of ARK's work, like the banks of six meter sockets on unistrut in the photos on this page. Call or text with the site location and the number of units.

How do I book?

Call or text (281) 507-8181, email arkelectricalservice@gmail.com, or fill in the form on this page so it opens an email with your details. ARK will reply to set up a time.

What should I send in a text?

Your name, your part of town and what is going on. A photo helps: the panel with its door open, the breaker that trips, or the spot where you want a generator. For a new build, the site location and the number of units.
