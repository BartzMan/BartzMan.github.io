01The roof

One roof. Layer by layer.

A shingle roof is a stack. A wooden deck, a waterproof underlayment over it, then rows of shingles that overlap like fish scales so water runs off down the slope instead of in.

From a drone it reads as a pattern of lines: every line is a course. That pattern is what ISL Roofing photographs when a roof is done.
