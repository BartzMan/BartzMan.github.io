Standing seam

Raised joints connect adjoining panels along the roof slope. The folded seam lifts the joint above the panel surface.

Ridge cap

A ridge cap covers the meeting point at the top of two slopes. Its shape bridges the joint.

Valley flashing

Valley flashing sits where slopes meet and gives runoff a route down the inside angle.

Gutter

Custom trim

South Bay homeowner guide

Metal or shingles?

Five questions to bring to a roof conversation.

How steep is the roof?

Look at the slope from the ground. Ask which products suit that pitch.

What shape fits the house?

Compare the strong lines of standing seams with the layered look of shingles.

Where does rain collect?

Notice valleys and walls where slopes meet. Ask how each option handles those joints.

How will maintenance work?

Ask what to check after storms and how future repairs would be handled.

What does the roof sound like indoors?

Ask how the deck, attic and insulation affect the sound of rain.
