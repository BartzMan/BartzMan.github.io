PYJ · 02 · UNDERLAYMENT · GRADE A

Four jobs, one roof.

Each one is a different layer of the same roof. Pick the job that sounds like yours.

01

Shingle roof replacement

The full new-roof job: old shingles off, the deck looked over, new shingles on, row by row up to the ridge.

02

Metal roofing

Long metal panels fastened over underlayment. A clean-lined roof, and a good fit for porches and low pitches.

03

Roof decking & underlayment

The layers you never see from the street: the plywood deck and the water-resistant sheet that goes under the shingles or metal.

04

Roof repair

A missing shingle, a soft spot, a stain on the ceiling: a repair, when the whole roof doesn’t need to come off.

PYJ · 03 · DRIP EDGE · TOP LAYER

Pick your top layer.

Shingle

  • Flat tabs that overlap, laid from the bottom edge up.
  • Nailed through the upper part so the next row hides the nails.
  • The most familiar look on a San Antonio street.

Metal

  • Long panels with raised ribs that run from the peak to the eave.
  • Fastened down through the panel or with hidden clips.
  • Finishes come in many colours, from white to deep red.
