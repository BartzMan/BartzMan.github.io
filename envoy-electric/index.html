Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Anything not here: call or text (832) 503-0182.

Can you add recessed lights to a room that has none?
Recessed lights are part of the lighting work Envoy Electric does. Every ceiling is different, so text a photo of the room and roughly how many lights you have in mind to (832) 503-0182 and you will get an answer about your room, not a general one.
Can you hang a heavy chandelier on a very high ceiling?
Chandeliers are part of the lighting work. The two-storey foyer at the top of this page went up from scaffolding. Send a photo of the ceiling and the fixture you picked, with its size and weight if you have them.
Some of my lights flicker. What should I do?
Flicker can come from a loose connection, a bulb that does not suit its dimmer, or something further back in the wiring. Note which lights flicker and when, then call or text (832) 503-0182 and describe it.
What should I send with my message?
Photos of the room and the ceiling, the fixture you picked if you have one, and your part of Houston or ZIP code. Text them to (832) 503-0182 or email dtorrelectric@gmail.com.
Where do you work?
Houston, TX and nearby. Say where the job is when you call or text.
