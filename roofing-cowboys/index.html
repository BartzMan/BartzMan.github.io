Entry 003 · Questions

Entry 003 Before you sign

New build or reroof in DFW: 5 things to ask your roofer

0 of 5 asked

  1. Underlayment is the second line of defence behind the shingles. Ask for the type by name, not just “felt” or “synthetic”.

  2. Ask for the ventilation plan: where fresh air comes in and where hot air leaves.

  3. Most leaks start where a roof meets something, not in the middle of a slope.

  4. The metal lip along the eaves sends water into the gutter instead of behind it.

  5. Ask about a magnet sweep of the yard and driveway, and where the debris goes.
