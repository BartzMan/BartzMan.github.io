Issue 02

The missions

Residential pest control

Pest control for the house you live in: kitchens, garages, yards and the corners you forget about. Call when you spot a pest more than once.

Commercial pest control

For restaurants, shops and offices, where pests hurt more than comfort. Worth a call at the first sighting.

Termite control

Termites work quietly inside wood. Think of it if you find mud tubes, swarms of winged insects or wood that sounds hollow.

Rodent control

Rats and mice settle in attics and walls. Scratching at night or gnawed packaging means it's time to call.

Wildlife trapping

Animals that move into attics, under decks or around the yard. Trapping is for when an animal is in a place it shouldn't be.
