Flat to semi-gloss: where each sheen goes.

The shinier the paint, the tougher and easier to wipe, and the more it shows every bump. Pick a sheen to see where it usually belongs in a house.

Sheen

Flat

Where: Ceilings, and walls that don’t get touched much.

Why: No shine means light skims across without showing bumps and patches. It’s the hardest to scrub.
