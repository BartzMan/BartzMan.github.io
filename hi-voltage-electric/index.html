Questions

Questions, switched on.

Can you add recessed lights to a ceiling that's already finished?

Usually, yes. Recessed lights made for existing ceilings go in through the hole cut for the light itself, and the wiring is fished between them above the ceiling, so it's rarely a big tear-out. Send a photo of the room and say roughly how many lights you want.

Do you do outdoor and landscape lighting?

Yes. Step lights, path and garden lights, and under-counter lighting for outdoor kitchens are all part of the work. A photo of the yard or steps in daylight helps plan where the lights go.

How do I know if my panel needs work?

Breakers that trip often, a panel that feels warm, or a renovation that adds a lot of new circuits are all reasons to have it looked at. A photo of the open panel door and its label is a good start.

What should I send with my message?

Your town, a short description of the job, and a few photos: the room, the fixture, the panel or the yard. Email works best for photos: hivoltage65@gmail.com.
