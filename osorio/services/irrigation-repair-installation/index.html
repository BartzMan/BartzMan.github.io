A broken line or a stuck valve wastes water, drowns one part of the yard and starves another. In a Mesa summer, a dry spot can turn a healthy plant or tree into a problem fast.

Osorio Tree and Land Service repairs and renovates irrigation systems, working with polypipe and PVC pipe, and installs valves, vacuum valves and timer (clock) systems.

What's included

  • RepairsBroken or leaking polypipe and PVC lines fixed.
  • Valve installationIrrigation valves installed and replaced.
  • Vacuum valvesVacuum valves installed as part of the system.
  • Timer (clock) systemsTimers installed and set up to suit your yard.
  • Renovating older systemsTired or patched-up systems brought back into working order.

Check first

Call 811 before digging

Before any digging, including irrigation trenching, call Arizona 811 (dial 811 or go to arizona811.com) so buried utilities are marked. It's free and the law requires it.

Common questions

What kinds of irrigation work do you do?
Repairs and renovation of polypipe and PVC irrigation, installing valves and vacuum valves, and installing timer (clock) systems.
How do I know my irrigation has a leak?
Common signs include soggy or sunken patches, spots that stay wet, dry spots elsewhere, a sudden jump in your water bill, or a valve that runs or won't turn off.
Can you update an old system?
Yes. Renovating older systems is part of what we do. Send us a photo of the valve box and the controller, if you have one.
Do you work on trees and yards too?
Yes. Irrigation sits alongside tree and palm trimming and yard maintenance, so one call covers the yard.
