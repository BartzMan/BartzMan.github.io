Before you call.

Four things Katy homeowners ask. Anything else: call or text (832) 902-5492.

Photo tip

A clear photo of the model plate on the outdoor unit tells us the make, size and age of the system.

My AC is running but the house isn’t cooling. What now?

Check that the thermostat is set to cool and the filter isn’t clogged. If the outdoor unit is silent, the lines are icing, or air from the vents is warm, switch the system off and call or text us: running it like that can make things worse.

How do I book a visit?

Call or text (832) 902-5492, or use the form below to send an email. Tell us what the system is doing and where you are in Katy or the Houston area.

What should I send in a message?

A short description of the problem, plus photos of the outdoor unit, the indoor unit and the model plate (the sticker with the model and serial number). They help us understand the system before we arrive.

I smell gas. Should I call you?

Not first. Leave the house, then call 911 or your gas company from outside. Once it’s safe, we can look at the heating system.
