Our services

Five things the ROYS crew does for homes in Phoenix and the West Valley.

  • AC repair

    Finding out why an AC stopped cooling, runs warm or won’t start, and fixing it.

  • AC unit replacement

    Taking out an old or worn-out AC unit and putting in new equipment.

  • Package units

    Rooftop and ground package units, the all-in-one systems on many Phoenix-area homes.

  • Heating

    Heating checks and repairs for the cooler desert months.

  • Maintenance

    Routine checks and tune-ups so your system is looked at before the heat sets in.
