Section 02 · before you call

Breaker tripped? Four steps.

Safe, plain checks for any home. Step through them; the panel drawing follows along.

Switch things off

Turn off the lights and unplug the heaters, hair dryers or appliances that were running on that circuit when the power went out.

Find the tripped breaker

Open the panel door. A tripped breaker sits in the middle position, or shows a small red or orange flag. The label card tells you which room it feeds.

Reset it once

Push the handle firmly all the way to OFF, then back to ON. Kitchen and bathroom outlets may also have a GFCI Reset button: press it.

Trips again? Leave it off

A breaker that trips again right away is protecting the wiring. Leave it off and call an electrician. A hot panel or a burning smell: call straight away.
