Piece 04 The label wall

Choosing bulb colour, room by room.

Four things to check before you buy bulbs. Step through them.

2700K warm3000K soft3500K neutral4000K cool
  1. 1

    Read the box.

    Every bulb lists a number followed by K. Lower numbers are warmer and more amber; higher numbers are whiter and bluer.

  2. 2

    One number per room.

    Warm, 2700K to 3000K, suits living rooms, bedrooms and dining rooms. Cleaner, 3500K to 4000K, suits kitchens, garages and workspaces.

  3. 3

    Check it dims.

    On a dimmer, buy bulbs marked dimmable and ask an electrician whether your dimmer suits them. Buzzing or flicker usually means a mismatch.

  4. 4

    Change them together.

    Bulbs of the same type and number match best. Replace every bulb in a fixture or room at once so the colour stays even.
